# [30th December 2013] Supplement to Official Gazette 1323

## S.I. 82 of 2013

# NATIONAL AIDS COUNCIL OF SEYCHELLES ACT, 2013
**(Act 13 of 2013)**

### National AIDS Council of Seychelles Act (Commencement) Notice, 2013

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 1 of the National AIDS Council of Seychelles Act, 2013 the Minister of Health hereby makes the following Notice-

1. **Citation:** This Notice may be cited as the National AIDS Council of Seychelles Act (Commencement) Notice, 2013.

2. **Commencement of Act 10 of 2013:** The National AIDS Council of Seychelles Act, 2013 shall come into operation on 1st January, 2014.

***

MADE this 26th day of December, 2013.

**MITCYLARUE**
MINISTER OF HEALTH
