# Job Aid for HCWs: Procedures for Notification of Death (Ministry of Health, 2023)

**ANY PERSON DIES**

| For ALL deaths | If no autopsy indicated | If medical autopsy required | If police case |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| - Medical practitioner (public or private) to confirm death clinically. <br> - Issue death notification for ALL deaths. <br> - If police case or requires autopsy, state on notification. <br> - Scan a copy of death notification (legible). <br> - Give original copy of death notification to Next-of-Kin. <br> - Medical practitioner is responsible to e-mail scanned copy of death notification within 24h to Civil Status Office at `infodeath@cs.gov.sc` and Ministry of Health at `vitalstats@health.gov.sc`. | - Treating medical practitioner issues Medical Certificate of Cause of Death (MCCOD) together with death notification. <br> - Explain cause of death to next-of-kin. Body can be released for funeral procedures. <br> - Original MCCOD in patient notes. <br> - MCCOD is not issued to next-of-kin, but they can request medical report through standard procedures for details of death. <br> - For deaths occurring at home, refer case to the treating medical practitioner to issue MCCOD (can be public or private clinic). <br> - Medical practitioner issuing MCCOD is responsible to submit copy of MCCOD within 24h to `infodeath@cs.gov.sc` and `vitalstats@health.gov.sc`. | - Provide post-mortem information sheet and explain need & procedure. <br> - Obtain consent for autopsy and fill relevant forms. <br> - Submit patient notes with above to pathology services. <br> - Transfer body to hospital mortuary. <br> - Pathology services perform autopsy per procedures; body release based on pathologist assessment. <br> - Pathologist issues MCCOD; original copy in patient notes; not issued to next-of-kin. <br> - Explain MCCOD to next of kin (ideally with last treating clinician). <br> - Next-of-Kin can request medical report through standard procedures for written documentation of cause of death. <br> - Pathologist is responsible to submit copy of MCCOD to `infodeath@cs.gov.sc` and `vitalstats@health.gov.sc`. | - Notify relevant officials at Seychelles Police through provided forms, and follow instructions per police. <br> - Forensic pathologist guides procedures, including autopsy. <br> - MCCOD issuance by forensic pathologist. <br> - Original copy to patient notes. <br> - Release of body per police instructions. <br> - Next-of-Kin can request any reports to relevant department of the Seychelles Police. <br> - Forensic pathologist is responsible to submit copy of MCCOD to `infodeath@cs.gov.sc` and `vitalstats@health.gov.sc`. |

***

*Ministry of Health, 26 June 2023; Version 1.0; Note: To report any issues with use, please go through Principal Medical Officers.*
