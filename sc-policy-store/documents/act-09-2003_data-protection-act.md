# PART I - PRELIMINARY

1. This Act may be cited as the **Data Protection Act, 2002** and shall come into operation on such date as the Minister may by notice in the Gazette appoint.

2. (1) The following provisions shall, unless the context otherwise requires, have effect for the interpretation of this Act.

(2) "Commissioner" includes a trade or profession.

(3) "Commissioner" means the Data Protection Commissioner appointed under section 4.

(4) "Court" means the Supreme Court.

(5) "Data" means information recorded in a form in which it can be processed by equipment operating automatically in instructions given for that purpose.

(6) "Data equipment" means equipment for the automatic processing of data, such as for recording information so that it can be automatically retrieved.

(7) "Personal data", means data concerning information, which relates to an individual who can be identified from the information (or informations and other information in the possession of the data user), including any expression of opinion about the individual,’s character or indications of the intentions of the data user in respect of that individual.

(8) "Data material" means any other document or material used in connection with such data equipment.

(9) "Data subject" means an individual who is the subject of personal data.

(10) "Data user" means a person who holds data, and a person who "holds" data means—

(a) the data form part of a collection of data processed or intended to be processed by or on behalf of that person or as specified in section 15;

(b) that person (either alone or jointly or in common with other persons) controls the contents and use of the data comprised in the collection, and
(c) the data are in the form in which they have been born or are intended to be processed as mentioned in paragraph (a) or (b) (though not for the time being in that form or in a form which is a translation of it converted after being so processed and on which a view to being further so processed as mentioned in a subsequent occasion.

(d) A person carries on a business on a **"computer bureau"** if he provides other persons with services in respect of data, and a person provides such services for a fee.

(e) as agent for other persons he causes data held by them to be processed as mentioned in subsection (5) or

(f) he allows other persons the use of the equipment in his possession for the processing as mentioned in that subsection of data held by them.

(12) **"De-registration notice"** means a notice under section 15.

(13) **"Prescribed"** means prescribed by regulations under section 46.

(14) **"Processing"**, in relation to data, means amending, supplementing, deleting or re-arranging the data or extracting the information constituting the data and using it, and includes all persons performing any one of those operations by reference to the data subject.

*Provided that this subsection shall not apply to any operation solely performed for the purpose of preparing the text of documents.*

(15) **"Disclosing"**, in relation to data, includes disclosing information extracted from the data and where the identification of the individual who is the subject of the data is not necessary, the information constituting the data and partly or other information in its possession as the processing of the data and shall not be regarded as disclosed or transferred unless the other information is also disclosed or transferred.

(16) **"Enactment"** includes an enactment subsequent to this Act.
(17) **"Enforcement notice"** means a notice under section 14.

(18) **"Register"** means the register maintained under section 8.

(19) **"Transfer prohibition notice"** means a notice under section 28.

### PART II. REGULATION OF DATA USERS AND COMPUTER BUREAUX

**8.** The Commissioner shall maintain a register of data users
who shall, and of persons carrying on computer bureaux, who
provide services in respect of personal data and shall make an
entry in the register in pursuance of each application for registration accepted by the Commissioner under this Part.

(2) Each entry shall state whether it is in respect of—
(a) a data user,
(b) a person carrying on a computer bureau,
or
(c) a data user who also carries on such a bureau.

(3) Subject to this section, an entry in respect of a data user shall contain the following particulars—
(a) the name and address of the data user;
(b) a description of the personal data to be held by him and of the purposes for which the data are (or have been) collected;
(c) a description of every source from which he intends or may wish to obtain the data or the information to be contained in the data;
(d) a description of every person to whom he intends to or may wish to disclose the data (otherwise than in a case contemplated by section 11 of this Act);
(e) the name of every country outside Seychelles to which he intends or may wish directly or indirectly to transfer the data; and
(f) one or more addresses for the receipts of requests from data subjects for access to the data.

(4) Subject to this section, an entry in respect of a person carrying on a computer bureau shall consist of his name and address.

(5) Subject to this section, an entry in respect of a data user who carries on a computer bureau shall consist of his name and address and, as respects the personal data to be held by him, the particulars specified in subsection (3) to this section.

(6) In the case of a registered company, the address referred to in subsections (3)(a), (4), and (5) is that of its registered office, and the particulars to be included in the entry shall include the company's number in the register of companies.
(7) In the case of a person (other than a registered company) carrying on a business the address referred to in subsections (3)(a), (3)(b), (4) and (5) is that of his principal place of business.

(8) The Minister may by Order published in the Gazette give variety of directions as indicated in section 40 of this Act.

### 9(1)

A person shall not hold personal data unless an entry in respect of that person as a data user, or as a data user who also carries on a business, is for the time being contained in the register.

(2) A person is in respect of whom such an entry is contained in the register shall have

(a) hold personal data of any description other than that specified in the entry;

(b) hold any such data, or use any such data held by him, for any purpose other than the purpose or purposes described in the entry;

(c) obtain such data, or information to be contained in such data, to be held by him from any source which is not described in the entry;

(d) disclose such data held by him to any person who is not described in the entry; or

(e) indirectly or directly transfer such data held by him to any country outside Seychelles other than a country specified in the entry.

(3) A servant or agent of a person to whom section (2) applies, subject as respects personal data held by that person, be subject to the same restrictions on the use and disclosure of the data to those subjects to which that person is subject under subsection (2).

(4) A person shall not, in carrying on a computer bureau, provide services in respect of personal data unless an entry in respect of that person as a person carrying on such a bureau is for the time being contained in the register.

(5) Any person who contravenes subsection (1) or
knowingly or recklessly contravenes any other the provisions of this section shall be guilty of an offence.

**10.(1)** A person applying for registration shall state whether he wishes to be registered as a data user, as a person carrying on a business with respect to data users, or as a person carrying on a business, and shall furnish the Commissioner, in such form as the Commissioner may require, with the particular information as shall be included in the entry to be made in pursuance of the application.

**(2)** Where a person intends to hold personal data for two or more purposes he may make separate applications for registration in respect of any of those purposes.

**(3)** A registered person may at any time apply to the Commissioner for the alteration of any particulars included in the entry relating to him in the register.

**(4)** Where the alteration would consist of the addition of a purpose for which personal data are to be held, the person may, notwithstanding the provisions of subsection (2), make a single fresh application for registration in respect of the additional purpose.

**(5)** A registered person shall make an application under subsection (3) whenever necessary for ensuring that the entry or entries relating to that person contain the up-to-date information, and any person who fails to comply with this subsection shall be guilty of an offence.

**(6)** Any person, who in connection with an application for registration or the alteration of registered particulars, knowingly or recklessly furnishes the Commissioner with information which is false or misleading shall be guilty of a relevant offence.

**(7)** Every application for registration shall be accompanied by the prescribed fee, and every application for the alteration of registered particulars shall be accompanied by such fee, if any, as may be prescribed.

**(8)** Any application for registration or the alteration of registered particulars may be withdrawn by notice in writing to the Commissioner at any time before the notice becomes a relevant notification in respect of the application under section 11(1).

# SCHEDULE

### Section 3

## DATA PROTECTION PRINCIPLES

#### PART I
**THE PRINCIPLES**

**Personal data held by data users**

1. The information to be contained in personal data shall be obtained, and personal data shall be processed, fairly and lawfully.

2. Personal data shall be held only for one or more specified and lawful purposes.

3. Personal data held for any purpose or purposes shall not be used or disclosed in any manner incompatible with those purpose or those purposes.

4. Personal data held for any purpose or purposes shall be adequate, relevant and not excessive in relation to that purpose or those purposes.

5. Personal data shall be accurate and, where necessary, kept up to date.

6. Personal data held for any purpose or purposes shall not be kept for longer than is necessary for that purpose or those purposes.

7. An individual shall be entitled —

    (a) to reasonable information without undue delay or expense

        (i) to be informed by any data user whether he holds personal data of which that individual is the subject and

        (ii) to access to any such data held by a data user, and

    (b) where appropriate, to have such data corrected or erased.

**Personal data held by data users or in respect of which services are provided by persons carrying on computer bureau services**

8. Appropriate security measures shall be taken against unauthorised access to or alteration of personal data and against accidental loss of or destruction of personal data.

## INTERPRETATION

### The first principle
1. (1) Subject to subparagraph (2), (2) in determining whether information was lawfully obtained, regard shall be had to the method by which it was obtained, including in particular whether any person from whom the information was obtained was deceived or induced to do so for purposes or purposes for which it is to be held, used or disclosed, unless

(2) Information shall only be an extent as treated as obtained unfairly if it is obtained

(a) is authorised by or under any enactment to supply it or be
(b) is required to supply it by or under any enactment or by any international obligation on the Republic of Seychelles;

and in determining whether information was obtained unfairly there shall be regard to any other circumstance of the case, including whether the information was obtained by or under any enactment or required by any such convention or other instrument as aforesaid.

### The second principle
Personal data shall not be used as held for a specified purpose unless that purpose is distinct or described in particulars registered under this Act unless the data subject consents.

### The third principle
Personal data shall not be treated as used or disclosed in contravention of this principle unless
(a) used otherwise than for a purpose of the description registered under this Act in relation to the data, or
(d) disclosed otherwise than to a person of a description so registered.

### The fifth principle
4. Any question whether or not personal data are accurate shall be determined as far as possible by reference to paragraph 29 but, in the case of such data as are mentioned in subsection (2) of that section, this principle shall not extend to requiring that any step be taken for the purpose of any inaccuracy in the information there mentioned if the requirement would not be feasible to be met where it been complied with.
## The seventh principle

5.1 (1) Paragraph (a) of this principle shall not be construed as conferring any rights inconsistent with section 28.

(2) In determining whether access to personal data is lawful or necessary, regard shall be had to the purpose for which the data, the purpose for which the data are held and the frequency with which the data are stored.

(3) Any restriction or erasure of personal data is appropriate only where necessary for ensuring compliance with the other data protection principles.

## The eighth principle

6. Regard shall be had—

(a) to the nature of the personal data and the harm that would result from such access, without discretion, closure, loss or destruction as mentioned in this principle; and

(b) to the place where the personal data are stored, to security measures protecting the personal data, to the equipment and to measures taken for ensuring the reliability of staffing services accessing the data.

### Use for historical, statistical or research purposes

7. Where personal data are held for historical, statistical or research purposes and not used in such a way that damage is, or likely to be, caused to any data subject—

(a) the information contained in the data shall not be regarded for the purposes of the first principle as obtained unlawfully by the possessor of the data, provided such purpose was not disclosed when the data were obtained; and

(b) the data may, notwithstanding the sixth principle, be kept indefinitely.
