## PART I — PRELIMINARY

1. This Act may be cited as the Data Protection Act, 2002 and shall come into operation on such date as the Minister may by notice in the Gazette appoint. Short title

2. (1) The following provisions shall, unless the context otherwise requires, have effect for the interpretation of this Act. Interpretation

(2) "controller" includes a trade or profession;

(3) "Commissioner" means the Data Protection Commissioner appointed under section 4;

(4) "Court" means the Supreme Court;

(5) "data" means information recorded in a form in which it can be accessed by equipment operating automatically in response to instructions given for that purpose;

(6) "data equipment" means equipment for the automatic processing of recorded information so that it can be automatically accessed;

(7) "personal data" means data consisting of information which identifies an individual who has been identified or is identifiable from the information (or from other information in the possession of the data user), including any expression of opinion about the individual, and any indication of the intentions of the data user in respect of that individual;

(8) "data material" means any other document or material used in connection with data equipment;

(9) "data subject" means an individual who is the subject of personal data;

(10) "data user" means a person who holds data, and a person who "holds" data means—

(a) the data form part of a collection of data processed or intended to be processed by or on behalf of that person or as specified in section 14;

(b) that person (either alone or jointly or in common with other persons) controls the contents and use of the data comprised in the collection, and

**THE PRINCIPLES**

**Personal data held by data users**

1. The information to be contained in personal data shall be
obtained, and personal data shall be processed, fairly and

lawful purposes.

used or disclosed in any manner incompatible with those pur-

4. Personal data held for any purpose or purposes shall be
adequate, relevant and not excessive in relation to that purpose
or those purposes.

date.

6. Personal data held for any purpose or purposes shall not be kept
for longer than is necessary for that purpose or those purposes.

## The seventh principle

5.1 (1) Paragraph (a) of this principle shall not be construed as
conferring any rights inconsistent with section 28.

(2) In determining whether access to personal data is requisite or
necessary, regard shall be had to the nature of the data, the
purpose for which the data are held and the frequency with which the
data are stored.

(3) The destruction or erasure of personal data is appropriate only
where necessary for ensuring compliance with the other data
protection principles.

## The eighth principle

6. Regard shall be had:

(a) to the nature of the personal data and the harm that
would result from such access, without discretion,
loss or destruction as mentioned in this principle; and

(b) to the place where the personal data are stored, to
security measures protecting the personal data, to
equipment and to measures taken for ensuring the
reliability of staffing access to the data.

### Use for historical, statistical or research purposes

7. Where personal data are held for historical, statistical or
research purposes and not used in such a way that damages it, or is
likely to be damaged, to correct any data imbalance:

(a) the information contained in the data shall not be
regarded for the purposes of the first principle as
obtained unlawfully by the personal data controller, provided
such purpose was not disclosed when it was obtained; and

(b) the data may, notwithstanding the sixth principle, be
kept indefinitely.
