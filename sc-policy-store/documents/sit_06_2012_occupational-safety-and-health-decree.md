# S.L. 6 of 2012

## OCCUPATIONAL-SAFETY AND HEALTH DECREE

**(Cap 151)**

### Occupational Safety and Health (Safety Officers) Regulations, 2012

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 26 of the Occupational Safety and Health Decree, the Minister of Education, Employment and Human Resource Development hereby makes the following Regulations—

1. These Regulations may be cited as the Occupational Safety and Health (Safety Officers) Regulations, 2012.

2. In these Regulations—

* **"Director"** means the Chief Executive of the Ministry or Department responsible for the administration of the Occupational Safety and Health Decree;
* **"safety officer"** means a person employed by an enterprise with more than 50 workers to deal with safety and health matters;
* **"safety representative"** means an employee elected or designated for the purpose of these Regulations.

3. (1) Every employer to whom these Regulations apply shall inform the Director in writing the name of each Safety Officer, Safety Representative or both, elected or designated in respect of the employer's premises.

## 8. A safety officer shall:

(a) advise the occupier, manager or other person in the charge of the workplace on any matter which may be taken in the interests of the safety and health of these persons;

(b) with the approval of the occupier, manager or other person implement such measures as necessarily may be required;

(c) inspect the workplace personally or direct any other person to do so, and report to the manager whether there is any machinery, plant, equipment or substance which may cause a danger, and give a description of maximal labour used in the enterprise, and in particular’s the measures taken to prevent injury to any person working or employed therein;

(d) discuss the findings of his inspection with the person who is in the charge of the machinery, plant, equipment or substance, and the worker involved, and report the findings to the employer;

(e) assist the person investigating any accident or dangerous occurrence in the workplace, and from the use of machinery, plant, equipment, substances or any other factor, and the officer shall check the investigator's report and the action taken by the employer to prevent recurrence;

(f) investigate every first aid case and dangerous occurrence in the workplace, and report to the enterprise and any industrial/ disease related issues to the employer;

(g) organise campaigns, training persons, competitions, contests and other activities encouraging the employees to take an interest in safety and health, and provide information on procedures to ensure safety and health in the workplace;

(h) advise the employer on any measure or alteration to be made to the worker or any extension thereon concerning the safety and health of the equipment or appliance to be installed or used in the workplace;

(i) co-operate with any health personnel engaged to look after the health of the persons employed in the enterprise, and suggest measures affecting the safety and health of these persons.
