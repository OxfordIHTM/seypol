# S.I. 55 of 2022
# CIVIL STATUS ACT
**(Cap 34)**

## Civil Status (Amendment) Act, 2021 (Commencement) Notice, 2022

IN EXERCISE OF THE POWERS CONFERRED BY SECTION 1 OF THE CIVIL STATUS (AMENDMENT) ACT, 2021, THE MINISTER RESPONSIBLE FOR CIVIL STATUS MAKES THE FOLLOWING NOTICE —

### Citation
1. This notice may be cited as the **Civil Status (Amendment) Act, 2021 (Commencement) Notice, 2022**.

### Commencement of Act 45 of 2021
2. Subject to regulation 3, the Civil Status (Amendment) Act, 2021, shall come into operation on 1st June, 2022.

### Commencement of specified provisions
3. Section 39A and 85 of the Civil Status (Amendment) Act, 2021, shall come into operation on 1st November, 2022.

MADE this 20th day of April, 2022.

**ERROL FONSEKA**
MINISTER OF INTERNAL AFFAIRS
