# MEDICAL PRODUCTS AND PHARMACY OPERATIONS
## BILL, 2022
*(Bill No. of 2022)*

**A BILL**

**FOR**

**AN ACT to provide for the regulation of quality, safety and efficacy of medical products and related substances; the operations of pharmacies, the repeal of the Pharmacy Act (Cap 169) and for other connected or incidental matters.**

**ENACTED** by the President and the National Assembly.

## PART I – PRELIMinary

### Short title and commencement
**1.** This Act may be cited as the **Medical Products and Pharmacy Operations Act, 2022** and shall come into operation on such date as the Minister may, by notice in the *Gazette*, appoint.

### Application
**2.**
(1) This Act applies to all medical products and operations of pharmacies.
(2) Where there is any inconsistency between any provision of this Act and provision of any other law relating to medical products or pharmacy operations, the provisions of this Act shall prevail.

### Interpretation
**3.** In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

*   **“administration”** means giving or applying to a human being or animal... (and other definitions follow)
*   **“advertisement”** in relation to a medical product, means a pictorial, visual or other descriptive matter... 
*   **“analyst”** means an analyst appointed under section 19;
*   **“authorised officer”** means an officer or employee of the Authority or any other person appointed as an authorized officer under section 17(1);
*   **“Authority”** means the Public Health Authority established under section 3 of the Public Health Authority Act, 2013;
*   **“authorised person”** means a person who is either, a medical practitioner, dentist, nurse, veterinarian, or pharmacist authorised to prescribe medicines;
*   **“batch”** means a defined quantity of medical products assembled in a single process... 
*   **“batch number”** means a distinctive combination of numbers or letters which uniquely identifies a batch;
*   **“Board”** means the Public Health Authority Board established under section 6 of the Public Health Authority Act, 2013;
*   **“certificate of marketing authorisation”** means a certificate providing authorisation to market a medical product and related substances in the Seychelles;
*   **“Chairperson”** means the Chairperson of the Committee appointed under section 8;
*   **“clinical trial"** means an investigation in respect of a medical product for use in humans or animals... 
*   **“Commissioner”** means the Public Health Commissioner appointed under section 7(1) of the Public Health Authority Act, 2013;
*   **“Committee”** means the Medical Products and Pharmacy Regulatory Committee established under section 7;
*   **“compassionate use”** means access to unregistered medical products in special or emergency situations... 
*   **“container”** means a primary, secondary or transportation container... 
*   **“dentist”** means a person who is qualified to practice as a dentist and registered as a dentist under the Medical Practitioners and Dentists Act, Cap 126;
*   **“Director”** means the Director of the Service as appointed under section 5 of the Act;
*   **“dispense”** means to prepare and supply to a patient a course of treatment on the basis of a prescription;
*   **“dispenser”** means a person other than a pharmacist who is permitted to dispense medical products... 
*   **“distribution”** means division and movement of pharmaceutical products... 
*   **“donation”** means the gift of a medical product to government, a non-governmental organisation or private entity... 
*   **“Ethics Committee”** means the Health, Research and Ethics Committee... 
*   **“expiry date”** means the date provided on an individual container... 
*   **“export”** includes to deliver or supply within the country for dispatch to a destination outside the country;
*   **“health professional”** means any one of the categories of health professionals listed in Schedule I of the Health Professionals Act, 2006;
*   **“import”** means the act of bringing into the country;
*   **“information management system”** means a database and transaction management system... 
*   **“insert”** means a document included in the package of a medical product... 
*   **“interchangeable pharmaceutical product”** means a pharmaceutical product which is therapeutically equivalent to a reference product;
*   **“geodemographic criteria”** means the taking into account of the analysis of the population... 
*   **“label”** means the process of identifying a medical product by means of label... including:
    *   (a) name of the product;
    *   (b) active ingredient;
    *   (c) dosage form;
    *   (d) batch number;
    *   (e) expiry date;
    *   (f) special storage conditions or handling precautions;
    *   (g) directions for use, warnings and precautions;
    *   (h) names and addresses of the manufacturer... 
*   **“magistral formula”** means any medicinal product prepared in a pharmacy in accordance with a prescription for an individual patient;
*   **“managing pharmacist”** means a pharmacist, with a minimum of five years of professional experience, appointed as the managing pharmacist under section 39;
*   **“manufacture”** means all operations of purchase of materials and starting materials... 
*   **“market”** includes a variety of systems, institutions, procedures... 
*   **“mark-up”** means the amount or value added to the cost price of goods to cover expenses and profit;
*   **“medicine”** means any substance or mixture of substances used or purporting to be suitable for use in—
    *   (a) the diagnosis, treatment, mitigation, modification or prevention of disease... 
    *   (b) restoring, correcting or modifying any somatic or psychic or organic function in humans...
*   **“medical device”** means any instrument, apparatus, implement... 
*   **“medical practitioner”** means a doctor who is registered to practice under the Medical Practitioner and Dentists Act (Cap 126);
*   **“medical product”** includes medicines, vaccines, vitamins, complementary medicine products, cosmetics, food supplements, diagnostics and medical devices;
*   **“Minister”** means the Minister responsible for Health;
*   **“mutual recognition”** means the acceptance of one national medical products regulatory agency’s certification... 
*   **“narcotics”** or **“narcotic substances”** means drugs listed in the 1963 Single Convention on Narcotic Drugs... 
*   **“nurse”** means a person registered under the Nurses and Midwives Act (Cap 150);
*   **“pharmacy”** means the science and technique of producing and dispensing medical products... 
*   **“pharmacy technician”** means a person registered as pharmacy technicians by the Council under the Health Professionals Act, 2006;
*   **“pharmacist”** means a person registered as pharmacist under the Health Professionals Act, 2006;
*   **“pharmacovigilance”** means activities relating to the detection, assessment... 
*   **“premises”** includes building or other permanent or temporary structure... 
*   **“prescribe”** means to issue an instruction in writing or electronically... 
*   **“prescription”** means an instruction in writing or in electronic form for a medicine... 
*   **“professional experience”** means the experience gained through full-time employment... 
*   **“promotion”** means all information and persuasive activities by manufacturers... 
*   **“psychotropic substance”** means a substance listed in the 1971 Convention on Psychotropic Substances... 
*   **“qualified technical person”** means a person responsible for the release of batches of finished products for sale;
*   **“Quality Management System”** means a set of policies, processes and procedures... 
*   **“quality control”** means the process covering all measures taken... 
*   **“recall”** means a process for withdrawing or removing a pharmaceutical product... 
*   **“reference product”** means a medical product which has been granted a marketing authorisation... 
*   **“referencing”** means taking into account the marketing authorisation decisions of a country... 
*   **“related substance”** means any natural or artificial material... 
*   **“reliance”** means relying on the assessment and inspection reports... 
*   **“re-scheduling”** means a review and update of the national classification system... 
*   **“sampling”** means the process of obtaining a representative portion... 
*   **“scheduled substance”** means any medicine or other substance described under section 23;
*   **“scheduling”** means the national classification system that controls how medicines are made available to the public;
*   **“sell”** means to sell by wholesale or retail... 
*   **“Service”** means the Medical Products and Pharmacy Regulatory Service constituted under this Act;
*   **“shelf-life”** means the period during which a pharmaceutical product... 
*   **“storage”** means storing of medical products up to their point of use;
*   **“substance”** means a natural or artificial substance... 
*   **“substandard or falsified medical product”** means the like-named product as defined by the World Health Organisation... 
*   **“supply”** includes having in possession for the purpose of supply;
*   **“technical working teams”** means the teams established in terms of section 9(d);
*   **“treatment”** in relation to disease, includes anything done or provided for alleviating the effects of the diseases... 
*   **“wholesale”** means sale of goods in large quantities, such as for resale by a retailer.

## PART II – ADMINISTRATION AND GOVERNANCE

### Establishment of the Service
**4.**(1) There is established for the purposes of this Act, a Medical Products and Pharmacy Regulatory Service.
1. The Service shall be functionally independent and accountable to the Authority.
2. The Service shall consist of—
    *   the Medical Products and Pharmacy Regulatory Service;
    *   the Medical Products and Pharmacy Regulatory Committee; and
    *   units or departments;

### Director of the Service
**5.**(1) There shall be a Director of the Service who shall be appointed by the Minister on the recommendation of the Committee...
(2) The Director shall be accountable to the Committee for the management of the affairs of the Service.
(3) The heads of units shall be appointed by the Committee on the recommendation of the Director.
(4) The Director shall be responsible for—
    *   management of the affairs of the Service;
    *   implementation of this Act regarding activities of the Service and reporting to the Authority through the Committee;
    *   execution of the decisions and directives of the Committee and making periodic reports to the Committee;
    *   any other responsibilities assigned to him or her by the Committee;
(5) The Director may delegate some of his or her responsibilities... 

### Functions of the Service
**6.** The function of the Service shall be to —
1. regulate the manufacture, import and export, distribution, sale and use of medical products;
2. recommend for the approval or withdrawal of a certificate of registration... 
3. regulate, monitor and inspect personnel and premises... 
4. maintain a register of medical products... 
5. regulate and ensure the oversight of clinical trials... 
6. test medical products regulated under this Act;
7. conduct post-marketing surveillance... 
8. regulate the promotion, advertising and marketing of medical products;
9. regulate the use of unregistered medical products for trial purposes or for compassionate use... 
10. disseminate information on quality and safety... 
11. disseminate specific information... 
12. establish and promote training programmes... 
13. collaborate with other national, regional and international institutions... 
14. recall products from the market;
15. levy, collect and utilise fees for the services it renders;
16. prescribe the standards appropriate for new medical products... 
17. investigate conduct related to the manufacture, import, export... 
18. formulate regulations and guidelines... 
19. perform such functions as may be assigned by the Committee.

### Establishment of Medical Products Regulatory Committee
**7.** There is established a Medical Products Regulatory Committee.

### Appointment of members to the Regulatory Committee
**8.** (1) The Minister shall appoint members to the Committee and shall designate one of the members as the Chairperson and Vice-Chairperson of the Committee.
(2) The Chairperson of the Committee shall be accountable to the Commissioner... 
(3) The salary and allowances... shall be in accordance with Government policies.
(4) The Committee shall consist of the following members—
    *   (a) the head of the Public Health Pharmacy Service;
    *   (b) the head of the Quality Control Laboratory;
    *   (c) a Veterinary Surgeon;
    *   (d) the Director of the Medicine Regulatory Service;
    *   (e) a pharmacist from the private sector;

### Functions of the Regulatory Committee
**9.** The functions of the Committee shall be to—
1. provide strategic guidance to the Service in the discharge of its functions;
2. review the annual reports presented by the Director of the Service;
3. monitor and evaluate the activities of the Service;
4. establish technical working teams... 
5. recommend, to the Minister, persons for appointment as the Director of the Service;
6. perform such other functions as maybe assigned by the Authority.

## PART III – NATIONAL REGULATORY SYSTEM

### Market authorisation
**10.**(1) All medical products, circulating in the area of jurisdiction of this Act must be registered and have a valid marketing authorisation... 
(2) The Service may determine that a medical product... shall be subject to exemption from marketing authorisation... 
(3) The Minister shall publish an authorization or determination... in the *Gazette*...
(4) [Transitional provision for existing products]...
(5) Sub-section (1) shall not apply in respect of the sale of any medical product compounded by a pharmacist for a particular patient... 

### Applications for market authorisation
**11.**(1) Every application for marketing authorisation ... shall be submitted to the Director in a prescribed form... 
(2) The Service may prescribe the standards appropriate for new medical products... 
(3) The Service may prescribe standards and procedures for referencing... 
(4) The Service, shall approve a medical product if it is satisfied—
    *   (a) that the product is suitable for the intended purpose in respect of its quality, safety and efficacy; and
    *   (b) that marketing authorisation is in the public interest.
(5) If the Service is not satisfied, it shall notify the applicant in writing... 
(6) A person who has been refused a market authorisation... shall have one month to make representation... 
(7) The Service shall publish the list or register of approved and registered medicines in the *Gazette*.
(8) Where the Service considers that circumstances may exist which would render necessary the consideration of whether the registration... should be varied, suspended or revoked... 

### Registration of manufacturers, importers, exporters
**12.**(1) No person shall manufacture, import, export, supply, store, distribute or sell at wholesale any medical product, unless the person is registered by the Authority.
(2) The conditions for registration... shall be as may be prescribed by the Service.
(3) Every manufacturer, importer, exporter, wholesaler and distributor shall comply with Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) and Good Distribution Practice (GDP)... 
(4) The Authority shall designate ports of entry for medical products imported into Seychelles.
(5) The Service shall maintain a register of all licensed premises... 

### Labels on medicines
**13.**(1) All medicines imported into Seychelles shall bear a label printed or affixed to the container legible in one of the national languages.
(2) The label ... shall be the original label produced by the manufacturer... 
(3) [Repackaging labels permission]...
(4) Subsections (1), (2) and (3) shall apply *mutatis mutandis* to inserts... 
(5) Any medical product... which does not conform... shall be liable to confiscation... 
(6) Whoever contravenes a provision under this section commits an offence... 

### Control of clinical trials of medical products
**14.**(1) No person shall conduct clinical trials of medical products in humans or animals without the authorisation from the Health Research and Ethics Committee... 
(2) All clinical trials shall be conducted in accordance with the guidelines... including GCP and GLP.
(3) A person shall not sell, dispense, supply, assemble or manufacture products for the purpose of a clinical trial... unless authorised... 
(4) The Service shall maintain a register of all clinical trials... 

### Promotion and advertisement of medical products
**15.**(1) The Service shall issue guidelines relating to the promotion and advertising of medical products... 

### National Pharmacovigilance Programme
**16.**(1) The Service shall established a National Pharmacovigilance Programme... 
(2) The Pharmacovigilance Programme shall—
    *   monitor and analyse the adverse effects... 
    *   identify and report adverse events relating to clinical trials;
    *   establish causality... 
    *   take appropriate action when necessary... 
(3) The Service shall—
    *   (a) issue guidelines to provide for reporting and submission of periodic safety updates... 
    *   (b) institute a risk-based testing scheme... 
(4) Where any medical product does not conform to the standards... the Director shall—
    *   (a) instruct the registered person... to discontinue the sale...
    *   (b) recall any portion of the batch already sold... 
    *   (c) provide the Service of proof of destruction... 
(5) The Director shall—
    *   (a) by order published in the *Gazette*, withdraw and strike off from the register, a medical product... hazardous to public health... 
    *   (b) issue a notice to the public... 
(6) If the Director is of the opinion that it is not in the public interest... the Director may direct that such product be withdrawn... 

### Appointment of authorised officers
**17.**(1) The Service shall—
    *   (a) appoint authorised officers with relevant qualifications... 
    *   (b) authorise such authorised officers to perform such functions as are stipulated under this Act.
(2) All authorised officers appointed under this Act shall carry valid identification... 

### Powers of authorised officers
**18.** An authorised officer appointed under this Act may—
1. at all reasonable times, enter any premises... 
2. examine or inspect any certificate of marketing authorisation, registration, book, electronic information storage system... 
3. take samples for analysis... 
4. seize any medical products or related substances... 
5. close the premises found to be in contravention of this Act;
6. recommend any contravention... for administrative, civil and or criminal proceedings.

### Donation of medical products
**19.**(1) Unless the Commissioner, by order exempts, all donations of medical products ... shall—
    *   (a) be based on an expressed need... 
    *   (b) not be sent without prior consent... 
    *   (c) be approved for use in the country... 
    *   (d) comply with the presentation, strength and formulation... 
    *   (e) be obtained from a reliable source... 
    *   (f) exclude products that were issued to patients and subsequently returned... 
    *   (g) upon arrival... have a remaining shelf-life of at least one year... 
    *   (h) be labelled in a language that is easily understood... 
    *   (i) as far as possible, be presented in large-quantity units... 
    *   (j) be packed in accordance with international shipping regulations... 
    *   (k) require... that the costs... be paid by the donor agency.
(2) Any donations being proposed to any non-governmental organisations and private entities shall notified to the Service... 

### Quality control laboratory
**20.**(1) There shall be established by the Service, a National quality control laboratory.
(2) The Commissioner shall... appoint a person ... as the head of the laboratory and such number of analysts... 
(3) The head of the laboratory shall... in particular —
    *   (a) analyse medical products... 
    *   (b) conduct research and training;
    *   (c) manage all the operations of the Laboratory;
    *   (d) train analysts... 
    *   (e) analyse products which are being sold, manufactured, or advertised... 
    *   (f) undertake such other function... 
(4) In performing its functions, the quality control laboratory may utilise... regulatory decisions... of any accredited Laboratory... 

### Scheduling and classification
**21.**(1) The scheduling, re-scheduling, and classification ... shall be determined by the Service and be published in the *gazette*.

### Control of medical products
**22.**
1. Control of medical products shall be based on the scheduling status... as follows:
    *   (a) scheduled substances that will be available for general sales... 
    *   (b) scheduled substances that will be available on the professional advice of a pharmacist... 
    *   (c) scheduled substances that will be available only on the prescription of an authorised person... 
    *   (d) scheduled substances that will be available only on prescription... subject to control measures (Narcotic/Psychotropic conventions)...
    *   (e) scheduled substances that may not be sold, except in accordance with a permit...

## PART VII - MONITORING AND EVALUATION

### Prohibition on import, export, manufacture etc. of medical products of scheduled substance without registration
**27.**(1) Any person who manufacture, import, export, supply, dispense, store, distribute or sell any medical products unless in possession of a registration... commits an offence.
(2) A person who commits an offence under subsection (1) shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding level 6... or to imprisonment not exceeding four years or to both.

**28.**(1) Any person who manufacture, import, export, supply, store, distribute or sell any substandard or falsified medical products commits an offence.
(2) A person who commits an offence under subsection (1) shall be liable... to a fine not exceeding level 6... or to imprisonment no exceeding five years or to both.

**29.**(1) Any person who obstructs or hinders any authorised officers... commits an offence.
(2) A person who commits an offence under subsection (1) shall be liable... to a fine not exceeding level 6... or to imprisonment not exceeding two years or to both.

### Fraudulent actions and misleading statements
**30.**(1) Any person who, with fraudulent intent, tampers with any sample... or makes any false or misleading statement... commits an offence.
(2) A person who commits an offence under subsection (1) shall be liable... to a fine not exceeding level 6... or to imprisonment not exceeding three years or to both.

### Other offences not provided for
**31.**(1) A person who contravenes any provision... for which no penalty has been specifically provided for commits an offence and shall be liable... to a fine not exceeding level 6... or to imprisonment not exceeding six years or to both.

### Appeals
**32.** A person who is aggrieved by a decision of the Service may lodge an appeal with the Appeals Board... 

## PART X - PHARMACIES AND RELATED ACTIVITIES

(6) For the purpose of this section a person shall not be eligible... to own or have a property share in more than 2 pharmacy businesses.
(7) Any person who contravenes subsection (3) and (4) commits an offence... 
(8) [Exceptions for executors, administrators, or bankruptcy trustees]...

### Requirement to provide information about pharmacy ownership
**35.**(1) The Service may require a person to give... any information or produce any documents relating to the ownership... 
(2) A person who fail or refuse... commits an offence... 

### Approval of pharmacy businesses
**36.**(1) The Authority may approve the premises of a pharmacy business if... suitable... 
(2) In approving a premises... the Authority shall have regard to—
    *   (a) the facilities, equipment, security... 
    *   (b) that the premises comply with good pharmacy practice;
    *   (c) any other requirements... 
(3) Without limiting the generality of subsection (2), the Authority may impose any condition... 

### Cause for disciplinary action
**37.** There shall be cause for disciplinary action... if the person —
1. registration or license renewal was improperly obtained;
2. fails to comply with any condition...
3. commits unprofessional conduct;
4. is for any reason no longer a fit and proper person... 

### Body corporate disqualified from authorisation to own or have a proprietary interest in pharmacy business under certain circumstances
**38.**(1) If a body corporate... is convicted of any offence under this Act... or any officer... has been convicted of a criminal offence... the Committee may... direct that the body cooperate is no longer authorised to own... 

### Managing pharmacist
**39.**(1) Every pharmacy shall be managed by a registered pharmacist with a minimum of five years professional experience.
(2) The managing pharmacist shall—
    *   (a) act as the managing pharmacist... 
    *   (b) ensure that he or she or another registered pharmacist sells or supervises the sale... 
    *   (c) keep any documents, information or evidence... 
    *   (d) carry out such obligations... 
    *   (e) nominate a substitute managing pharmacist... 
    *   (f) comply with regulations... 
    *   (g) dispose of medical products... 
(3) no pharmacist may act as a managing pharmacist for more than one pharmacy.

### Dispensing of medical products
**40.**(1) A medical product shall be prepared or dispensed only from a pharmacy and by a registered pharmacist...
(2) In carrying out his functions... a pharmacist shall adhere to such standards... 

### Sale of medical products
**41.**(1) A pharmacy shall sell or dispense only such medical products... as may be prescribed by regulations.
(2) Unless otherwise provided... a medical product shall be sold only from a registered pharmacy... 

### Dispensing of prescription medical products
**42.**(1) A pharmacist shall prepare or dispense any medical product... unless he or she has a justifiable concern... 
(2) When... a pharmacist discovers... a reason why the medical product should not be dispensed... the pharmacist shall be bound to draw the attention thereto of the person prescribing... 
(3) ... a pharmacist shall dispense the medical product prescribed or an equivalent medical product having the same chemical entity... 
(4) The pharmacist shall assume full responsibility for the dispensing of medical products which do not require a prescription... 

### Dispensing without prescription
**43.** A pharmacist shall not dispense any medical product except on the prescription of a medical or dental practitioner, veterinary surgeon... unless the medical product is deemed not to require a medical prescription... 

### Form of prescription
**44.** The Service may prescribe by regulations the format, content and presentation of a prescription.

### Labelling of dispensed medical products
**45.** The pharmacist shall label each medical product... in accordance with the regulations... 

### Prohibition on sale and disposal of imperfect medical products
**46.**(1) A managing pharmacist shall not sell, allow the sale of, dispense or supply... 
    *   (a) any imperfect, deteriorated or harmful medical products... 
    *   (b) any medical product bearing an expiry date which has expired;
