# Seychelles Strategy for the Prevention and Control of Noncommunicable Diseases, 2016-2025

The Strategy was presented at Cabinet and approved on the 30th of June 2016 (C16/D/MEM/154). The Strategy was subsequently presented by the Minister of Health at the National Assembly with no objection (Nation 28 July 2016).

**Note:** Noncommunicable diseases (NCDs) considered in this Strategy refer to four main noncommunicable diseases (cardiovascular disease, cancer, diabetes and obstructive pulmonary disease), which account for more than 65% of all deaths in Seychelles, and to four main underlying risk behaviours (tobacco use, unhealthy nutrition, physical inactivity and harmful use of alcohol). This strategy is abbreviated in this document as the “NCD Strategy”.

## Foreword

The National NCD strategy is our nation's response to the increasing prevalence of noncommunicable diseases, the risk factors that lead to these conditions and the threats that they pose to the health of the Seychellois. 

We know, from our own studies and experience, that four main noncommunicable diseases (cardiovascular disease, cancer, diabetes and obstructive pulmonary disease) account for more than 65% of all deaths in Seychelles. We know, again from our studies and from world-wide experience, that four main risk behaviours (tobacco use, unhealthy nutrition, physical inactivity and harmful use of alcohol) underlie these diseases.

**Mitcy Larue**  
Minister of Health

## Priority population and high risk interventions

### Table 2. Best buys for NCD prevention and control

| Risk factor or disease | "Best buy" interventions |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Tobacco use** | Raise taxes on tobacco, Ban smoking in public/work places, Warn about dangers, Enforce bans on advertising |
| **Harmful use of alcohol** | Raise taxes on alcohol, Restrict access to retailed alcohol, Enforce bans on advertising |
| **Unhealthy diet and physical inactivity** | Reduce salt intake, Replace trans fat, Promote public awareness |
| **CVD and Diabetes** | Counselling and multi-drug therapy for high risk, Treat heart attacks with aspirin |
| **Cancer** | Hepatitis B immunization, HPV vaccine, Screen/treat pre-cancerous lesions |

### Table 3. Good buys for prevention and control of NCDs

| Risk factor or disease | "Good buy" interventions |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Tobacco use** | Offer counselling to smokers |
| **Harmful use of alcohol** | Enforcing drink-driving laws, Brief advice for hazardous drinking |
| **Unhealthy diet and physical inactivity** | Restrictions on marketing high salt/fat/sugar foods, Food taxes/subsidies, School nutrition environments, Physical activity guidelines, Workplace programs |
| **CVD and Cancer** | Nicotine replacement therapy, Nutrition info and counselling |

# Part II: Situation and response in Seychelles

## Trends in NCDs
Cardiovascular disease and cancer account for more than 65% of all deaths in the country. While the total NCD burden is increasing due to population aging, age-standardized mortality rates for CVD and cancer have decreased.

# PART III. STRATEGY

## Vision
Seychelles free of the avoidable burden of NCD.

## Goal
To reduce the preventable and avoidable burden of morbidity, mortality and disability due to NCDs to ensure that all inhabitants reach the highest attainable standards of health, quality of life, and productivity.

## Objectives
1. Raise the priority accorded to prevention and control of NCDs.
2. Strengthen national capacity, leadership, governance, and multisectoral action.
3. Reduce modifiable risk factors through health-promoting environments.
4. Strengthen health systems (Primary Health Care and Universal Health Coverage).
5. Promote and support national capacity for high-quality research and development.
6. Monitor trends and determinants of NCDs.

## Overarching Principles
- **Human Right Approach**: Health as a fundamental right.
- **Equity-based Approach**: Addressing social determinants of health.
- **National Action & Personal Responsibility**: Balancing individual choices with supportive environments.
- **Multisectoral Action**: Whole-of-government approach.
- **Life Course Approach**: Interventions from antenatal care to healthy ageing.
- **Empowerment**: Involving communities in decision making.
- **Evidence-based Strategies**: Using scientific evidence and cost-effectiveness.
- **Universal Health Coverage**: Access without financial hardship.
- **Conflict of Interest Management**: Protecting public health from commercial interests.
