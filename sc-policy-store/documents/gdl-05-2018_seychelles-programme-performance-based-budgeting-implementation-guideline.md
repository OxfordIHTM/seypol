# The Government of Seychelles

**Programme Performance-Based Budgeting: Implementation Guideline**

*Version 7: May 2018*

## FOREWORD

### Purpose of the Guideline

This Guideline set out the PPBB System in the Seychelles. It is intended to guide the Ministry of Finance, Trade and Economic Planning (MoFTEP) and the MDAs on the steps required to first implement a PPBB medium-term budget in an MDA, as well as on the routine processes and institutions of the system once implemented.

The Guideline is a living document, reflecting the learning of the Government of Seychelles in implementing PPBB medium-term budgeting. This is the seventh version, issued in April 2018, and takes into account the learning from implementing PPBB since 2012.

The Guideline therefore replaces all previous versions.

### Main guideline structure

The Guideline is set out in three main parts, with constituent chapters.

#### PART 1: PPBB in the Seychelles
This Part provides an overview of the PPBB system, and the roll-out plan. It comprises one chapter:
* **Chapter 1:** The PPBB system in the Seychelles: Implementation and key features

#### PART 2: Preparing the Budget According to Programmes and Performance
This Part provides guidance on processes and rules to design programme structures, and develop performance information. It comprises two chapters:
* **Chapter 2:** The Use of Programmes in PPBB
* **Chapter 3:** Developing and managing programme performance information

#### Part 3: PPBB Budget Preparation and Implementation
This Part provides guidance on processes, roles and responsibilities, rules and systems to prepare and implement budgets in the Seychelles PPBB system, for spending institutions, the Ministry of Finance, Trade and the Blue Economy and Cabinet. It also provides guidance on standard expenditure analyses that budget actors can undertake in preparing and assessing budgets. It comprises three chapters:
* **Chapter 4:** MDA Budget Preparation, Submission and Presentation
* **Chapter 5:** The Central Budget Process: Assessing and allocating PPBB Budgets
* **Chapter 6:** Expenditure analyses for PPBB
* **Chapter 7:** Budget Implementation, Reporting and Audit in PPBB

### Headline summary pages

Each chapter includes at the start a headline summary section with the key concepts, rules and step-by-step instructions covered in the Chapter. This summary is set out in accessible language, and linked to the sections in the main text. The summaries are intended to assist users to get the relevant headline information for each chapter, without having to search through the detailed explanations of the main guideline.

# PART 1: SUMMARY OF THE PPBB SYSTEM

## Chapter 1: THE PPBB SYSTEM IN SEYCHELLES: IMPLEMENTATION AND KEY FEATURES

### HEADLINE SUMMARY

#### Section 1: PPBB in Seychelles: Implementation and main concepts

**A. The purpose of PPBB**
Programme performance-based budgeting (PPBB) is a budgeting and reporting system which aims to improve the effectiveness and efficiency of public expenditure. 

**Benefits of PPBB:**
* **Oversight:** Better information to see whether plans and budgets are linked to government’s priorities.
* **Managerial:** Less micro-management by higher levels and more commitment from operational levels.
* **Political:** Clearer link between policies, expenditure, and outcomes.

**B. Roles of budget actors in a PPBB system**

| Actor | Accountabilities and Roles |
| :--- | :--- |
| **MDAs** | Accountable for policy performance and regular use of resources. Develop policies, propose trade-offs, plan performance, and report on financial/non-financial results. |
| **MoFTEP** | Accountable for fiscal performance and cost-effective use of resources across MDAs. Sets macrofiscal framework, allocates resources, enforces controls, and sets system rules. |
| **Cabinet** | Politically accountable for fiscal and policy performance. Sets national priorities, approves budget proposals, and monitors government performance. |
| **Legislature (NA)** | Politically accountable to voters for oversight. Reviews and approves expenditure proposals and holds the executive to account. |

**C. Implementing PPBB in the Seychelles**
* **2013:** Started with pilot ministries (MOFA and MOE).
* **2014:** First PPBB format budgets prepared.
* **2015:** Implementation of pilot budgets; three more pilot ministries started.
* **2016:** All MDAs started budgeting in "Presentational PPBB" (without performance information).
* **2017+:** Transition to full PPBB systems.
* **2018:** All MDAs planning, budgeting, and implementing using PPBB for the 2019 budget.

#### Section 2: Seychelles approach to PPBB

PPBB is part of a larger results-based management reform including:
* Medium Term National Development Strategy (MTNDS).
* Performance Monitoring and Evaluation (PME).
* Performance Management System (PMS).

**New Structures, Roles and Responsibilities:**
* **Portfolios:** A ministry plus its departments and agencies within a functional sector.
* **Portfolio Working Group (PWG):** Allocates resources within a single ceiling between MDAs in the Portfolio.
* **MDA Accounting Officers:** Lead and manage the entire PPBB process for their MDA.
* **MDA PPBB Working Groups:** Ensure strategic plans are incorporated into budgeting and coordinate submissions.
* **PPBB Programme Managers:** Responsible for a budget programme/sub-programme; set performance measures and targets.

### TOP DOWN, BOTTOM UP AND RECONCILIATION PROCESS

| Phase | Top Down Processes | Bottom Up Processes | Reconciliation Processes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Strategic Phase** (March-June) | MoFTEP issues indicative resource envelopes and MTES circular. | MDAs draft MTES strategies against ceilings; undertake baseline costing. | MoFTEP reviews submissions, meets with MDAs, and issues budget ceilings. |
| **Detailed Budget Planning** (July-Aug) | MoFTEP issues budget ceilings by MDA and economic category. | Budget submissions due; Draft PPBB Statements prepared in PPBB Tool. | MoFTEP sets Review meetings; Cabinet agrees final allocations; Allocation letters issued. |
| **Budget Presentation** (Sept-Oct) | MoFTEP issues final allocation emails. | MDAs revise draft PPBB Statements based on final allocations. | MoFTEP reviews statements for content and consistency; compiles Budget document for NA. |

# PART 2: CENTRAL BUILDING BLOCKS OF PPBB: PROGRAMMES AND PERFORMANCE MEASURES

## Chapter 2: THE USE OF PROGRAMMES IN PPBB

### SECTION 1: Defining budget programmes

Budget programmes are coherent groupings of activities designed to fulfil common objectives. 

**Key Features:**
* Budget by objective, not administrative structure.
* Stability over the medium/long term.
* Typically 3-5 programmes per MDA (approx. 20-33% of budget each; not less than 10%).

**Programme 1: Governance, Management and Administration**
All MDAs create this programme to cover central leadership, HR, financial management, and IT support. Performance measures are generally not required for Programme 1.

### SECTION 2: Revising programme structures

Revisions occur if the mandate changes or the structure is not conducive to strategic choices. These changes must be consulted with MoFTEP and occur between January and March.

**Process for developing structure:**
1. List all functions (core vs. management).
2. Group functions into programmes/sub-programmes.
3. Align with long-term mandates.
4. Develop objective statements.
5. Map administrative units to the structure.
6. Identify funding overlaps.
7. Identify Programme Managers.
8. Review against checklist.

## Chapter 3: DEVELOPING AND MANAGING PPBB PERFORMANCE INFORMATION

### SECTION 1: Defining Performance Information for PPBB

**Hierarchy of Performance Concepts:**
* **Inputs:** Resources (Personnel, equipment).
* **Activities:** Processes that use inputs to produce outputs.
* **Outputs:** Final products/services delivered (e.g., teaching hours).
* **Outcomes:** Medium-term results for beneficiaries (e.g., literacy rates).
* **Impacts:** Long-term results (e.g., poverty reduction).

**Required Indicators per Programme:**
* At least 1 Outcome indicator.
* At least 1 Output or Efficiency indicator (per sub-programme).

### SECTION 2: Steps in developing PPBB Performance Indicators

1. **Discuss Strategic Improvements:** Identify service delivery gaps $\rightarrow$ convert to priorities $\rightarrow$ build Theory of Change.
2. **Design Measures:** Link priorities to outcomes $\rightarrow$ design output/efficiency measures.
3. **Assess Quality:** Use SMART criteria (Specific, Measurable, Appropriate, Relevant, Time-bound) and check data availability.
4. **Record Base Year:** Data from most recent fiscal year.
5. **Determine Targets:** Set annual medium-term targets.
6. **Review:** Senior management approval against likely resources.

# PART 3: PPBB MEDIUM-TERM BUDGET PREPARATION AND IMPLEMENTATION

## Chapter 4: MDA BUDGET PREPARATION, SUBMISSION AND PRESENTATION

### Section 1: Baseline projections

A baseline is the calculation of forward costs with no changes from the previous year, accounting only for external factors (inflation) and confirmed commitments.

### Section 2: Preparing an MTES

**MTES (Medium-Term Expenditure Strategy)** is the strategic instrument used to make trade-offs within a ceiling. 

**Spending Space formula:**
`New spending space = MDA indicative envelope + other revenue – MDA baseline projections`

### Section 4: The PPBB Statements

This is the final document presented to the National Assembly. It includes:
* Budget Summary table.
* Ministry Strategic Overview (Mandate, achievements, challenges, priorities).
* Programme Strategic Overview (Structure, performance measures, targets, expenditure tables).

## Chapter 5: THE CENTRAL BUDGET PROCESS

### Section 2: MoFTEP Roles

1. **Strategic Phase:** Issue indicative ceilings $\rightarrow$ assess MTES documents $\rightarrow$ hold review meetings $\rightarrow$ recommend ceilings to Cabinet.
2. **Budget Planning Phase:** Issue firm budget ceilings $\rightarrow$ review draft PPBB Statements $\rightarrow$ finalize allocations via Cabinet.
3. **Presentation Phase:** Review, edit, and compile final PPBB Statements for the NA.

## Chapter 6: EXPENDITURE ANALYSES FOR PPBB

**Budget Analysis Techniques:**
* **Comparing Planned vs. Realised:** Evaluates budget system efficiency.
* **Budget Share Analysis:** Identifies real priorities (e.g., personnel vs. services).
* **Trend Analysis:** Tracks growth rates over time.
* **Real Value Conversion:** Adjusts nominal figures for inflation using a deflator.
* **Unit Cost Analysis:** Measures economy and efficiency (cost per output/outcome).

**Economic Analysis Types:**
* **Cost Analysis:** Affordability.
* **Cost-Benefit Analysis:** Evaluating if an intervention should be undertaken.
* **Cost-Effectiveness Analysis:** finding the cheapest way to achieve an outcome.
* **Benefit-Incidence Analysis:** Checking equity in distribution.

## Chapter 7: BUDGET IMPLEMENTATION, REPORTING AND AUDIT

### Section 1: PPBB Budget Implementation

**Virement Rules:**
* **Permitted:** Between programmes/sub-programmes (within limits) and between "blue" (lower-level) economic items.
* **Prohibited:** Between "black" (higher-level) economic items and from capital to recurrent without MoFTEP/NA approval.

### Section 2: PPBB Reporting

| Report Type | Frequency | Recipient | Content |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Internal Financial** | Monthly | MoFTEP | Actual vs. Budget; Trend vs. Prior Year |
| **Internal/External** | Quarterly | MoFTEP / Public | Detailed expenditure by programme/economic item |
| **Mid-Year Report** | Bi-annual | NA / MoFTEP | Comprehensive revenue/expenditure; Policy performance $|
| **Section 31 Report** | Annual | NA / MoFTEP | Audited outturns; Performance targets vs. actuals |

### Section 3: Audit and Oversight

* **Internal Audit:** Include PPBB processes from 2020.
* **External Audit:** Auditor General phases in performance information auditing from 2020.
