24. (1) When an instance of a notifiable disease occurs in an organization, the head of the organization shall, as soon as possible, notify the Commissioner.

(8) If a person fails to comply with any provision of this section, that person commits an offence and is liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding five thousand rupees or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding six months, or to both.

(b) any act done or omitted by any person in contravention of this Act, shall be an offence and such person shall be liable to a fine not exceeding the amount specified in the schedule to this Act, or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding the period specified in the schedule to this Act, or to both.

(c) any person who is convicted of an offence under this Act, is liable to a further fine not exceeding the amount specified in the schedule to this Act, for every day during which the offence continues after conviction.

(d) any person who is convicted of an offence under this Act, may be ordered by the court to rectify the breach of the Act, and shall be liable to a further fine for every day which the breach continues after the date onspecified by the court for rectification.

(e) any court may impose a penalty or fine on any person who has failed to comply with any provision of this Act, or any regulation made under this Act, or who has committed an offence under this Act.
