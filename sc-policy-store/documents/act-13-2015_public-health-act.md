26. **Offences and Penalties**

(1) Any person who contravenes any provision of this Act
commits an offence and shall be liable on conviction to a fine
not exceeding [Amount] or to imprisonment for a term not
exceeding [Duration], or to both.

(2) Where an offence under this section is committed by a
body corporate, every person who at the time of the commission
of the offence was a director, manager, secretary or other
similar officer of the body corporate shall be deemed to have
committed the offence unless he proves that the offence was
committed without his knowledge, consent or connivance.

(3) A person shall not be liable to be convicted of an offence
under subsection (2) if he can prove that he exercised all
due diligence to prevent the commission of the offence.

(4) The court may, upon conviction of a person for an offence
under this section, make an order for the forfeiture of any
property or assets that were used in the commission of the
offence or were obtained as a result of the commission of the
offence.

(5) The court may also, in addition to any penalty imposed
under this section, order the offender to pay compensation
to any person who has suffered loss or damage as a result of the
offence.

(6) The Minister may, in the case of a first offence, and where
the offence is of a minor nature, elect not to prosecute the
