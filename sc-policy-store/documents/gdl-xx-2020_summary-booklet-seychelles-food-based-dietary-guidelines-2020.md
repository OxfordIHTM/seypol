# Seychelles Food-Based Dietary Guidelines 2020
**General population (from 5 years old)**

---

## Daily Meal Plan (Serves 1 person)

### Breakfast
* 1 tub plain yoghurt + 2 slices bread
* **Frittata**
    * **Ingredients:** 2 eggs, ½ cup of vegetables (e.g. carrot, capsicum, spinach, tomato, and onion), 1 clove garlic (optional), 1 boiled potato, 1tbsp of cheese, 1tbsp of fresh parsley, pinch of salt & ground black pepper, 1tsp olive oil.
    * **Instructions:**
        1. Whisk eggs in a bowl. Add salt, pepper & herbs.
        2. Heat a pan with one teaspoon oil then stir-fry all vegetables and potato for a few minutes. Add eggs and cook until edge starts to set.
        3. Place under a hot grill until eggs are completely cooked. Serve immediately.

### Lunch
* 1 medium fruit (e.g. banana)
* 2 medium pieces of steamed breadfruit
* 3tbsp Chutney (e.g. papaya, pumpkin, eggplant)
* 3tbsp Lentils
* **Grilled Fish (~85g)**
    * **Ingredients:** 1 medium size fish fillet/steak, 2tsp of fresh parsley, ½ tsp of lemon juice, 1tsp of olive oil, chopped tomato, onion, pinch of salt and ground black pepper.
    * **Instructions:**
        1. Marinate the fish with mixture of all ingredients for at least one hour.
        2. Grill the fish until cooked.

### Dinner
* 2 medium pieces of steamed cassava
* Vegetable salad (~1 cup of any vegetables e.g. carrots, cucumber, lettuce, watercress, gourd)
* **Fish Curry**
    * **Ingredients:** 1 medium size (85g) fish fillet chopped into cubes, ½ tomato chopped, ½ onion chopped, 2tsp of oil, 1tsp curry powder, ½ tsp turmeric, 1 tsp of garlic and ginger crushed.
    * **Instructions:**
        1. Stir-fry onion, ginger, garlic in oil for a few minutes.
        2. Add turmeric and curry powder and cook for an additional 2 to 3 minutes.
        3. Add in tomatoes, stir, then add in some water. Season with salt and pepper.
        4. Add the fish and cook for about 5 minutes or until fish is cooked through.

## Dietary Recommendations

### 1. Variety and Balance
Enjoy a variety of foods from all the food groups every day to maintain good health. Eat a balanced diet everyday by including a wide variety of foods from the five food groups.

**Key recommendations:**
* Eat foods in the right proportions as shown in the Seychelles Food Guide (Figure 1).
* Choose more plant-based foods including fruits, vegetables, grains, tubers, breadfruit, plantains, pulses, nuts & seeds every day.
* Have moderate amounts of fish, poultry, eggs, milk & milk products.
* Reduce your intake of red meat.
* Include smaller amounts of healthy fats such as olive/canola oil.

### 2. Fruits and Vegetables
Eat different coloured fruits and vegetables every day to keep your digestive system healthy. This can reduce risk of type 2 diabetes, hypertension and some cancers.

**Key recommendations:**
* Eat at least 2 portions of fruits & 3 portions of vegetables (5 total) daily.
* Include a wide variety of different coloured fruits & vegetables.
* Choose fresh, local, and seasonal produce; prefer raw over cooked/canned.
* Limit the use of sauces, salt, sugar, or fat in these dishes.

**What is a portion?** (Equivalent to 80g)
* 1 medium fruit (banana, orange, star fruit)
* 1 slice of a large fruit (pawpaw, pineapple, melon)
* 2 small fruits (star fruit, guavas, plums)
* 3 tablespoons / 1/2 cup of fresh fruit salad
* 3 tablespoons / 1/2 cup of raw, cooked, frozen or canned vegetables
* 1 cup of green leafy vegetables (e.g. moringa soup)

### 3. Beans, Lentils, Nuts and Seeds
Regular intake can help prevent obesity, some cancers, diabetes and heart disease.

**Key recommendations:**
* Include a variety of pulses, nuts and seeds in your diet.
* Swap one meat dish for a plant-based alternative at least twice per week.
* Aim for one portion daily or several times a week.

**What is a portion?**
* ½ cup or 3 tablespoons of cooked dry beans, peas and lentils
* ½ cup of canned cooked pulses
* 3 tablespoons of nuts
* 1 tablespoon nut butters

### 4. Fish and Healthy Proteins
Eat fish regularly, including oily fish (fresh tuna, bonito, mackerel) for a healthy brain.

**Key recommendations:**
* Eat a variety of fish on at least 5 days a week.
* Include oily fish at least twice per week.
* Prefer grilled, baked, steamed or chutney over fried.
* Limit meat to 1 portion per day (85g) and no more than 2 portions per week.

**What is a portion?**
* 100g cooked fish (without bones)
* 85g cooked poultry or red meat (without bones)
* 2 whole cooked eggs

### 5. Whole Grains and Local Tubers
Choose whole grains and local tubers instead of refined rice for a healthier heart.

**Key recommendations:**
* Replace white bread/cornflakes with cassava flat bread or oatmeal.
* Substitute white rice for starchy roots and tubers regularly.
* Limit sauces, salt, sugar, and fat in these meals.

**What is a portion?** (Recommended 5-6 portions daily)
* 2 thick slices (40g) of bread
* 1 medium bowl (80g) of wholegrain breakfast cereal
* 1 cup (250g) cooked oats
* 1 cup (120g) cooked rice / 1 cup (160g) cooked pasta
* 1 large (80g) boiled plantain
* 2 medium (130g) boiled potato/sweet potato/taro/yam
* 2 pieces (90g) boiled cassava
* 2 large pieces (140g) breadfruit / 2 cassava flat bread

### 6. Milk and Milk Products
Consume daily for stronger bones and teeth (calcium).

**Key recommendations:**
* Choose low-fat, plain, and unsweetened milk products.
* If unable to have dairy, find alternative calcium sources.

**What is a portion?** (Recommended 2-3 portions daily)
* 1 cup of milk (250ml)
* 1 small piece of cheese (40g)
* 1 small pot of yoghurt (150ml)

**Alternative calcium sources (approx. 300mg):**
* 3½ Tbsp dry moringa leaves / 60g fresh moringa
* 100g sardines with bone
* 1½ cups of chickpeas
* 1 cup fortified soya/almond milk

### 7. Hydration
Drink water throughout the day instead of sugar-sweetened drinks.

**Key recommendations:**
* Adults/Adolescents: minimum 8 glasses (1.5 litres) of plain water daily.
* Drink a glass every 2 hours.
* Increase intake if exercising, working in heat, or consuming alcohol/caffeine.

**Tips to increase intake:**
* Keep a refillable bottle with you.
* Add slices of lime or orange for flavor.
* Drink a glass of water after every trip to the toilet.

### 8. Salt and Sugar Reduction
Reduce intake to prevent high blood pressure, heart disease, and obesity.

**Salt Guidelines:**
* Adult limit: ≤ 5g (1 tsp) per day.
* Children limit: ≤ 2g (½ tsp) per day.
* **Tip:** Use herbs, spices, lemon, lime, ginger, and garlic instead of salt.

**Sugar Guidelines:**
* Adult recommendation: < 12 tsp per day (ideally < 6 tsp).
* Children: < 6 tsp per day.
* **Tip:** Choose water over fizzy drinks; choose fresh fruit over sugary snacks.

### 9. Physical Activity
Be physically active every day for a healthy body and mind.

**Table 1: Physical Activity Recommendations**

| Target Group | Key Recommendation | Additional Details |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Adults (>18 years)** | At least 150m moderate aerobic OR 75m vigorous aerobic per week | Aerobic bouts should be at least 10 mins. Muscle-strengthening 2+ days/week. |
| **Children (5-17 years)** | At least 60m moderate-to-vigorous physical activity daily | Most should be aerobic. Muscle/bone strengthening 3+ times/week. |

* **Moderate examples:** Brisk walking, dancing, household chores.
* **Vigorous examples:** Running, fast cycling, fast swimming, moving heavy loads.

### 10. Alcohol Consumption
Avoid alcohol to prevent liver damage and other chronic diseases.

**Key recommendations:**
* Limit intake to max 2 units/day and 14 units/week.
* Abstain for at least 3 days per week.
* Non-drinkers should continue to abstain.
