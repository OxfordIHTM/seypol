## Definitions
- **Abortion:** Termination of a pregnancy before it is viable as an independent life outside of the womb.
- **Disease:** Any condition that causes pain, dysfunction, distress, social problems, and / or death.
- **Emergency:** Any event / crisis that exceeds the community’s ability to respond.
- **Essential Health Services:** The set of health promotion, disease prevention, curative and rehabilitative services critical to the population of Seychelles.
- **Health:** A state of complete physical, mental and social well-being and not merely the absence of disease or infirmity.
- **Health System:** The mechanism to ensure access to quality health care services as demanded by the population.
- **Incidence:** The number of new cases of a variable of interest, in a defined period of time for a given population.
- **Prevalence:** The proportion of the population having a variable of interest.

## CHAPTER 1: INTRODUCTION

### 1.1 Background to the NHSP
The creation of a new national health system at the end of the 1970s was inspired by the principles adopted at the birth of the Seychellois nation and the "Health for All" movement. Government undertook responsibility to provide health services financed exclusively from public funds.

### 1.2 Rationale for the NHSP
The Plan flows from the National Health Policy and sets milestones to optimize investment in health. It acknowledges that health is a societal enterprise and invites all sectors to engage in coordinated action.

### 1.3 Overview of the Sustainable Development Framework
Sustainability demands that human well-being be built on a model that values and protects the environment. The challenge is to maximize opportunities from a globalized world while mitigating negative lifestyle influences.

### 1.4 Overview of the existing policy foundations
Health strategies are consistent with the sustainable development agenda. Key references include:
- Seychelles Sustainable Development Strategy 2012–2020
- Seychelles Strategic Plan 2015–2040
- SAMOA Pathway (SIDS Accelerated Modalities for Action)
- UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)

### 1.5 Development process and outline of the NHSP
The NHSP provides guidance to sectoral strategic plans and forms the basis for budgeting. 

**Planning Framework Summary:**
`Global Health Agenda` $\rightarrow$ `Seychelles Health Policy` $\rightarrow$ `NHSP (2016-2020)` $\rightarrow$ `Budget Framework` $\rightarrow$ `Annual Operational Plans`.

## CHAPTER 2: HEALTH CHALLENGES AND CONTEXT

### 2.1 Demographics
In 2014, the population was estimated at 91,400. There is a gradual shift towards older age brackets (45-64 and 65+), which will increase demand for specialized health services.

### 2.2 The State of Health in Seychelles
**Life Expectancy:** In 2014, women averaged 78.3 years and men 68.4 years. The gap between genders remains wide.

#### 2.2.3 Mortality Causes & Disease Burden
Non-communicable conditions (NCDs) are the main causes of morbidity. 
- **Circulatory system:** 28% of total deaths.
- **Neoplasms:** 17% of total deaths.
- **Respiratory system:** 14% of total deaths.

**HIV/AIDS Focus:** 441 persons were living with HIV in 2014. There was a marked increase in new cases in 2014 (91 cases).

## CHAPTER 3: THE NHSP STRATEGIC AGENDA

### 3.1 Overarching Focus
**Vision:** Attainment by all people in Seychelles of the highest level of physical, social, mental and spiritual health.
**Mission:** To relentlessly promote, protect and restore health & quality of life and dignity of all people.

### 3.2 Health Objectives and Targets
- 25% relative reduction in mortality from CVD, cancer, diabetes, and chronic respiratory diseases.
- 30% relative reduction in current tobacco use (15+ years).
- 90% of people living with HIV will know their status.
- Infant mortality rate target: 10 per 1,000 live births.

### 3.3 Essential Health Services Package

### 3.4 Strategic Investment Priorities

#### 3.4.1 Strengthened Integrated Health Care
Focuses on: Organization of delivery, Infrastructure, Emergency services, Health Information Management (HIS), Quality of care, and Products/Technologies.

#### 3.4.2 Promoting and Protecting Health
Focuses on addressing Behavioral, Environmental, and Metabolic risk factors.

#### 3.4.3 Human Resources for Health
Focuses on regulation, production (training), recruitment, remuneration, and retention of the workforce.

#### 3.4.4 Sustainable Financing for Health
Focuses on innovative resource generation, cost awareness, efficiency, and Public-Private Partnerships (PPP).

#### 3.4.5 Research and Innovation
Focuses on evidence-based decision making, biomedical and systems research, and knowledge management.

#### 3.4.6 Partnership and Coordination
Focuses on multi-stakeholder engagement including other Ministries, NGOs, and international partners.

#### 3.4.7 Governance and Leadership
Focuses on stewardship, the legal/regulatory framework, and accountability.

## CHAPTER 5: IMPLEMENTATION FRAMEWORK

### Roles and Responsibilities
- **Cabinet of Ministers:** Monitor contribution to national strategic goals.
- **Ministry of Health:** Oversee national health sector and mobilize funds.
- **Public Health Authority:** Regulate health services and premises.
- **Health Care Agency:** Deliver health services in line with Policy/NHSP.

### Implementation Risks
1. Separation of MOH and Management of Health Services (High Probability/High Impact).
2. Financial decentralization and capacity building.
3. External influences (Finance Ministry, Public Admin).
4. Resource availability vs. investment needs.
