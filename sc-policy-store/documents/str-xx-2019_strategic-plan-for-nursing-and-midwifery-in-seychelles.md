### SECTION ONE: SITUATION ANALYSIS

#### 1.1 INTRODUCTION AND BACKGROUND

##### 1.1.1 INTRODUCTION

The Republic of Seychelles is a small, high-income island state situated in the western Indian Ocean. The country comprises 115 tropical islands spread over 45,166 hectares. Seychelles is more than 1,500 km from the East Coast of Africa, indicating its remoteness from the mainland continent.

With gross national income per capita of US$14,760 in 2015, Seychelles is classified as a high-income country. Tourism accounts for 35 percent of GDP, 25 percent of employment, and 70 percent of foreign exchange earnings.

##### 1.1.2 THE HEALTH SECTOR

The overarching health sector vision according to the National Health Policy is *“the attainment, by all people in Seychelles, of the highest level of physical, social, mental and spiritual health and living in harmony with nature”*.

**Key Public Bodies:**
* **Health Ministry:** Responsible for formulation of health sector policy development, planning, monitoring and evaluation.
* **Health Care Agency (HCA):** Manages the provision of primary, secondary and tertiary care. Main employer of nurses and midwives.
* **Public Health Authority (PHA):** Regulates the health sector and provide for the protection of the population's health.
* **National Institute of Health and Social Services (NIHSS):** The academic arm providing pre-service and continuous in-service education.

### SECTION TWO: STRATEGIC PLAN FOR NURSING AND MIDWIFERY

#### 2.1 VISION STATEMENT
To achieve professional excellence in education, clinical practice, research, and management, using nursing expertise in responding to population health needs and shaping health policies through purposeful engagement with all partners for quality and cost-effective nursing/midwifery care and the attainment of the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.

#### 2.2 MISSION STATEMENT
Nurses and midwives advancing the profession, using professional knowledge, and applying advanced skills and compassion to provide person and family-centered care based on best practice for the optimal well being and positive health outcomes.

#### 2.3 GUIDING PRINCIPLES
* Delivering the highest level of care with compassion and competence.
* Health is a human right; patients must be empowered.
* Integrity and respect for the dignity of every person.
* Commitment to lifelong learning.
* Accountability and advocacy for patients.

### SECTION THREE: STRATEGIC DIRECTIONS (THEMES)

#### 3. STRATEGIC GOALS AND INTERVENTIONS

1. **Nurse Workforce Planning:** Ensure an effective, competent, sustainable nursing workforce.
2. **Education Transformation:** Strengthen workforce through targeted professional development.
3. **Research & Innovation:** Promote an enabling environment for evidence-based practice.
4. **Regulation Modernization:** Institute high standards of self-governance and public protection.
5. **Leadership & Governance:** Strengthen management at all levels to maximize professional contribution.

**Strategic Directions Tables (Summary)**

| Direction | Key Activities | Key Outputs |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Workforce** | Workforce audit, scoping new roles, establishing nurse-patient ratios. | Audit report, staffing norms, career pathways. |
| **2. Education** | Build educator capacity, revise curricula, strengthen CPD, innovative marketing. | Revised curricula, CPD programs, higher enrollment. |
| **3. Research** | Establish research bodies, build capacity in research, implement models of care. | Research agenda, evidence-based culture, ICT training. |
| **4. Regulation** | Revise Nurses and Midwives ACT 1985, audit scopes of practice. | Revised Act, defined scopes of practice, national standards. |
| **5. Leadership** | Build leadership capacity, mentoring programs, empower nurses in policy. | Trained leaders, increased visibility in health policy. |
