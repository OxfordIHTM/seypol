## 1. INTRODUCTION

Seychelles needs a National Climate Change Policy (NCCP) to guide all stakeholder actions for Climate Change taking place on the islands... The target is to keep the earth’s average temperature from rising more than 2⁰ C and more importantly for the Seychelles for climate proofing.

---

## 2. BACKGROUND

### 2.1 Seychelles and Climate Change

**Geography of Seychelles**

The unique Seychelles islands are an archipelago made up of about 115 islands and a massive Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ) of about 1.37 million square kilometres...

**The responsibility of Seychelles to Climate Change**

Although Seychelles still remains an insignificant emitter of the GHGs, its level of susceptibility to the negative impacts of Climate Change will keep increasing... Seychelles emits less than 0.003% of global GHGs.

**Impacts of Climate Change in Seychelles**

Climate Change impacts both present and future generations of Seychelles... For SIDS like Seychelles, extreme weather events and SLR are major concerns.

**Solutions to Climate Change in Seychelles**

Seychelles addresses the negative impacts of Climate Change through adaptation and reduces its carbon footprint through mitigation.

### 2.2 Institutional Developments

*   **National Climate Change Committee:** Established in 1992 to provide overall coordination of the national climate programme.
*   **Seychelles Meteorological Authority (SMA):** Established in 2016 to provide early warning on weather and climate phenomena.
*   **Seychelles Energy Commission (SEC):** Set up in 2009 to regulate, manage, and develop energy resources.
*   **Ministry of Environment, Energy and Climate Change (MEECC):** Formed in 2015, comprising the DECC and DE.

---

## 3. THE CROSS-CUTTING NATURE OF CLIMATE CHANGE

Climate Change should not be seen as an isolated and stand-alone issue but one which is cross-cutting and interlinked to the economy, society and the environment.

---

## 4. APPROACH

**Definition of Climate Change (UNFCCC, 1992):**
> “A change of the climate which is attributed directly or indirectly to human activity that alters the composition of the global atmosphere and which is in addition to natural climate variability observed over comparable time periods.”

In recognition of these consequences, the NCCP adopts a **Framework Approach**.

---

## 5. APPLICABILITY AND SCOPE

The NCCP applies to the jurisdiction of Seychelles.

---

## 6. GUIDING CLIMATE PRINCIPLES including principles of Climate Justice

*   **A Rights-Based Approach to Climate Change:** Focus on protecting the rights of those most vulnerable.
*   **Common But Differentiated Responsibilities:** Recognition that responsibilities differ between developed and developing states.
*   **Precautionary Principle:** Lack of full scientific certainty should not be an excuse for failing to act.
*   **Integration and Interrelationship:** Full participation of all people in the community.
*   **Sustainable Development:** Meeting needs of the present without compromising future generations.

## 8. POLICY VISION

> **“A sustainable, climate resilient and low-carbon Seychelles”**

---

## 9. POLICY OBJECTIVES

**Overall Objective:** To facilitate a coordinated, coherent, proactive and effective response to the local, regional and global challenges and opportunities presented by Climate Change.

**Specific Objectives:**
1.  **Advance understanding** of Climate Change and its impacts.
2.  **Strengthen capacity** and social empowerment.
3.  **Mainstream and integrate** Climate Change considerations into all sectors.
4.  **Achieve transition** to a low carbon economy.
5.  **Put in place measures to adapt**, build resilience and minimize vulnerability.
6.  **Contribute effectively** to regional and global negotiations.

---

## 10. STIPULATIONS AND POLICY IMPLEMENTATION

### 10.1 INSTITUTIONAL FRAMEWORK

**National Climate Change Council:**
*   **Chair:** Vice President of Seychelles.
*   **Vice-Chair:** Minister for Energy and Climate Change.
*   **Composition:** Maximum 15 members (Government, Private Sector, Civil Society, Youth).

**Functions of the Council:**
*   Ensure mainstreaming of Climate Change across sectors.
*   Oversee reporting and target assessment.
*   Advise government on legislative and policy measures.
*   Coordinate spending of adaptation funds.

---

## 11. POLICY REVIEW PROCESS

The NCCP will be reviewed in 5 years’ time, or when viewed as necessary.

## GLOSSARY

*   **Adverse effects of Climate Change:** Changes in the physical environment resulting in significant deleterious effects.
*   **Climate Change:** Anthropogenic alteration of the global atmosphere.
*   **Greenhouse Gases:** Gaseous constituents that absorb and re-emit infrared radiation.
