# PRESCRIPTION FEES

## Introduction

In the Seychelles Government budget speech for the fiscal year 2019, the Minister of Finance, Trade, Investment and Economic Planning (MFTIEP) announced that the introduction of a prescription fee of **SCR 25** in the health service. It was also stated that the medicine will remain free of charge; the children under the age of 18 years, full-time students, elderly and patient with chronic disease will be exempted from the prescription fee; and that it will be implemented in the first quarter of 2019.

The MFTIEP has indicated that cashiers will be seconded to HCA and that HCA will be assisted with the collection of the fees.

## Exemption Criteria

1. **Elderly** - 63 years old and above, early retirement with proof
2. **Children** - 18 and below
3. **Students** over 18 with proof
4. **Patients requiring treatment for chronic conditions**:
    1. Epilepsy
    2. Mental health disorders
    3. Dementia
    4. Parkinson’s disease
    5. Neuropathic Pain
    6. Substance dependence
    7. Cancer
    8. Palliative
    9. Cardiovascular diseases
    10. Prostate disease
    11. Obstructive airway diseases
    12. Osteoporosis
    13. Chronic bowel disorders
    14. Chronic inflammatory skin diseases
    15. Chronic allergic conditions (sinusitis, rhinitis/nasal polyps, dermatology)
    16. HIV /AIDS
    17. Hepatitis B & C
    18. Endocrine disorders (Diabetes, Cushing, Disorders of bone metabolism, Thyroid disorders and other hormonal disorders)
    19. Chronic bladder and urinary disorders (urinary retention, erectile dysfunction)
    20. Contraceptives
    21. Immune system and malignant disease
    22. Antenatal care
    23. Chronic Eye conditions
    24. Chronic vestibular disorders
    25. Peripheral vascular diseases
    26. Chronic/End-stage kidney diseases

* Medications when discharged of hospital

## Non-exemption criteria

New prescription due to loss of medicine

## Accident & Emergency

The medications for 24 hours will be provided free of charge by the nurses on duty.

Prescription fee will apply on the medications as of the following day.
