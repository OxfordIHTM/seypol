## Results Based Management Framework

The Cabinet has approved for the whole government roll-out of Results Based Management (RBM) since most preparatory work beginning immediately and ready for the 2023 budget process including:

* Adoption of the proposal for the portfolio based sector planning approach which provides a framework for the delivery of outcomes and the remaining 3 pillars as well as the formulation of National Development Strategies, which will reflect priorities at the highest level of government.
* Adoption and use of the PM&E tool for monitoring PM&E across government. This scalable strengthening tool is designed to improve the quality of performance information for monitoring and reporting on performance.
* The roll-out of the PM&E pillar as a whole of government approach to policy coordination and (i) delivering results across government.

To further ensure the successful implementation of RBM and Integration of the four pillars, the whole process will be steered at national level by the High Level RBM Steering Committee which will be the overarching one to ensure successful implementation of the RBM policy framework across government.
The between RBM Committee (RBM) shall be established as a focal point in
each portfolio to implement the RBM policy framework for a more integrated
approach to public service management and accountability.

MDAs are requested to provide details of their internal RBM committee by
November 2021 to be submitted to the Executive Secretary of the RBMA-SC Ms.
Margaret Mournee on mrellay@datastore.gov.sc. Further details on the role of
the RBM can be found in the RBM Manual.

The RBM committee will be conducting individual briefing sessions with all portfolios and
catals of which will be provided shortly.

The Guy More Institute in partnership with the RBM committee will be
undertaking the necessary training for the MDAs, to provide the require skills
necessary to implement the rollout of the RBM.

Attached are the relevant RBM documents which will also be uploaded on the
website of the Ministry of Finance, Economic Planning & Trade and the
Department of Public Administration.
