# Children Act
**Chapter 28**
*Commenced on 15 July 1982*

## Part I – Preliminary

### 1. Short title
(1) This Act may be cited as the Children Act.

### 2. Interpretation
In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—
* **"affiliation order"** means an order under section 12;
* **"adoption order"** except in sections 65 to 69, means an order in terms of section 33;
* **"adult"** means a person of 18 years of age or older;
* **"child"**, except where used to express a relationship and except in sections 9 to 14, means a person under 18 years of age and includes a young person;
* **"guardian"** means a person appointed as guardian of a child by a parent under Article 397 of the Civil Code, by the Court under Article 402, or as sub-guardian under Article 420 or 422 of the Civil Code.

### 2A. Courts, tribunals to be guided by certain principles
(1) Whenever a court or tribunal determines any question with respect to the upbringing of a child, the child’s wellbeing shall be its primary consideration.

### 3. Duty to promote welfare of child and delegation of powers of the Director
(1) In reaching any decision relating to a child, the Director shall have regard to all the circumstances, first consideration being given to the need to safeguard and promote the welfare of the child.

### 3A. National Commission for Child Protection
(1) There shall be established a National Commission for Child Protection consisting of members appointed by the Minister.

---

## Part II – Maintenance etc.

### 4. Obligation to maintain
A person under an obligation to maintain a child must ensure that the child is:
* Given adequate nutritious food;
* Adequately clothed;
* Provided with housing protecting him against bad weather;
* Protected against illness.

### 6. Enforcement of maintenance
(3) A parent who fails to maintain his child in terms of section 4 is guilty of an offence and is liable to imprisonment for two years and to a fine of R10,000.

---

## Part V – Adoption

### 33. Adoption orders
(1) An adoption order is an order vesting the parental rights and duties relating to a child in the adopters.

---

## Part VII – Compulsory care

### 77. Family Tribunal
(1) There shall be a Family Tribunal consisting of a Chairperson, two Vice-Chairpersons and five other members, as the President may appoint.

---

## Schedules

### Schedule 2 (Section 79(1)(d))
**Specified offences against children**
1. Any offence under Chapter XV (Offences against Morality) of the Penal Code.
2. Any offence under Part VI (Offences against Children) of this Act.
3. Any other offence involving bodily injury to a child.
