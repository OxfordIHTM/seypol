## Definition/Glossary

In this policy terms and expressions defined in this section shall bear the same meaning unless the context otherwise requires¹:

## 1. BACKGROUND

### 1.1. Introduction
This is the first National Gender Policy of the Government of Seychelles. It signals Government’s continued commitment to the attainment of Constitutional rights including gender equality as a corner stone for ending poverty, achieving long-term sustainable social change and meeting the aspirations of the Seychellois especially women and girls who were previously disadvantaged.

The Policy provides a guide to the design and implementation of gender programmes across all sectors within the framework of MDG three (gender equality and equity) as well as the 28 targets of the SADC Protocol on Gender and Development to be achieved by 2015...

### 1.2. Policy and legal framework
The Seychelles Constitution of the Third Republic (1993) is the country’s supreme law... Article 5 states that “any other law found to be inconsistent with this Constitution is, to the extent of the inconsistency, void.”

**Key Highlights:**
* **Preamble:** Proclaims inherent dignity and equal rights free from discrimination.
* **Charter of Fundamental Human Rights and Freedoms:** Guarantees 25 rights equally applicable to both men and women.
* **Article 30:** Recognises the unique status and natural maternal functions of women in society.

The policy is aligned with the SADC Protocol on Gender and Development, CEDAW, the Beijing Declaration, and the MDGs.

---

## 2. JUSTIFICATION

Seychelles is a leader in the African region in socio-economic development. There are now slightly more women (50.07%) compared to men (49.3%) in Seychelles.

**Existing Challenges:**
* Gender Based Violence (GBV).
* Higher levels of poverty among single-parent households (predominantly female).
* Increasing incidence of sex work and gender dimensions of HIV and AIDS.
* Perception that boys and men are being marginalised in service provision.

## 3. POLICY FRAMEWORK – VISION, GOAL, OBJECTIVES AND GUIDING PRINCIPLES

### 3.1. Vision
A nation of women, men, girls and boys who are equal contributors to and beneficiaries of national development able to achieve their full potential in both the public and private spheres; and have the freedom to make responsible life choices.

**Mission:** To achieve gender equality and equity as enshrined in the Constitution, regional and international gender-related instruments that Seychelles is party to.

### 3.2. Policy Goal
To provide national guidelines for institutionalising and operationalising gender as an integral component of social, economic and political development.

### 3.3. Specific Policy Objectives
* **4.4.1** Harmonise legislative and administrative frameworks of international and (sub)regional gender-related instruments.
* **4.4.2** Promote equal representation and participation of women and men at all levels of decision-making.
* **4.4.3** Ensure the mainstreaming of gender perspectives into all policies, structures, systems, programmes and activities.
* **4.4.4** Provide an overarching framework to guide State and Non State Actors (NSAs).

### 3.4. Guiding Principles
* **Rights based approach:** Women and children’s rights are human rights.
* **Harmonisation:** Aligning national legislation with regional and international instruments.
* **Empowerment of women:** Ensuring skills, confidence and agency.
* **Male involvement:** Men and boys have a key role in achieving gender equality.
* **Gender as a cross cutting issue:** Recognition that every policy affects genders differently.
* **Multi-sector approach:** Coordination between state and NSAs.
* **Poverty eradication:** Gender equality is key to eradicating poverty.
* **Household and family relations:** Equitable decision-making within families.
* **Identifying emerging issues:** Ability to respond to an ever-changing environment.
* **Sex-disaggregated data:** Commitment to gathering disaggregated information for monitoring.
* **Strategic and practical gender needs:** Responding to both immediate and long-term needs.
* **Resource allocation:**Budgetary and human resources responsibility across all sectors.

## 4. POLICY PROGRAMME AREAS, OBJECTIVES AND STRATEGIC ACTIONS

### 4.1. Gender, Constitutional and legal rights
**Commitment:** The Government shall create an enabling environment that guarantees human rights, Constitutional and legal protection to every person.

**Objective:** To facilitate Constitutional and legal protection of women and men equally on all levels.

**Strategic Actions:**
* Advocate for Constitution amendments to explicitly include non-discrimination on the basis of sex.
* Review and repeal discriminatory laws; enact specific criminal law for domestic violence.
* Build justice system capacity to protect vulnerable witnesses.
* Continuous gender sensitisation for the judiciary, police, and politicians.
* Enact a law providing for a quota system in decision-making positions.

### 4.2. Governance
**Commitment:** The Government shall promote good governance through gender-balanced leadership.

**Objective:** Achieve gender balanced representation and participation through capacity development and promoting male participation in civil society.

**Strategic Actions:**
* Implement temporary special measures to reach 50% women in senior decision-making positions.
* Leadership training and mentoring for women in parliament and judiciary.
* Promote women leadership to address corruption and improve accountability.
* Build capacity of men and boys to participate in the gender movement.

### 4.3. Education and training
**Commitment:** Mainstream gender in all education and training to ensure equal representation and performance, with special emphasis on boys and young men.

**Objective:** Increase access to quality education and training free of gender stereotypes.

**Strategic Actions:**
* Adopt policies addressing retention in all education levels and teenage pregnancy.
* Establish mechanisms to improve boys' academic performance.
* Mainstream generic gender modules in all educational levels.
* Encourage women and girls to study STEM subjects.

### 4.4. Productive resources and employment, economic empowerment
**Commitment:** Ensure economic policies are gender responsive, address poverty, and increase decent work equally.

**Objective:** Ensure full and equal access to and control over benefits of socio-economic growth.

**Strategic Actions:**
* Build NBS capacity to conduct time-use studies regarding unpaid care work.
* Implement Gender Responsive Budgeting (GRB).
* Identify and address barriers for women in business; provide economic literacy training.
* Promote responsible fatherhood and positive masculinity.
* Review employment laws for the safety of staff working unsociable hours.

### 4.5. Climate change, sustainable development and disaster risk management
**Commitment:** Recognise the gender perspective in all sustainable development and climate change policies.

**Objective:** Ensure equal participation and recognise gender impacts in addressing climate change and disaster risk.

**Strategic Actions:**
* Analyse gender-specific impacts of floods, droughts, and diseases.
* Use women’s unique knowledge in mitigation and adaptation strategies.
* Mainstream gender in risk and disaster management documents and projects.

### 4.6. Food and nutrition security
**Commitment:** Provide appropriate support to ensure food and nutrition security for all.

**Objective:** Ensure access to sufficient, safe, and nutritious food for all.

**Strategic Actions:**
* Promote inclusion of women/youth in food production and men/youth in food preparation.
* Review National Food Security Strategy to integrate a gender perspective.

### 4.7. Gender based violence
**Commitment:** Promote an environment of zero tolerance to all forms of GBV.

**Objective:** Prevent and eliminate GBV in public and private spheres and provide integrated services.

**Strategic Actions:**
* Launch "UNiTE for Peace to End Gender Based Violence" awareness.
* Enact specific laws on domestic violence, sexual offences, and human trafficking.
* Provide rehabilitation for perpetrators and psycho-social support for survivors.
* Ensure safety and security in public spaces.

### 4.8. Health
**Commitment:** Ensure all Seychellois have the chance to enjoy safe sexual relationships and healthy children.

**Objective:** Ensure access to sexual and reproductive health (SRH) information and encourage male involvement.

**Strategic Actions:**
* Accelerate prevention of HIV, STIs, and blood-borne viruses.
* Improve access to family planning, infertility services, and reproductive cancer screenings.
* Targeted support for vulnerable groups (sex workers, prisoners, etc.).

### 4.9. HIV and AIDS
**Commitment:** Ensure equal access to the HIV continuum of prevention, care, and treatment.

**Objective:** Create an enabling environment to prevent and support HIV interventions for both men and women.

**Strategic Actions:**
* Ensure HIV testing and counselling for all pregnant women.
* Implement harm reduction measures for injecting drug users and sex workers.
* Assess specific risks of boys and men to HIV infection.
* Encourage greater participation of men in caregiving.

### 4.10. Peace building
**Commitment:** Ensure Seychelles continues enjoying peace and stability.

**Objective:** Foster women's representation in national affairs, including defence and police.

**Strategic Actions:**
* Increase women in leadership in security structures (target 50% by 2015).
* Encourage women to choose career paths in police and defence forces.
* Mainstream gender in security training curricula.

### 4.11. Media, Information and Communications
**Commitment:** Promote gender equality and equity in and through the media.

**Objective:** Mainstream gender in media policies and challenge stereotypes.

**Strategic Actions:**
* Promote media house ownership by women.
* Support gender training for journalists and media practitioners.
* Increase women’s access to ICT resources.

## 5. INSTITUTIONAL FRAMEWORK FOR IMPLEMENTATION

### 5.1. Commitment to enable environment
* Sustain Cabinet commitment.
* Strengthen the Gender Secretariat (human and financial resources).
* Establish permanent gender mechanisms in all line-ministries.

### 5.2. Institutional structures
**Gender Management System (GMS):**
* **Gender Focal Points:** Propose mechanisms and monitor sector implementation.
* **Gender Secretariat:** National coordination, monitoring, and evaluation.
* **National Gender Management Team (NGMT):** Sector-level coordination and awareness.
* **GBV Working Group:** Coordination of GBV activities.
* **Gender Commission:** Popularisation within civil society.
* **Women in Politics Association:** Linkages with the national assembly.

### 5.3. Support gender mainstreaming mechanisms
* Support sector-level gender analysis.
* Promote Gender Responsive Budgeting.
* Strengthen the Management Information System (Gender Seychelles website).

### 5.4. Monitoring and evaluation
* Develop a Monitoring and Evaluation framework with time-bound plans.
* Strengthen national capacity for reliable gender-disaggregated statistics.

### 5.5. Gender responsive budget
* Capacity building for the Ministry of Finance on GRB.
* Lobby for appropriate financial and human resources for the National Gender Policy.

### 5.6. National Gender Policy Implementation Plan
Develop a costed national gender plan of action to operationalise the Policy, identifying priority areas and timeframes.
