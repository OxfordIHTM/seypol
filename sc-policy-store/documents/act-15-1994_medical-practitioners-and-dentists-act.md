## PART I - PRELIMINARY

1. This Act may be cited as the **Medical Practitioners and Dentists Act**.

2. In this Act -

- **"Chairman"** means the Chairman of the Council referred to in the Schedule;
- **"Council"** means the Council established under section 3;
- **"member"** means a member of the Council;
- **"Register"** means the Register of medical practitioners and dentists or any part thereof referred to in section 5;
- **"registered"** means registered under this Act;
- **"Registrar"** means the person appointed as such under paragraph 3 of the Schedule.

## PART II - MEDICAL AND DENTAL COUNCIL

3. (1) There is established as a body corporate the **Seychelles Medical and Dental Council**.
(2) The Schedule shall have effect with respect to the Council.

4. (1) The functions of the Council are -
    - (a) to monitor the competence and regulate the performance of persons exercising the profession of medical practitioners and dentists in Seychelles for the purpose of promoting and upholding the highest possible standard of medical and dental practices in Seychelles;
    - (b) to cause to be kept, subject to this Act, a register of medical practitioners and dentists practising in Seychelles;
    - (c) to investigate allegations of serious professional misconduct and malpractice and take appropriate action in respect thereof.

(2) The Council or any member of the Council shall not be liable for any act or default done or made by the Council or any member of the Council in good faith in the performance of its functions under this Act.

## PART III - REGISTER

5. (1) The Council shall cause to be kept by the Registrar a register of medical practitioners and dentists practising in Seychelles.

(2) The Register shall contain in respect of each medical practitioner or dentist, the following -
    - (a) the full name and residential address;
    - (b) the address where the medical practitioner or dentist practises;
    - (c) the particulars of qualifications, training and specialization, if any;
    - (d) the period of registration;
    - (e) any remark and endorsement;
    - (f) any other matter which the Council may from time to time decide to include in the Register.

(3) The Register shall be kept in the custody of the Registrar and shall, on application by a member of the Council, a registered medical practitioner or dentist... be open to inspection free of charge during normal working hours.

(4) Subject to subsection (5), the Council shall cause a list of medical practitioners and dentists whose names remain on the Register on the 31st December in each year to be published in the Gazette...

(5) A medical practitioner or dentist who has been suspended from practising as such... shall not... be included in the list.

(6) In any proceedings under this Act... a copy of the Gazette containing the list... shall be evidence that -
    - (a) a person included in the list was registered as a medical practitioner or dentist;
    - (b) a person not included in the list was not registered.

(7) In any proceedings... a certificate purporting to be signed by the Registrar... shall... be prima facie evidence of the facts stated.

6. (1) The Registrar may amend the Register -
    - (a) as to addresses or other relevant particulars... for preserving the accuracy of the Register;
    - (b) by inserting or removing therefrom, upon the direction of the Council or the Court, the name of or any relevant particular;
    - (c) for the purpose of complying with this Act.

(2) Without prejudice to its power under section 10, the Council may direct that there be removed from the Register the name of a person who -
    - (a) requests the removal in writing;
    - (b) dies;
    - (c) is continuously absent from Seychelles for a period of more than six months and has not given notice of intention to return.

7. Subject to this Act, a person is qualified to be registered upon the Council being satisfied that the person -
    - (a) is of good character;
    - (b) in the case of an application, other than a renewal -
        - (i) is registered under the law of any foreign country... and the standard would not undermine the function of the Council;
        - (ii) has a degree in medicine or dentistry... and completed a period of houseman ship of at least one year at the Victoria Hospital;
        - (iii) has not been the subject of disciplinary proceedings... so as to undermine the function of the Council;
    - (c) in the case of a renewal, was registered under this Act immediately before the application;
    - (d) in the case of a first registration... was registered under the repealed Medical Practitioners and Dentists Act.

8. (1) An application for registration... shall be made in the prescribed form to the Council.
(2) Where the Council is satisfied... it shall, upon payment of the prescribed fee, cause the person to be registered -
    - (a) for a period of not more than six months; or
    - (b) for a period of 24 months.
(3) If not satisfied, the Council shall refuse registration and notify the applicant in writing.
(4) The Council shall not consider a second application if made within 6 months from the date of refusal.

8A. (1) Notwithstanding section 7(b)(ii), the Council may cause a person serving a period of housemanship to be registered for that purpose.
(2) Sections 8 and 9 shall mutatis mutandis apply.

9. (1) Upon registration, the Registrar shall issue a certificate of registration.
(2) For lost or destroyed certificates, a duplicate shall be issued upon payment of the prescribed fee.
(3) A person whose name has been removed from the Register... shall return the certificate to the Registrar.

10. (1) Where the Council is satisfied that a registered practitioner/dentist has been convicted of an offence, guilty of professional misconduct, obtained registration by fraud, or provided false particulars, the Council may:
    - A. Order removal or correction of particulars in the Register;
    - B. Order suspension for an initial period not exceeding 12 months (aggregate total not exceeding 12 months);
    - C. Caution or reprimand the person;
    - D. Require payment of a penalty of not more than R1000;
    - E. Take other prescribed disciplinary measures.

(2) A person whose name is removed may apply for registration under section 8.

11. (1) A person may, within 14 days after notice of a decision under section 8 or 10(1), appeal to the Supreme Court.
(2) The Chief Justice may make rules for such appeals.

## PART IV - FUNDS AND PROPERTY

12. (1) The Funds and property of the Council shall consist of -
    - (a) money appropriated by an Appropriation Act;
    - (b) prescribed fees or penalties under section 10;
    - (c) grants, donations, or loans;
    - (d) money raised by the Council;
    - (e) other money/property lawfully received.

(2) The Council shall keep proper accounts and prepare an annual statement of accounts.
(3) Accounts shall be audited by an auditor appointed by the Council.
(4) Audited statements shall be sent to the Minister.
(5) The financial year ends on 31st December.

## PART V – MISCELLANEOUS

13. A person who -
    - (a) purports to act as a registered practitioner/dentist without being one;
    - (b) uses professional qualifications/titles not entered in the Register;
    - (c) knowingly makes false statements to obtain registration;
    - (d) uses a certificate with intent to deceive;
    - (e) fails to comply with section 9(3);
    - (f) fails to comply with paragraph 4(7) of the Schedule;
    - (g) fails to comply with regulations,

is guilty of an offence and liable to a fine of **R25,000** and to imprisonment for **3 years**.

14. The Minister may, after consultation with the Council, make regulations regarding prescribed forms, fees, disciplinary inquiry procedures, and amendments to the Schedule.

15. (1) The Medical Practitioners and Dentists Act is hereby repealed.
(2) Persons registered under the repealed Act are deemed registered under this Act for 6 months or until they register under this Act.

# SCHEDULE (Section 3(2))

1. (1) The Council shall consist of a minimum of 11 members:
    - (a) The Director of Health Services;
    - (b) 3 persons (non-practitioners) nominated by the Minister;
    - (c) A registered dentist nominated by the Minister;
    - (d) A registered medical practitioner nominated by the Minister;
    - (e) A representative of each association with at least 20% membership;
    - (f) 3 registered medical practitioners (at least one in private practice) nominated by practitioners;
    - (g) 2 registered dentists (one in private practice) nominated by dentists.

(2) Notice of nomination meetings shall be given in a local newspaper 14 days prior.
(3) Members are appointed by the Minister.
(4) Term of office is 2 years, eligible for reappointment.
(5) Membership ceases if the person ceases to be a registered practitioner/dentist.
(6) Members may resign via letter to the Minister.
(7) Replacements serve the remaining period of the person replaced.

2. (1) The Council shall elect a Chairman.
(2) The Chairman’s term is 2 years.
(3) The Chairman ceases to hold office upon resignation or leaving the Council.

3. (1) There shall be a Registrar appointed by the Council.
(2) The Registrar acts as secretary, keeps the Register and records, and implements Council decisions.

4. (1) The Council shall meet at least twice a year, or as the Chairman directs, or upon request by 5 members.
(2) Time and place are determined by the Chairman.
(3) Five members constitute a quorum.
(4) Meetings are presided over by the Chairman or an elected member.
(5) Decisions are made by a simple majority.
(6) Each member has one vote; the presiding member has a casting vote.
(7) Members with direct interest in a matter must notify the Chairman and shall not vote/be present unless authorized.

5. Decisions via circulation of papers are valid if approved unanimously in writing.

6. Vacating members continue in office for 3 months or until new appointments are made if the entire Council vacates simultaneously.

# SUBSIDIARY LEGISLATION

## Medical Practitioners and Dentists (Disciplinary Inquiries) Regulations [4th September 1995]

1. Cited as the **Medical Practitioners and Dentists (Disciplinary Inquiries) Regulations**.
2. Definitions: "Code of Practice" and "Registrar".
3. Serious professional misconduct/malpractice includes: contravening the Code of Practice, lack of knowledge/skill/judgment, or serious disregard for patient welfare.
4. Complaints must be in writing, signed, and forwarded to the Registrar. The Registrar acknowledges receipt and notifies the respondent.
5. The Council refers meritorious complaints to an investigating committee.
6. The committee consists of 3 members appointed by the Chairman.
7. (1) The committee fixes a date for investigation (min 14 days notice).
(2) Respondent may be represented by a friend or legal practitioner.
(3) Proceedings can continue in the absence of the respondent.
(4) Evidence and documents may be required.
(5) A report is forwarded to the Council.
8. (1) The Council may impose disciplinary measures (A-E of section 10(1)) or dismiss the complaint.
(2) Respondents are informed of the right of appeal to the Supreme Court.
(3) Criminal matters may be referred to the Commissioner of Police.
