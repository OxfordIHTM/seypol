## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
The Republic of Seychelles and the World Health Organization (WHO) have collaborated to develop the **fourth Country Cooperation Strategy (CCS-4)** for the period of 2024 to 2030. The CCS-4 outlines the strategic directions and priorities for WHO's partnership with the Government and other stakeholders in the health sector over the next seven years.

### Alignment
*   **Seychelles Vision 2033**
*   **National Development Strategy 2024-2028**
*   **National Health Policy 2015**
*   **Seychelles National Health Strategic Plan (NHSP) 2022-2026**
*   **UN 2030 Sustainable Development Agenda**
*   **Seychelles UN Sustainable Development Cooperation Framework (UNSDCF) 2024-2028**
*   **Africa Health Strategy (2016-2030)**
*   **WHO GPW13 and GPW14**

### Strategic Priorities
1.  **Enhance healthy living** and accelerate actions to address the risk factors and determinants of health.
2.  **Strengthen universal health coverage (UHC)** and service quality and address priority communicable and noncommunicable diseases.
3.  **Safeguard the health and well-being** of the population against public health emergencies.
4.  **Elevate WHO health leadership**, advocacy, and innovations for health.

## INTRODUCTION

### COUNTRY CONTEXT
Seychelles maintains a clear national vision and strategic plan for health development, in alignment with Article 29 of its 1993 Constitution.

### STRATEGIC FRAMEWORK
The CCS-4 serves as the guiding framework for cooperation between Seychelles and WHO over the next seven years.

---

## HEALTH AND DEVELOPMENT CONTEXT

### POLITICAL SITUATION
The Government of Seychelles has two other branches: the National Assembly and the Judiciary.

### DEMOGRAPHIC CONTEXT
There is a gradual shift towards an ageing population which will increase demand for health and social care.

### SOCIAL SITUATION
Combination of widespread substance abuse, harmful use of alcohol, and poverty is fertile ground for social ills, including gender-based violence (GBV).

### ECONOMIC CONTEXT
GDP contracted from Int$ 3.339 billion in 2019 to Int$ 3.096 billion in 2020.

### CLIMATE AND ENVIRONMENT
Seychelles is vulnerable to ocean acidification, sea level rise, and storm surges. The UNFCC Updated NDC 2021 notes impacts on human health due to increased rainfall.

### HEALTH SYSTEM IN SEYCHELLES
**Vision:** The attainment, by all people in Seychelles, of the highest level of physical, social, mental and spiritual health and living in harmony with nature.

#### Health Financing
Health expenditure per capita was US$727. 
*   Domestic general government: 84.5%
*   Household out-of-pocket (OOPs): 14.2%
*   Private firms: 1.1%
*   External health expenditure: 0.2%
