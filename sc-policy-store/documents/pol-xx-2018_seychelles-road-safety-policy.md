# 1. INTRODUCTION

## 1.1 International Perspective

In March 2010, the United Nations General Assembly resolution 64/2551 proclaimed 2011-2020 the **Decade of Action for road safety** with the goal to stabilise and reduce the forecast level of road fatalities around the world.

### The 5 Major Pillars of the Global Plan:
1. **Road safety management**: Strengthening institutional capacity and multi-sectorial partnerships.
2. **Safer roads and mobility**: Improving safety of road networks, planning, and design.
3. **Safer vehicles**: Improving vehicle safety standards and technologies.
4. **Safer road users**: Improving behaviour through awareness and laws (seat belts, helmets, drink-driving).
5. **Post care response**: Improving emergency treatment and rehabilitation.

#### Key Focus Areas for Road Users:
* **Reducing Drink-Driving**: Enforcement of BAC limits. In Seychelles, the law states 0.035 g/100ml or 35 mg/100ml.
* **Reducing Speed**: Every 1km/h reduction in average speed results in a 2% reduction in crashes.
* **Motorcycle Helmet Use**: Can reduce risk of death by almost 40%.
* **Seat-Belt Use**: Reduces fatality risk among front-seat passengers by 45-50%.
* **Child Restraint Use**: Can reduce fatalities by approximately 90% among infants.

### Key Institutional Roles
* **Department of Land Transport**: Policy development and planning assessment.
* **Seychelles Land Transport Agency (SLTA)**: Implementation, design, and construction of road networks.
* **Road Transport Commission (RTC)**: Education, parking regulation, and vehicle testing (VTS).
* **Seychelles Police**: Law enforcement and driver testing.
* **Seychelles Licensing Authority (SLA)**: Issuing driving and vehicle licenses.
* **Ministry of Health**: Post-crash response and rehabilitation.
* **Seychelles Fire and Rescue Services Agency**: Emergency response and hazardous spills.

## 1.3 Road Safety Policy Formulation Process

The process involved stakeholder workshops (September & November 2015), forums with driving instructors, and consultations with PSE teachers, school wardens, and the general public throughout 2016.

# 2. SITUATIONAL ANALYSIS

## 2.1 Current Situation
* **Engineering**: Measures like white lining and crash barriers exist, but monitoring needs reassessment.
* **Education**: Limited to TV spots; school curriculum (PSE) exists but is insufficient.
* **Enforcement**: Low seat belt usage (40% front, 2% rear) and low speed enforcement.
* **Health Care**: Significant improvements in post-crash response and trauma units.

## 2.2 Way Forward
* Honor the Decade of Action for Road Safety commitment.
* Revive the Road Safety Advisory Committee (RSAC).
* Retrain and empower enforcement officers.
* Implement a computerized system for recording and analysing road traffic accidents.

# 3. INSTITUTIONAL FRAMEWORK

## 3.1 The Secretariat
The **Department of Land Transport (DOLT)** serves as the secretariat to the RSAC.

## 3.2 The Road Safety Advisory Committee (RSAC)
Aim: Reduce deaths and injuries by more than 50%.

**Members include:** DOLT, RTC, SLTA, SPTC, Police, Ministry of Health, Ministry of Education, Media Commission, APDAR, SLA, NGOs, and various industry associations.

# 4. POLICY STATEMENT AND STRATEGIES

## 4.1 SAFER ROADS

### 4.1.1 Land Use Planning
* **Policy Statement**: The Government shall include land transportation infrastructure in all land use planning.
* **Strategies**: Develop legal frameworks for land acquisition and strengthen road widening mechanisms.

### 4.1.2 Road Safety Design and Safety Engineering
* **Policy Statement**: Promote road safety engineering as a compulsory mechanism for design to reduce crashes.
* **Strategies**: Monitor high-crash locations, upgrade old colonial roads, and ensure safety audits on new constructions.

### 4.1.3 Parking Facilities
* **Policy Statement**: Continue land use management for parking via Town and Country Planning Act.
* **Strategies**: Encourage park-and-ride services and improve loading/unloading zones in Victoria.

## 4.2 SAFER VEHICLES

### 4.2.1 Vehicle Safety, Inspection and Roadworthiness
* **Policy Statement**: Ensure vehicle designs and specifications deter the use of unsafe vehicles.
* **Strategies**: Adopt international standards (EU, SABS), modernize registration computerization, and introduce reflective number plates.

### 4.2.2 Vehicle Weight Control
* **Policy Statement**: Control importation of oversized vehicles to prevent road deterioration.
* **Strategies**: Use portable weighbridges and introduce tonnage taxes.

## 4.3 SAFER DRIVERS & PASSENGERS

### 4.3.1 Vehicle Licensing and Registration
* **Policy Statement**: Strengthen driver licensing and registration procedures.
* **Strategies**: Introduce a Demerit Point System and stricter testing standards.

### 4.3.2 Training of Driving Instructors
* **Policy Statement**: Ensure instructors are trained by DOLT and re-trained every two years.

### 4.3.3 Driver Training and Testing
* **Policy Statement**: Establish guidelines to facilitate the training of new drivers.
* **Strategies**: Raise minimum motorcycle/vehicle license age to 18; maintain 80% theory pass rate.

### 4.3.4 Driving Unit of Excellence
* **Policy Statement**: Establish a Driving Unit of Excellence under the RTC to manage examinations and training.

### 4.3.5 Child Passengers
* **Policy Statement**: Develop legislation for child restraints.
* **Strategies**: Advocate for car seats and sensitize parents on risks.

### 4.3.6 Passenger Carrying Services
* **Policy Statement**: Intensify awareness about passenger safety.
* **Strategies**: Ensure buses hold first aid kits and drivers wear seat belts.

### 4.3.7 Safety for Vulnerable Road Users
* **Policy Statement**: Ensure road facilities consider needs of physically challenged persons.

## 4.4 EMERGENCY RESPONSE AND VICTIM SUPPORT
* **Policy Statement**: Provide road victims with prompt and holistic care.
* **Strategies**: Develop national trauma plans and implement Emergency Vehicle Operator Courses (EVOC) for ambulance drivers.

## 4.5 ROAD TRAFFIC SAFETY EDUCATION AND TRAINING
* **Public Awareness**: Organise innovative media campaigns and retrain traffic offenders.
* **Schools**: Integrate road safety into the compulsory school curriculum.
* **Partners**: Create mechanisms for private sector and NGO participation in campaigns.

## 4.6 TRAFFIC ENFORCEMENT
* **Policy Statement**: Strengthen Traffic Police and Wardens.
* **Strategies**: Increase enforcement of speeding and belts; review fines to be a real deterrent.

## 4.7 ROAD SAFETY DATABASE
* **Policy Statement**: Enhance data coordination and create a database for road casualties.
* **Strategies**: Introduce accident analysis software (IMAAP).

## 4.8 RESEARCH ON ROAD SAFETY
* **Policy Statement**: Enhance research to guide programs and legislation.

## 4.9 ENVIRONMENTAL CONTROL
### 4.9.1 Transportation of Dangerous Goods
* **Policy Statement**: Ensure safe transportation and transit of hazardous materials.
* **Strategies**: Create a National Emergency System for Transport of Hazardous Materials (NESTHM).

### 4.9.2 Vegetation and Cables
* **Policy Statement**: Balance environmental protection with road safety.

## 4.10 LEGAL, INSTITUTIONAL AND FINANCIAL ENVIRONMENT
* **Policy Statement**: Review frameworks in consultation with stakeholders to meet current standards.
