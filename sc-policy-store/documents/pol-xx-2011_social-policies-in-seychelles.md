## Introduction

This working paper was produced as a country case study as part of the UNRISD–Commonwealth Secretariat project on social policy in small states. Seychelles is a critical case study due to its extensive social policy programmes and high rankings in the UNDP Human Development Index (HDI).

## Part 2: Political Economy and Development Policy

### 2.1 Overview
Seychelles is a small state of approximately 81,000 people and 455 sq km. The economy is physically isolated and vulnerable, relying heavily on top-end tourism and canned tuna exports.

## Part 5: Conclusion and Policy Implications

Seychelles has seen resounding success in social indicators but faces severe structural debt and economic vulnerability. Future policy must balance social progress with economic adjustment.

### Table 5.1: Timeline of fiscal policy reform (2008-2009)

| Withholding tax on interest income | 1 Oct 2008 |
