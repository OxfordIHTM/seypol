## TEXT CONTENT

**Assent:** J. A. Michel, President, 25th April, 2014.

**Purpose:** An Act to prevent the entry of animal and plant pests and diseases into, and their establishment and spread in, Seychelles, to regulate and control the movement of animal and plant pests and diseases and of animals and plants within Seychelles, to facilitate international trade and cooperation in respect of animal and plant pests and diseases and for matters connected therewith and incidental thereto.

### PART I - PRELIMINARY

**1. Short title and commencement**
This Act may be cited as the **Animal and Plant Biosecurity Act, 2014** and shall come into operation on such date as the Minister may by notice published in the Gazette.

**2. Interpretation**
(Selection of key definitions):
- **Agency:** The authority designated by the President under section 4.
- **Biosecurity:** A strategic and integrated approach that encompasses the policy and regulatory framework for analysing and managing relevant risks to animal and plant life and the associated risks to the environment, economy and public health.
- **Regulated article:** Includes any animal or animal product, any plant or plant product, any living organism, and other prescribed items.
- **Pest:** Any species, strain or biotype of a plant, animal, microbe or pathogenic agent, alien invasive species or any organism, which causes disease or is detrimental to animals, plants, humans or the environment.

### PART II - AGENCY RESPONSIBLE FOR BIOSECURITY...

**4. Designation and responsibility of agency**
The President may designate a statutory or governmental body to be the agency responsible for biosecurity, tasked with implementation and enforcement of this Act.

**5. Functions of the Agency**
- Regulate entry of regulated pests and diseases.
- Carry out surveillance and assess status of pests/diseases.
- Prevent establishment and spread of regulated pests.
- Facilitate safe importation and exportation of regulated articles.
- Facilitate international cooperation.

### PART III - BIOSECURITY OFFICERS, POWERS AND FUNCTIONS

**30. Powers of biosecurity officers**
Powers are exercised solely for ascertaining the existence of a biosecurity risk and eliminating or reducing it to an acceptable extent.

**31. Power of entry, search and seizure**
Officers may search incoming/outgoing vessels/aircraft and premises (excluding dwelling houses without a warrant) to ascertain the presence of regulated articles.

### PART IV - VESSELS AND AIRCRAFT

**45. Biosecurity arrival declaration**
Masters of vessels or captains of aircraft must declare arrival details, cargo nature, and presence of live animals/plants to the Director.

### PART V - BIOSECURITY BORDER CONTROL

**58. Importation of regulated articles**
The Agency may regulate the importation of particular regulated articles from all or specific countries based on risk analysis.

### PART VI - BIOSECURITY IMPORT PROCEDURES

**68. Application for a biosecurity import permit**
Persons seeking to import regulated articles must apply for a permit, accompanied by the prescribed fee.

### PART VII - BIOSECURITY QUARANTINE

**80. Designation of quarantine stations**
 The Minister may designate land as a quarantine station for animals, animal products, plants or plant products.

### PART VIII - BIOSECURITY INTERNAL CONTROL

**89. Pest and disease surveys**
The Agency may direct surveys to ascertain the status of pests or monitor specific diseases.

### PART IX - BIOSECURITY EMERGENCIES

**108. Declaration of a biosecurity emergency area**
 The Minister may declare a biosecurity emergency area upon being satisfied that an emergency has arisen.

### PART X - BIOSECURITY EXPORT PROCEDURES

**117. Requirement for biosecurity export clearance**
Articles requiring a sanitary or phytosanitary certificate or other biosecurity measures must have export clearance.

### PART XI - OFFENCES AND PENALTIES

**127. Penalties**
Penalties are based on the Schedule. Body corporates may be liable for fines five times the amount for individuals.

### PART XII - MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS

**132. Appeals Board**
An Appeals Board consisting of a Chairperson, a representative of the Attorney General, and a technical expert is established to review decisions.
