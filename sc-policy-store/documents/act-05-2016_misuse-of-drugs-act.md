# MISUSE OF DRUGS ACT, 2016

**Date:** 15th April 2016

An Act to provide for effective measures to prevent abuse and diversion of controlled drugs and precursors, to facilitate the investigation and prosecution of offences involving controlled drugs, in particular drug trafficking, to promote the treatment, education, rehabilitation, recovery, and social reintegration of drug users and drug dependent persons, to ensure the availability of controlled drugs for legitimate medical and scientific use, to facilitate the implementation of Seychelles' commitments under the international drug control conventions and for matters consequential thereon.

## PART I: PRELIMINARY

### 1. Short Title
This Act may be cited as the **Misuse of Drugs Act, 2016**.

### 2. Interpretation

*   **"1961 Convention"** means the Single Convention on Narcotic Drugs of 1961.
*   **"1971 Convention"** means the Convention on Psychotropic Substances of 1971.
*   **"approved facility"** means a place declared under this Act to be an approved facility for the purpose of providing treatment, rehabilitation, or harm reduction services.
*   **"approved institution"** means a place declared under this Act to be an approved institution for the purposes of inpatient treatment and rehabilitation.
*   **"article liable to seizure"** means anything including cash and any materials used in the cultivation or manufacture of controlled drugs.
*   **"cannabis plant"** means a plant of the genus Cannabis.
*   **"controlled drug"** refers to narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances as classified in the First Schedule.
*   **"controlled delivery"** means the practice of allowing suspicious consignments of controlled drugs to pass into or through Seychelles under supervision to identify persons involved in offences.

### 3. Classification of Controlled Drugs
Controlled drugs shall be classified into three classes:

| Class | Description | Examples/Criteria |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Class A** | Subject to special measures of control; high potential for harm. | Schedule IV of 1961 Convention |
| **Class B** | Medical/scientific use but requires control to prevent harm. | Schedule II of 1971 Convention |
| **Class C** | Medical/scientific use; lowest level of control relative to Class A/B. | Schedule III/IV of 1971 Convention |
