**AN ACT** to provide for the establishment of the Disaster Risk Management Division for the effective management of disaster through a comprehensive and integrated all-hazard approach and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto.

**ENACTED** by the President and the National Assembly.

## PART I - PRELIMINARY

**1. Short title and commencement**
This Act may be cited as the **Disaster Risk Management Act, 2014** and shall come into operation on such date as the Minister may, by notice published in the Gazette, appoint.

**2. Interpretation**
In this Act, unless the context otherwise require-

*   **"disaster"** means a serious disruption in the functioning of a community or a society involving widespread human, material, economic or environmental threats, losses and impacts, whether arising from accident, social disruption, nature or human activity, whether developing suddenly or as result of long term process;
*   **"disaster risk"** means the potential disaster losses, in lives, health status, livelihoods, assets and services, which could occur to a particular community or a society over some specified future time period;
*   **"disaster risk reduction"** means the concept and practice of reducing disaster risks through systematic efforts to analyse and manage the causal factors of disasters, including through reduced exposure to hazards, lessened vulnerability of people and property, wise management of land and the environment, and improved preparedness for adverse events;
*   **"disaster risk area"** means an area where a disaster has occurred or an area identified as a disaster risk area in the national disaster risk management plan or by contingency planning processes and related measures;
*   **"disaster area"** means a district, region, island or part thereof declared or designated as a disaster area for intervention and assistance as a result of disaster related losses;
*   **"Division"** means the Disaster Risk Management Division established under section 3;
*   **"Director General"** means the person appointed under section 7;
*   **"early warning system"** means the set of capacities needed to generate and disseminate timely and meaningful warning information to enable individuals, communities and organisations threatened by a hazard to prepare and to act appropriately and with sufficient time to reduce the possibility of harm or loss;
*   **"Fund"** means the National Disaster Risk Management Fund established by section 31;
*   **"government institution"** includes a Ministry, Department of Government, administrator of a district, statutory body, government agency or any other organisation performing a public service;
*   **"Minister"** means the Minister responsible for disaster risk management;
*   **"National Committee"** means the National Disaster Risk Management Committee established by section 9;
*   **"prescribed"** means prescribed by regulations made under this Act.

There is hereby established a division within the Government to be known as the Disaster Risk Management Division.

(1) The Functions of the Division shall be to-
(a) function as the national body for disaster risk management on a national level and in supporting regional and local level disaster risk management initiatives and response;
(b) implement and monitor a comprehensive integrated disaster risk management system at all levels... [truncated for brevity]

**5. Integrated emergency management arrangement**
In implementing the integrated emergency management and coordination system, the Division shall-
(a) set out specific requirements and uniform procedures on matters which can be standardised nationally...

**6. Other committees**
The Division may, in consultation with the Minister, set up other committees as deemed necessary for the purpose of carrying out its functions under this Act.

**7. Director General and other staff of Division**
(1) The President shall, by order published in the Gazette, appoint a suitably qualified and experienced person to be the Director General...

**8. Functions of Director General**
In carrying out the functions under this Act, the Director General shall-
(a) advise the Minister on matters relating to disaster risk management;
(b) review and assess the various programmes and activities of the government...

**9. National Disaster Risk Management Committee**
(1) There shall be established a National Disaster Risk Management Committee comprising of persons with high level capacity to advise the President and the Cabinet...

**10. Composition of National Committee**
(1) The National Committee shall consist of the following members, to be appointed by the President:
(a) the Director General;
(b) a representative of the President's office;
(c) Principal Secretaries of the Ministries or departments responsible for: (i) environment, (ii) land use and housing, (iii) health, (iv) community development and sports, (v) transport, (vi) labour;
(d) one representative each from:
*   the Seychelles Broadcasting Corporation
*   the Meteorological Office
*   the Police Department (not below rank of Superintendent)
*   the Red Cross Society of Seychelles
*   the Seychelles Fire and Rescue Services Agency.

**11. Functions of National Committee**
The functions of the National Committee shall be to-
(a) advise and to make recommendations to the President and the Cabinet in relation to the application of this Act;
(b) ensure that the principles of disaster risk reduction are integrated into the activities of each relevant government institution...

**12. Meetings of National Committee**
(1) The National Committee shall meet not less than four times a year in the first year... and not less than twice a year thereafter.

**13. Establishment of Vulnerability Assessment Committee**

**14. Functions of Vulnerability Committee**

## PART III - DISASTER RISK AND EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT

**18. National disaster risk management strategy**
(1) The Director General shall prepare a National Disaster Risk Management Strategy which shall consist of measures for prevention, mitigation, preparedness, and capacity building.

**19. Director General to prepare other plans**
The Director General shall prepare other plans which shall contain details of arrangements for hazard identification, risk assessment and mitigation.

**20. Other bodies to develop a disaster risk management programme**
The Director General shall give guidance to government institutions, private sector entities, etc., to assess and prevent or reduce the risk of disasters.

**21. Preparation of strategy**
(1) The Director General may order to organise drills and exercises to test their system.

**22. Resources to be used in an emergency**
Every government institution shall ensure that resources available for use in an emergency or disaster is maintained so as to be ready for use.

**23. Emergency management operation centers**
(1) The Director General shall be responsible for the establishment and maintenance of a National Emergency Operations Centre.

**24. Shelter**
(1) The Director General shall establish and maintain a shelter list of premises available and suitable for use as shelter.

**25. Assessment of loss, injury and evacuation**
(1) The Director General shall assess the potential for loss of life and injury to persons where there is a threat of a hazard or where a disaster has occurred.

## PART IV - COMMUNICATIONS LINK

**26. Directory of persons involved in disaster risk management, etc.**
(1) The Director General shall develop and maintain a directory of all government institutions, private sector entities and non-governmental organisations involved in disaster risk management.

**27. Emergency management and response**
(1) The Director General shall collect information on all aspects of disasters and maintain an electronic database.

## PART V - INTERNATIONAL ASSISTANCE

**28. International assistance**
(1) The Director General shall, in consultation with the Minister, make an initial estimate as to whether domestic capacities are sufficient.

**29. Commencement and termination of International assistance**
Assistance shall commence when request for assistance is accepted and terminate after an assessment concludes it is no longer required.

**30. Minister may terminate international assistance**
The Minister may terminate international assistance by issuing a notice in writing to the international organisation.

## PART VI - NATIONAL DISASTER RISK MANAGEMENT FUND

**31. National Disaster Risk Management Fund**
(1) There is hereby established a Fund to be known as the National Disaster Risk Management Fund.

**32. Accounts and audit**
(1) The Division shall maintain proper accounts and other relevant records of the Fund.

## PART VII - OBLIGATIONS OF PERSONS INVOLVED IN DISASTER RISK MANAGEMENT

**33. Volunteers**
(1) The Director General shall maintain a register of volunteers known as the Register of Volunteers.

**34. Employees who assist in emergency situations**
(1) Where the Director General certifies that the services of a person were needed for emergency tasks, the employer shall pay the salary and benefits for that period.

**35. Risk management officers**
(1) The Director General shall appoint officers to be known as risk management officers.

**36. Multi-Hazard Risk Communication Alert and Warning System**
(1) There shall be established a National Multi-Hazard Risk Communication Alert and Warning System for Seychelles.

## PART VIII - REPORTING, DOCUMENTATION AND DATA COLLECTION

**37. Obligation to supply information**
Every government institution shall supply to the Director General requested information in the prescribed manner.

**38. Obligation to make a damage report assessment**
(1) Every government institution shall prepare a report on damage assessment in the aftermath of a disaster.

## PART IX - DECLARATION OF DISASTERS

**39. Declaration of disasters**
(1) The President may declare a state of emergency and specify any area as a "disaster area" or "potential disaster area".

**40. Access to private places**
Where a declaration has been made, the Police may prohibit or restrict public access to any road or pathway leading to the disaster area.

## PART X - MISCELLANEOUS

**41. Protection from liability**
(1) No legal proceedings shall lie against a person in respect of an act done in good faith in the exercise of powers under this Act.

**42. Compensation for loss or injury suffered by public officer**
Public officers suffering physical injury or property loss in the course of duties shall be entitled to compensation from public funds.

**43. Authorised officers**
The President may appoint authorised officers during a state of emergency.

**44. Powers of authorised officers**
An authorised officer may direct any person to supply information, enter premises, or order evacuations.

**45. Power take control of land or property during disaster**
(1) An authorised officer may take control over any land or other property for the purpose of dealing with the situation.

**46. Compensation for taking possession or control of land or property**
Persons whose land or property has been taken, damaged, or destroyed are entitled to payment of compensation.

**47. Indemnity for actions of person acting under this act**
Persons acting in good faith under this Act are not liable for loss or damage caused.

**48. Power to make regulations**
The Minister may make regulations consistent with this Act to give effect to its provisions.
