## FOREWORD
Primary health care is essential health care based on practical, scientifically sound and socially acceptable methods and technology made universally accessible to individuals and families in the community through their full participation and at a cost that the community and country can afford to maintain at every stage of their development in the spirit of self-reliance and self-determination.

Primary health care forms an integral part both of the country's health system, of which it is the central function and focus, and of the overall social and economic development of the community.

It is the first level of contact of individuals, the family and community with the national health system bringing health care as close as possible to where people live and work, and constitutes the first element of a continuing health care process.

This Seychelles Primary Health Care package serves several purposes. It is a guiding document, a reference document and an operational document at the same time.

It sets out the reason for the existence of the primary health care programmes contained herein and the length and breadth of services that the state will offer free at the point of use in its institutions, as provided for in the Constitution.

The implication is that whatever, we have left out of this document (for example optometry services) do not constitute free primary care in the view of the government.

Although this document focuses only on the health aspects of primary health care, we must emphasize that primary health care goes far beyond services and programmes offered by the health sector or within the health sector.

This Primary Health Care Package will evolve with the evolving disease pattern of Seychelles and the Ministry of Health will continually update this document according to the needs of the time.

**Dr Bernard Valentin**

# 1. INTRODUCTION

The right to the protection of health and to the enjoyment of the highest attainable standard of physical and mental health is enshrined in the Constitution adopted in 1993. Article 29 of the Constitution further provides for the provision of free primary health care (PHC) in State institutions for all citizens and promotes individual responsibility in health matters.

The World Health Organization defines primary health care as a whole-of-society approach to health and well-being centred on the needs and preferences of individuals, families and communities. It addresses the broader determinants of health and focuses on the comprehensive and interrelated aspects of physical, mental and social health and wellbeing.

### 1.1 LEADERSHIP AND GOVERNANCE
The MoH has overall responsibility for the management and development of the health sector. The statutory entities under the purview of the MOH have the following mandates:

* **The HCA:** To promote, protect and restore the health of the public.
* **The PHA:** To regulate health and provide protection of the population’s health.
* **The National AIDS Council (NAC):** To provide strategic guidance and coordination of HIV activities on a national level.

### 1.2 PURPOSE OF THE PHC PACKAGE
The main objective of the PHC package of services is to achieve UHC, ensure equity, quality and efficiency in health, and contribute to the health sector goals. It is based on three principles:
1. Meeting people’s health needs through comprehensive care throughout the life course.
2. Systematically addressing the broader determinants of health.
3. Defining a comprehensive PHC health care package to inform resource requirements.

### 1.3 PROCESS OF PACKAGE DEVELOPMENT
The PHC package was developed by a technical working group under the leadership of the Principal Secretary. The process was widely consultative, including heads of health centres and units, programme managers and other frontline staff.

### 1.4 COMMUNITY HEALTH SYSTEM: FACILITIES, PROGRAMME AREAS AND COMPONENTS
The Health Centre is the bedrock of PHC in Seychelles. PHC care services are arranged under six broad categories:
* Family Health
* Disease Prevention and Control
* Oral Health
* Rehabilitation
* Health Promotion
* Integrated Management of Priority Diseases and Other Common Conditions

# 2. FAMILY HEALTH

## 2.1 MATERNAL HEALTH PROGAMME
**Aims:** To improve maternal and child survival through the provision of patient–centred care and evidence-based health services.

### Key Interventions

| Areas | Interventions |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Antenatal Care** | Midwives consultation (10 consultations), BP, weight, mental health assessment. |
| **Laboratory Screening** | Full blood count, Blood group, Glucose challenge test, Urinalysis, VDRL/TPHA, HIV, Hep B/C, HTLV-1. |
| **Health Education** | Healthy lifestyles, diet, danger signs, early breastfeeding. |
| **Ultrasound** | Three routine ultrasound scans for all pregnant women. |
| **Preventive** | Tetanus Immunisation, De-worming, Iron and folic acid supplements. |
| **PMTCT** | HIV testing, ART for positive pregnant women, ARV for infants. |
| **Intrapartum Care** | Safe birth experience, 1:1 midwifery care, CTG use, limited intervention. |
| **Postpartum Care** | Monitor mother/baby, hearing screening, Pulse oximetry, BCG vaccination. |

## 2.2 EXPANDED PROGRAMME OF IMMUNISATION
**Aims:** To prevent mortality and morbidity due to vaccine-preventable diseases.

| Areas | Interventions |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Health Promotion** | IEC on benefits of immunisation. |
| **Disease Prevention** | Vaccines according to National EPI Schedule, school entry checks. |
| **Curative** | Care for adverse events following vaccination (AEFI). |

## 2.3 CHILD HEALTH PROGRAMME
**Aims:** To provide safe, accessible, and high-quality care to all young children in Seychelles to reduce morbidity and mortality.

| Areas | Interventions |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Postnatal Home Visit** | Routine exam of mother/new-born, breastfeeding support. |
| **District Assessment** | Denver Development Screening Test (6 weeks). |
| **Immunisation** | According to National Immunisation Schedule. |
| **Growth Monitoring** | Monthly from 3 months until age one year. |
| **Developmental** | Denver Assessment (6 months, 2, 3, 4 years). |

## 2.4 SCHOOL HEALTH PROGRAMME
**Aims:** To provide comprehensive health promotion and prevention services to schoolchildren.

| Areas | Interventions |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Health Promotion** | Lifestyle education, sexual/reproductive health, counselling. |
| **Prevention** | ECCE Health Profile, UPCCD screening (P4, S1, S4), Medical screening (S5), Vaccines (P1, P6, S3). |
| **Curative** | Referrals to speech pathologists, psychologists, audiologists, etc. |

## 2.5 ADOLESCENT AND YOUTH HEALTH PROGRAMME
**Aims:** To protect and improve the physical and mental health of adolescents (10-19) and youth (20-30).

| Areas | Interventions |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Health Promotion** | Physical activity, nutrition, 24-hour youth Helpline, drug misuse education. |
| **Prevention** | Contraceptives, Family Planning, STI/HIV/Hep C screening, Pap Smears. |
| **Curative** | STI treatment, home-based care for complex needs. |
| **Psychosocial** | Abortion support, school-aged mother support, Psychologist services. |

## 2.6 SEXUAL AND REPRODUCTIVE HEALTH PROGRAMME
**Aims:** To ensure safe and satisfying sexual relationships and healthy pregnancies.

| Areas | Interventions |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Health Promotion** | Talks at facilities, schools, workplaces, mass media. |
| **Family Planning** | Modern and natural methods of contraception. |
| **Prevention** | Pap smear, Breast examination, STI/HIV testing. |
| **Clinical** | Pelvic examinations, referrals for infertility or abortion services. |
| **GBV Care** | First-line support, EC (120h), HIV PEP (72h), mental health support. |

## 2.7 HOME-BASED CARE PROGRAMME
**Aims:** To provide high quality essential health care in the home environment.

| Area | Interventions |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Health Promotion** | Basic health promotion and dietary education. |
| **Prevention** | Physical, speech, and occupational therapy. |
| **Curative** | Individualised Care Plan, wound care, pain management, assistive devices. |
| **Psychosocial** | Pastoral/spiritual care, emotional support, Respite Care. |

## 2.8 HEALTHY AGEING AND HEALTH OF THE ELDERLY
**Aims:** To improve health and wellbeing of the elderly (63+), ensuring functional ability.

| Areas | Interventions |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Health Promotion** | Age-appropriate education, physical activity, nutrition. |
| **Prevention** | Geriatric assessment, vaccinations, home safety modification. |
| **Curative** | Individualised care plans, dementia care, medication review. |

## 2.9 MEN’S HEALTH PROGRAMME
**Aims:** To improve men’s health and well-being and reduce premature mortality.

| Areas | Interventions |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Health Promotion** | Gender transformative health promotion, health literacy on NCDs/SRH. |
| **Prevention** | NCD prevention (diet/exercise), condom promotion, HBV/HBP/Diabetes screenings. |
| **Curative** | Routine health checks, addiction management, adherence strategies. |

## 2.10 PALLIATIVE CARE PROGRAMME
**Aims:** To provide equitable palliative care services in the continuum of care.

| Areas | Interventions |
| :--- | :--- |

# 3. DISEASE PREVENTION AND CONTROL

## 3.1 NUTRITION PROGRAMME
**Aims:** To improve nutritional status throughout the life course.

| Target Group | Interventions |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Women (15-49)** | Preconception nutrition education. |
| **Pregnant Women** | Nutrition sessions at Mont Fleuri, gestational diabetes counselling. |
| **Mothers/Infants** | Breastfeeding support, weaning/complementary feeding. |
| **Children (<5)** | Anthropometric monitoring, meal plans for wasting/stunting. |
| **Schoolchildren** | Weight management clinic, nutrition assessment. |
| **Adults/Elderly** | Weight loss programmes, group sessions in elderly homes. |

## 3.2 DIABETES PREVENTION AND CONTROL PROGRAMME
**Aims:** To reduce incidence and premature mortality from type 2 diabetes.

| Areas | Interventions |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Promotion** | Community awareness and education. |
| **Primary Prevention** | Physical activity, healthy eating, smoking cessation. |
| **Early Detection** | Routine blood sugar screening. |
| **Routine Care** | Pharmacological treatment, HbA1c/Lipid/Renal monitoring. |
| **Complications** | Annual foot and fundal exams, BP checks, influenza vaccine. |

## 3.3 HIV/AIDS PREVENTION AND CONTROL PROGRAMME
**Aims:** Prevention of new infections, improving health of PLWH, and reducing mortality.

| Areas | Intervention |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Promotion** | Safe sex practices, condom distribution, stigma reduction. |
| **Prevention** | PrEP, PEP, HIV testing/counselling, PMTCT, harm reduction for IDUs. |
| **Curative** | ART, treatment of opportunistic infections, psychosocial support. |

## 3.4 CANCER CONTROL PROGRAMME
**Aims:** To reduce incidence and mortality from cancer.

| Areas | Interventions |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Promotion** | Awareness, tobacco cessation, healthy eating. |
| **Prevention** | HPV/HBV vaccinations, avoiding occupational carcinogens. |
| **Diagnosis** | Population screening programmes, symptom assessment. |
| **Curative** | Prompt referral, integrated palliative care. |

## 3.5 PREVENTION AND CONTROL OF CARDIOVASCULAR DISEASES
**Aims:** To reduce incidence of CVD and other NCDs through primary prevention.

| Component | Interventions |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Public Health** | Surveillance, Tobacco Control Act 2009, NCD Strategy 2016-2025. |
| **Promotion** | Smoke-free campaigns, healthy diet, physical activity. |
| **Prevention** | Weight reduction, smoking cessation, reducing alcohol. |
| **Curative** | BP measurement, CVD risk calculation, antihypertensive meds. |

## 3.6 MENTAL HEALTH PROGRAMME
**Aims:** To improve mental health and protect human rights of persons with mental disorders.

| Areas | Interventions |
| :--- | :--- | 
| **Promotion** | Life skills, stress management, suicide prevention. |
| **Integrated Care** | Diagnosis/treatment of psychiatric emergencies, counselling. |
| **Community Care** | Psychosocial rehabilitation, day care, family support. |
| **Special Pop.** | Drug/alcohol detox, memory clinics for elderly, adolescent services. |

## 3.7 PUBLIC HEALTH PROGRAMME IN PHC
**Aims:** To promote health literacy and prevent diseases from environmental risks.

**Core Areas of Intervention:**
* Health promotion
* Personal health and hygiene
* Home and environmental sanitation
* Food and water safety
* Occupational health and safety
* Vector control

# 7. INTEGRATED MANAGEMENT OF PRIORITY DISEASES AND OTHER COMMON CONDITIONS (IMPD&CC)
**Aim:** Delivery of integrated clinical services at the first level of care by a multidisciplinary team.

| Category | Examples |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Chronic Diseases** | Diabetes, CVD, Metabolic disorders, Obesity, Asthma, Cancers. |
| **Common Conditions** | Gastroenteritis, Gynaecologic infections, Sports injuries, Mental Health, STDs. |

| **Tests/Exams** | ECG, Visual acuity, HIV rapid test, Blood sugar, Medical reports. |

# 10. IMPLEMENTATION CONSIDERATIONS
**Goal:** Achieve Universal Health Coverage (SDG 3.8).

**Key Implementation Steps:**
* Define necessary inputs (funding, HR).
* Develop service standards and clinical guidelines.
* Implement the Chronic Care Model (CCM) for chronic disease management.
