## Forward

Statistics are important in helping policy makers allocate scarce resources, monitor national progress and make government more transparent and accountable. The National Bureau Statistics (NBS) is the apex agency responsible for the production of statistics in Seychelles, and provides key statistics on a wide range of economic and social issues.

A wide range of statistics are being produced across government through statistical collections, administrative and transactional data. To get the best value from these assets requires a holistic and coordinated approach to the production of national statistics. The NBS plays an important leadership and coordination role in relation to the statistical activities of other official bodies, both within Seychelles and internationally.

For the NBS to perform better, it is essential that it determines its future hence the “raison d’être” of this Strategic Plan 2014-2020. This Plan will enable NBS to be more efficient and effective in the management of statistical activities so as to better meet the increasing needs of it users.

This Plan has provided a view of where NBS is and where it wants to be by 2020.

Never has so much been expected from statistics; never have statisticians had such means at their disposal; and never has there been so much willingness to learn from each other and share internationally the outcomes of that learning.

In order to keep pace with the rapid transformation of the structure of the socio-economic environment of the Seychelles and to be able to respond to the increasing demand for statistics, in particular for monitoring Government of Seychelles on-going reforms, it is essential to plan for the long-term while at the same time maximizing performance in the short-term. Having a corporate plan enables NBS to ensure that day-to-day decisions align with the long-term goal.

## Introduction

The National Bureau of Statistics (NBS) is Seychelles official national statistical agency. It was formerly known as the National Statistics Bureau (NSB) which was created on 12 February 2005, as a semi-autonomous organisation, following enactment of the National Statistics Bureau Act 2005. The Act was revised in 2010 and the name changed to the National Bureau of Statistics.

### History of Statistics in Seychelles

The compilation of statistics dates back to the early seventies when a small statistics unit operated within the Ministry of Economic Planning. Prior to independence it was called the Economic and Statistics Unit in the Office of the President.

Over the years, it had been subject to a number of changes in terms of name, location and parent Ministry. Over the 25 year period (1980-2005), it changed parent Ministry 5 times until it finally became a semi-autonomous organisation in February 2005.

* **1980:** Known as the “Statistics Division” in the Department of Finance under the President’s Office.
* **January 1986:** Transferred to the President’s Private Office.
* **August 1986:** Renamed the Information Systems Division (ISD), comprising Statistics and Database Administration Section (SDAS) and Systems Development Section (SDS).
* **January 1989:** Renamed Management and Information Systems Division (MISD) under the Ministry of Administration and Manpower.
* **1 April 1998:** Moved to the Ministry of Information Technology and Communications (MITC) until December 2003.
* **January 2004:** Moved to the Ministry of Economic Planning until early 2005.
* **12 February 2005:** NBS created as a semi-autonomous organisation.
* **July 2010:** Name changed to National Bureau of Statistics (NBS).

---

### Role of the NBS

The NBS provides statistics on a wide range of economic, social, population and environmental matters, covering government, business and the community. It also has an important coordination function with respect to the statistical activities of other official bodies.

**Mission:** Continuously build and develop a coherent, reliable, efficient and demand driven “National Statistical System” to encourage and support informed decision and policy making.

**Vision:** Become the centre of excellence in production and dissemination of statistics in Seychelles.

**Objectives:**
1. Strengthen and assist in the development of the National Statistical System (NSS) to meet the challenges of emerging data needs;
2. Improve the quality of our products and services and be the primary source of “official statistics”; and
3. Improve data dissemination.

### Values – ‘QUALITE’

* **Quality:** Focus on high quality products in accordance with international standards. Professionalism and excellence.
* **User-friendly statistics:** Maintaining a responsive national statistical system by cooperating with users and suppliers.
* **Accessibility:** Ensuring statistics are accessible, relevant, and reliable.
* **Learning organisation:** Constantly improving capacity and skills to bring innovation.
* **Integrity:** Promoting principles of objectivity, impartiality, and independence.
* **Trust:** Maintaining trust through consistency, honesty, and reliable application of fundamental principles.
* **Employees:** Valuing human resources and promoting general welfare and well-being.

---

### Legislation

The National Bureau of Statistics Act 2010 provides the CEO with the authority to conduct statistical collections and maintain confidentiality. 

**Responsibilities of the Bureau:**
* (a) Planning, authorising, coordinating and supervising all official statistical programs within the NSS.
* (b) Collecting, compiling, analysing, abstracting, publishing and disseminating statistical information.
* (c) Assisting Government Ministries and Departments in statistical collection.
* (d) Eliminating duplication of collection within the NSS.
* (e) Sharing micro data with authorised parties upon payment of a fee.
* (f) Providing advice and assistance to official bodies.
* (g) Co-ordinating activities between Seychelles and international organisations.

**Functions of the Board:**
* (a) Formulate policy and monitor programs.
* (b) Approve annual budget and action plans.
* (c) Make senior staff appointments.
* (d) Determine structure, staffing levels, and service conditions.
* (e) Establish rules for staff discipline, procurement, and financial management.
* (f) Keep the Minister informed quarterly.
* (g) Perform other functions assigned by the Minister.

### Organizational Structure

NBS is headed by the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) and a Deputy CEO. The Bureau is organized into three broad technical sections:

1. **Economic Statistics:** National Accounts, Prices, Trade, Tourism, Employment and Production.
2. **Census, Surveys and GIS unit:** Data Acquisition & Census, and Demography and Geographical Information System (GIS).
3. **Social Statistics:** Quarterly Labour Force, Crime and Justice, Gender Statistics, Poverty and SDGs.

Supported by the **Corporate Support Section**: Human Resource, Administration & Accounts, and Information Technology.

### What is the National Statistical System (NSS)?

The NSS is a community of government agencies and official bodies involved in the production and use of official statistics, including custodians of administrative datasets. The NBS has legislative responsibility for its coordination.

### Special Data Dissemination Standard (SDDS)

NBS aims to comply with the IMF SDDS to enhance the availability of timely macroeconomic statistics. 

#### SDDS Pillars:
1. **Data coverage, periodicity, and timeliness:** Comprehensive economic and financial data.
2. **Access by the public:** Advance release calendars and simultaneous release.
3. **Integrity:** Objectivity, professionalism, and transparency of procedures.
4. **Quality:** Documentation on methodology and sources.

### Millennium Development Goals (MDGs)

Seychelles committed to the MDGs by 2015 to eradicate poverty and achieve sustainable human development.

**The 8 goals include:**
* Goal 1: Eradicate Extreme Poverty and Hunger
* Goal 2: Achieve Universal Primary Education
* Goal 3: Promote Gender Equality and Empower Women
* Goal 4: Reduce child Mortality
* Goal 5: Improve Maternal Health
* Goal 6: Combat HIV/AIDS, Malaria and other Diseases
* Goal 7: Ensure Environmental Sustainability
* Goal 8: Develop a Global Partnership for Development
