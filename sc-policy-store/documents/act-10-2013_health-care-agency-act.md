# HEALTH CARE AGENCY ACT, 2013
**(Act 10 of 2013)**

**Assent:**
- **1 A. Michel**, President
- **Date:** 14th November, 2013

AN ACT to establish a Health Care Agency and to provide for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto.

ENACTED by the President and the National Assembly.

## PART I - PRELIMINARY

### 1. Short title and commencement
This Act may be cited as the **Health Care Agency Act, 2013** and shall come into operation on such date as the Minister may, by Notice published in the Gazette, appoint.

### 2. Interpretation
In this Act—

- **"Agency"** means the Health Care Agency established under section 3;
- **"Board"** means the Health Care Agency Board established under section 7;
- **"Chief Executive Officer"** means the Chief Executive Officer appointed under section 11;
- **"community health care facility"** means a facility specified in Part B of the First Schedule;
- **"community health care services"** means primary health care services delivered or provided by a:
    - (a) community health care facility;
    - (b) person employed by the Agency, in the course of that person's employment, at a place other than a community health care facility;
- **"Deputy Chief Executive Officer"** means the Deputy Chief Executive Officer appointed under section 12;
- **"Director of Seychelles Hospital"** means the Director of Seychelles Hospital appointed under section 13;
- **"Director of Community Health Services"** means Director of Community Health Services appointed under section 14;
- **"essential medicines"** means medicines that satisfy the priority health care needs of the population as determined by the Ministry;
- **"health care services"** means primary, secondary and tertiary health care services;
- **"Member"** means a member of the Board;
- **"Minister"** means the Minister responsible for Health, and "Ministry" is construed accordingly;
- **"prescribed"** means prescribed by regulations made under section 24;
- **"primary health care"** means the basic level of promotive, preventive, diagnostic, curative or rehabilitative health services provided by a health care professional at the first point of care, and includes:
    - (a) prevention, diagnosis and treatment of common ailments;
    - (b) emergency health care;
    - (c) reproductive and sexual health service including family planning;
    - (d) immunisations, pre-natal care, parent and child health services;
    - (e) individual or group health education and promotion services;
    - (f) laboratory, X-ray and other diagnostic services;
    - (g) nutrition education services;
    - (h) health screening;
    - (i) home health care;
    - (j) dental care;
    - (k) environmental health and other public health services;
    - (l) mental health services;
    - (m) physiotherapy and occupational therapy services;
- **"secondary health care"** means the diagnostic, curative or rehabilitative health care, provided by specialist health care professionals at a health care facility offering secondary health care to which a patient is referred by a primary or secondary health care provider;
- **"Seychelles Hospital"**:
    - (a) means the health care facility specified in Part A of the First Schedule which provides health care services and related services to patients admitted or referred to that facility; and
    - (b) includes the health care facilities known as:
        - (i) Seychelles Hospital situated at Mont Fleuri, Mahe;
        - (ii) North East Point Hospital situated at North East Point, Mahe; and
        - (iii) Wellness Centre situated at Les Cannelles, Mahe;
- **"tertiary health care"** means a specialised, highly technical level of health care for which a patient is referred by a secondary health care provider.

## PART II - HEALTH CARE AGENCY

### 3. Establishment of the Health Care Agency
(1) There is hereby established for the purpose of this Act, an agency to be known as the **Health Care Agency**.
(2) The Agency shall be a body corporate with perpetual succession and a common seal and may sue and be sued in its corporate name.

### 4. Object of the Agency
(1) The principal object of the Agency is to promote, protect and restore the health of the public.
(2) The Agency in carrying out its objectives shall:
- (i) take appropriate measures to prevent, treat and control illness and to prevent disability and death resulting from illness or other causes; and
- (ii) meet the prescribed requirements and standards for the provision and delivery of health care services.

### 5. Functions of the Agency
The functions of the Agency are to:
- (a) plan, deliver and evaluate primary, secondary and tertiary health care services to the public in accordance with Government policies, programmes, and standards;
- (b) provide and deliver health care services within the available budget while meeting priority needs of the population;
- (c) establish procedures to ensure public access to essential medicines and that medicines comply with written law;
- (d) establish and evaluate structures and processes to improve the quality of health care;
- (e) promote research for the improvement of the health care services;
- (f) participate in the development of Government health policies and plans;
- (g) report accurately and timely on the provision and delivery of health care services to the Minister;
- (h) develop annual plans for infrastructure, equipment, human and financial resources;
- (i) ensure the well-being and continuous professional development of health care professionals;
- (j) collaborate with non-government institutions locally and abroad;
- (k) educate patients on their rights and responsibilities;
- (l) facilitate community participation in health planning and evaluation;
- (m) increase the integration, adaptability and responsiveness of the health system;
- (n) perform such other functions as may be prescribed by the Minister.

### 6. Power of Minister to issue policy directives
The Minister may give directions in writing to the Agency on matters of policy and general administration.

## PART III - HEALTH CARE AGENCY BOARD

### 7. Establishment and composition of the Board
(1) There is hereby established a Board known as the **Health Care Agency Board** consisting of 13 members appointed by the President.
(2) The Board shall consist of:
- (a) a Chairperson and 8 Members qualified in health services management, clinical governance, financial management, business administration, HR, law or relevant fields;
- (b) the Chief Executive Officer, the Deputy Chief Executive Officer, the Director of the Seychelles Hospital, and the Director of the Community Health Services (ex officio members).
(3) Ex officio members under subsection (2)(b) shall have no right to vote.

### 8. Functions of the Board
The functions of the Board are to:
- (a) provide direction to the Chief Executive Officer for effective performance;
- (b) set performance targets in consultation with the Minister and monitor progress.

### 9. Procedures and proceedings
The Second Schedule has effect with respect to the procedures and proceedings of the Board.

### 10. Committees
(1) There shall be Committees of the Board to review, monitor and evaluate specific strategic issues, as prescribed.

## PART IV - CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER, OTHER OFFICERS AND STAFF

### 11. Chief Executive Officer
(1) The President shall appoint a Chief Executive Officer (CEO) on terms determined by the President.
(2) The CEO shall be appointed for a term of three years and is eligible for re-appointment.
(3) The CEO is responsible for the day-to-day administration and management of the Agency under the Board's directions.

### 12. Deputy Chief Executive Officer
(1) The President shall appoint a Deputy Chief Executive Officer.
(2) The Deputy CEO is responsible to the CEO and shall perform functions conferred by the CEO or act as CEO when the CEO is unable to do so.

### 13. Director of Seychelles Hospital
(1) The Minister, in consultation with the Board, shall appoint the Director of the Seychelles Hospital.
(2) The Director is responsible for the day-to-day operation, planning, and coordinating of health care services at the Seychelles Hospital.
(3) The Director is responsible to the CEO.

### 14. Director of Community Health Services
(1) The Minister, in consultation with the Board, shall appoint the Director of Community Health Services.
(2) The Director is responsible for the provision and delivery of community health care services.
(3) The Director is responsible to the CEO.

### 15. Officers and other staff
(1) The Agency shall employ necessary staff on terms determined by the Agency, subject to public body employee procedures.
(2) Every employee shall be under the administrative control of the CEO.

## PART V - FINANCIAL PROVISIONS

### 16. Fund of the Agency
(1) The Agency functions through moneys appropriated by the Appropriation Act.
(2) The fund consists of:
- (a) moneys appropriated by an Appropriation Act;
- (b) grants, donations, and gifts from legal sources.
(3) Funds may be applied for the discharge of expenses, debts, and remuneration of Board members and staff.

### 17. Fees and Revenue
(1) The Agency may charge prescribed fees for health care services (excluding primary health care).
(2) Fees received shall be paid into the Consolidated Fund.

### 18. Accounts and audit
(1) The financial year is the calendar year.
(2) Proper accounts must be maintained in a form approved by the Auditor General.
(3) Article 158 of the Constitution applies to auditing.
(4) Audited statements shall be submitted to the Minister and the Minister for Finance.

### 19. Annual report
The Agency shall prepare an annual report within three months following the calendar year and forward it to the Minister for tabling before the National Assembly.

### 20. Financial statement
The Agency shall submit an annual financial statement for the next financial year at least 90 days before the beginning of the year, distinguishing between internal sources and government requirements.

## PART VI - MISCELLANEOUS

### 21. Application of the Penal Code
Board members, Committees, CEO, Deputy CEO, Directors, and staff are deemed employees of the public service for the purpose of sections 91 to 96 of the Penal Code.

### 22. Protection of acts done in good faith
No suit lies against the Agency or its members/staff for acts done in good faith in pursuance of this Act.

### 23. Disclosure of information
Unauthorized disclosure of information acquired by reason of office is an offence liable to a fine not exceeding SCR 20,000.

### 24. Regulations
(1) The Minister, in consultation with the Board, may make regulations to give effect to this Act.
(2) Regulations may include: amending schedules, prescribing fees for non-primary services, prescribing standards for health care, and forming Board committees.

### 25. Savings and transitional provisions
(1) Relevant Ministry staff employed prior to this Act are deemed employees of the Agency on no less favourable conditions.
(2) Existing government agreements relating to health care services continue as if the Agency were the party.
(3) Pending legal proceedings against the Government regarding health care services shall continue as if the Agency were the party.

### 26. Transfer of property
(1) Movable property used by the Ministry for health care services is transferred to the Agency.
(2) A certificate by the Minister is conclusive evidence of such transfer.

## SECOND SCHEDULE (Section 9)
### Procedures and proceedings of the Board

1. **Terms of office:** Members hold office for three years, eligible for re-appointment, but limited to three terms.
2. **Vacation of office:**
    - (1) Resignation requires three months' notice to the President.
    - (2) The President may remove a member for: incapacity, neglect of duty/misconduct, conviction/imprisonment of 3+ months, or absence from three consecutive meetings without leave.
    - (3) Vacancies are filled for the remaining term of the previous member.
    - (4) Vacancies do not affect the validity of proceedings.
3. **Meetings:**
    - (1) Meet whenever necessary; places/times determined by the Board.
    - (2) Meet at least four times a year.
    - (3) Quorum is five members.
4. **Chairperson:** Presides over meetings. If absent, members elect a presiding member.
5. **Decisions:** Simple majority vote; Chairperson has a casting vote in case of equality.
6. **Co-option:** The Board may co-opt experts for specific matters; co-opted members have no vote.
7. **Disclosure of interest:** Members must disclose interests and recuse themselves from relevant deliberations/decisions.
8. **Regulation:** The Board regulates its own proceedings subject to this Act.
