## Foreword

The 2030 United Nation Agenda for Sustainable Development, adopted by world leaders from 193 member countries in September 2015 at an historic UN summit held at New York city USA. It urges nations to develop and adopt policies and strategies to ensure that the 169 targets of the 17 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) are attained in the next fourteen years. Agenda 2030 also recognizes the key role Science, Technology and Innovation (STI) plays in attaining these goals.

The Government leadership of Seychelles not only recognizes the importance of STI in attaining the SDGs but also the larger role that knowledge plays in socio-economic development. Consequently, the High-Level Steering Committee for Knowledge-Based Economy (KBE) mandated National Institute for Science Technology and Innovation to evolve a national Policy and Strategy for Science, Technology and Innovation 2016-2025, to provide the framework for Seychelles’ transition towards a more Knowledge-Based Economy.

The policy and strategy document, the first by NISTI since its enactment and establishment in 2014, is based on the Key Result Areas of national development strategies. It provides guidelines for other implicit national policies and strategies related to building and strengthening the foundation of Governance and the four pillars of a KBE, postulated by the World Bank Institute – namely, Economic Incentive and Policy Regime; An Educated, Knowledgeable and Skilled Work Force; an efficient National Innovation System; and a Modern and Adequate Information Infrastructure.

The Government is committed to provide full support for the implementation, monitoring and evaluation of these policy initiatives and would like to put in place the institutional arrangements to catalyse this transformation.

Science, technology and innovation should not be viewed as just buzz words or technical jargon, but as means for making people’s lives better. This policy is hence aimed at offering the kind of quality of life, opportunities and socio-economic environment that enhances people’s lives and encourages businesses to invest. Every implementation measure of this policy and strategy document must therefore remain focussed towards attaining this aim.

**Vincent Meriton**,
Vice President, Republic of Seychelles
Chairman, High Level Steering Committee
For Knowledge-Based Economy

## Preview

The Seychelles Policy and Strategy for Science, Technology and Innovation 2016-2025 is not just a statement of desirable intent, but of focussed objectives and clear strategies for Seychelles to transit to a knowledge-based economy.

The policy and strategy incorporates initiatives for building an STI Eco-System through prioritised policy measures and institutional arrangements like the establishment of a People’s Group, National Research Foundation, Research Data Centre, STI Knowledge Hub, Business, Technology and Innovation Incubator and an Integrated STI Governance System, which will help in cross-sectoral, vertical and horizontal integration of STI.

The policy and strategy will be implemented through two five year plans with clear policy objectives converted into well planned and integrated programmes, included in the annual PPBB plan of NISTI, MDAs and related stakeholders to ensure that Seychelles’ transition to a KBE is smooth and timely.

I am sanguine that this being an evidence-based policy and strategy, will receive the wholehearted support of all stakeholders for the integration of STI into the socio-economic development of Seychelles.

## 1.0 FRAMEWORK FOR THE NATIONAL SCIENCE, TECHNOLOGY AND INNOVATION POLICY AND STRATEGY

### 1.1 Background

#### 1.1.1 Socio-Economic Background
Seychelles faces unique challenges: limited population, industrial capacity, and technical human resources. While attaining high-income status, the economy remains vulnerable due to reliance on tourism and fisheries. Transitioning to a **Blue Economy** necessitates significant investment in Biosciences and STI.

#### 1.1.2 STI Management in Seychelles
Historically, STI management lacked institutional support. The **NISTI Act of 2014** established the National Institute for Science, Technology and Innovation as an autonomous body to act as an umbrella for STI development.

#### 1.1.3 Existing STI Policy Environment
Explicit policy is found in the NISTI Act 2014. Implicit policies include SSLUDP 2040, SSDS 2012-20, and NDS 2015-19.

### 1.2 Conceptual Framework
- **1.2.1 Knowledge-Based Economy (KBE):** Focuses on four pillars: economic incentive, education, innovation system, and ICT infrastructure.
- **1.2.2 Role of STI:** Critical for sustainable growth and attaining UN SDGs 2030.
- **1.2.3 GERD:** Aiming for GERD of at least 1% of GDP by 2020.
- **1.2.4 National Innovation System:** Integration of Government, Academia, Industry, and People.

### 1.3 Gaps and Challenges
- Absence of National Planning Body
- Lack of STI Advisory Body
- Institutional Rivalry
- Funding gaps (GERD)
- Inadequate Capacity/Capability
- Lack of Data Repository
- Low Private Sector involvement
- Weak NIS
- Disconnect from community needs
- Outdated IT Eco-System
- Fragmented International Partnerships
- Absence of Evaluation Mechanisms

### 1.4 Vision, Mission and Strategic Objectives
- **Vision:** ‘A knowledge-driven, innovative, prosperous and pristine Seychelles’.
- **Mission:** Embed science, technology, research and innovation into the socio-economic transformation of Seychelles.
- **Strategic Objective:** Transform Seychelles into a KBE and attain UN SDGs by 2030.

### 1.5 Specific Policy Objectives
Build political leadership, increase GERD to 2% by 2025, build STEM skills, create tech enterprises, and establish world-class research infrastructure.

## 3.0 BUILDING AND STRENGTHENING THE PILLARS OF A KNOWLEDGE-BASED ECONOMY

### 3.1 Economic Incentives
Focus on impact assessments of institutional regimes and long-term implications of trade agreements.

### 3.2 Creating a Knowledge Society
- **STEM I&E Education:** Establish COSTEMIE committee, review curriculum, build a national Science Centre.
- **Reversing Brain Drain:** Map diaspora and provide incentives for return.
- **Global Opportunities:** NISTI to act as central coordinator for research funding/training.

### 3.3 Evolving a National Innovation System
- **STI Capacity:** National Research Infrastructure Plan, Technology Transfer Centre.
- **Data Management:** Establish Research Data Centre (RDC) and STI Knowledge Hub; adopt Open Access (OA) Policy.
- **Regional Excellence:** Blue Economy Research Institute (BERI).
- **Entrepreneurship:** Establish BTI Incubators and consolidate the National IPR Act.

### 3.4 Information Infrastructure
- Shift toward cloud-based ERP systems and integrated E-Government architecture.

---

## 4.0 USING STI FOR THE PEOPLE

- **People’s Group:** Involving citizens in the National Research Plan.
- **People-Centric Research:** Prioritizing health (obesity, cancer) and social issues.
- **Social Innovation:** Negating digital divides and obsessive device use.
- **Healthcare:** Using Decision Analytic Models (DAMs) and remote diagnostics.

---

## 5.0 STI IN SOCIO-ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT OF SECTORS

- **Tourism:** Virtual tourism, biometric identity recording, green buildings.
- **Agriculture:** Reducing import dependence (currently 90%), promoting ‘Made in Seychelles’.
- **Blue Economy:** Technology evaluation committees, GIS for artisanal fishermen.
- **Energy/Construction:** Smart Victoria, Carbon Neutral Seychelles (E-cars/E-Bicycles).

---

## 6.0 SMART INTERNATIONAL PARTNERSHIPS

- **Bilateral:** Mega projects (Smart City, 100% RE).
- **Regional:** Compliance with STISA 2024 and SADC/RISDP 2020.
- **International:** Linkages with UN ‘Technology Facilitation Mechanism’ for SDGs.

## 7.0 STRATEGY FOR RESEARCH, TECHNOLOGY AND INNOVATION MANAGEMENT

### 7.1 Overall Strategy
Focus on: Research Potential $\rightarrow$ Planning $\rightarrow$ Data Management $\rightarrow$ Knowledge Society $\rightarrow$ GERD Financing $\rightarrow$ Innovation/Entrepreneurship.

### 7.2 Institutional Arrangements

| Arrangement | Purpose | NISTI Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Committee for STEM I&E | Develop STEM I&E Model | Secretarial support & monitoring |
| National Research Foundation (NRF) | Prioritisation & Funding | Feasibility study, Technology Audit, GERD planning |
| National Research Data Centre | Data repository/Knowledge Hub | Project proposal & collation of research data |
| BTI Incubator | Support MSMEs/Entrepreneurs | Project proposal with Finance & Trade ministries |
| Integrated STI Governance System | Stakeholder linkages | Appoint Advisory Group, Liaison Officers, People's Group |

---

## 8.0 MONITORING, EVALUATION AND IMPACT ASSESSMENT

- **Indicators:** Amalgamate Frascati, Oslo, and World Bank KAM indicators.
- **Mechanism:** STI Focal Persons per sector, biennial physical surveys, and online annual feedback.
- **Review:** Linked to national development strategy review (max every 3 years).

---

## CONCLUSION

Seychelles aims to transition to a KBE not in a general sense, but specifically in **environment management, biosciences, and the Blue Economy**. The guiding motto: *“Think Big, Start Small, and Act Now!”*
