## 1. POLICY INTRODUCTION AND RATIONALE

Since the 2008 financial crisis, the Government has embarked on a series of reforms with the aim of improving public sector performance. While initially the aim was to reduce the size of the public sector, subsequently it was also to improve service delivery. In 2013, the Government adopted **Results-Based Management (RBM)** as the organizing principle behind its public sector reform strategy. RBM comprises four pillars:

1.  Strategic Planning (SP)
2.  Program Performance Based Budgeting (PPBB)
3.  Performance Management System (PMS)
4.  Performance Monitoring and Evaluation (PM&E)

ICT will be a cross-cutting foundation as an RBM enabler to accelerate RBM implementation.

The Government of Seychelles is committed to strengthening PM&E as part of an integrated effort to enhance public sector performance. As the fourth RBM pillar, PM&E is a core element of the policy cycle from planning, budgeting, public service management and monitoring and evaluation.

**Benefits of PM&E:**
*   **(i) Efficiency:** Increases public sector efficiency, creating greater fiscal savings through greater value for money, reduced human/financial resources, and quicker delivery of programs and projects.
*   **(ii) Effectiveness:** Enhances public sector effectiveness, including innovative ways of service-delivery, structures, and processes.
*   **(iii) Transparency:** Strengthens transparency, making information more accessible and government more open.
*   **(iv) Accountability:** Ensures government delivers on its mandate to implement service-delivery as planned, by the responsible staff, within existing budgets and timeframes.

### Specific Objectives
*   **i)** Support the design and implementation for an effective PM&E system across Government.
*   **ii)** Ensure timely and accurate monitoring and introduce a formal evaluation function.
*   **iii)** Clarify the institutional architecture for PM&E across stakeholders.
*   **iv)** Increase the use of PM&E information for policymaking and implementation.
*   **v)** Promote a culture of performance (moving from compliance to continuous learning).
*   **vi)** Strengthen accountability, transparency, and effectiveness of public service-delivery.

## 2. INSTITUTIONAL ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

*   **Office of the President:** Champions and promotes PM&E; uses information for the State of the Nation Address; holds government to account.
*   **Cabinet:** Approves PM&E Policy; uses findings for policy-making; adopts national evaluation plans.
*   **Department of Public Administration (DPA):** Coordinates design/implementation; develops guidelines; oversees tool development; manages capacity-strengthening.
*   **Ministry of Finance, Trade and Economic Planning (MoFTEP):** Integrates PM&E with PPBB and SP; sets targets in national plans; allocates budget based on findings.
*   **MDAs:** Monitors implementation of sector strategic plans; submits evaluations; uses findings to inform sector policy.
*   **RBM Steering Committee:** Harmonizes RBM pillars; adopts the PM&E manual; reviews tool design.
*   **Sectoral RBM Committee:** Harmonizes RBM at the sector level; aligns tools with national practices.
*   **National Bureau of Statistics (NBS):** Collects and analyses data; provides quality assurance and capacity-strengthening.
*   **Department of ICT:** Develops and maintains government-wide data systems and dashboards.
*   **National Assembly:** Oversight of executive management; uses PM&E information to hold the executive to account.
*   **Office of the Auditor General:** Conducts quality audits of performance information.
*   **Private Sector:** Uses PM&E for business strategy and investment; provides feedback on services.
*   **Citizens and Civil Society:** Engage in participatory monitoring and hold government to account.
*   **Tertiary Education:** Develops national capacity and addresses PM&E talent management.

## 3. PM&E FUNCTIONS, TOOLS AND USE

### Monitoring
Monitoring is a continuous management tool to measure progress. It includes systematic collection of data from project level up to the Seychelles National Development Strategy level.

**Reporting Requirements:**
*   **Monthly reports:** Brief progress snapshots to identify actionable recommendations.
*   **Quarterly reports:** Progress towards SNDS targets and key sector/ministerial results.
*   **Annual progress reports:** Comprehensive reports on policy, program, and project implementation to inform Cabinet and budgetary decisions.

### Evaluation Function
Evaluations are periodic, objective assessments to determine relevance, efficiency, effectiveness, impact, and sustainability.

**Types of Evaluation Tools:**
1.  **Diagnostic evaluation:** Establishes baseline and theory of change.
2.  **Design evaluation:** Analyzes theory of change early in the intervention.
3.  **Implementation evaluation:** Understands how the intervention is working.
4.  **Impact evaluation:** Measures change in outcomes (e.g., well-being).
5.  **Economic evaluation:** Focuses on cost-effectiveness or cost-benefit analysis.
6.  **Evaluation synthesis:** Generalizes findings across government.

### Integrating PM&E across RBM Pillars
PM&E provides performance information for:
*   **Strategic Planning:** Assessing progress of SNDS and sector plans.
*   **Budget Process:** Defining PPBB indicators and tracking results for future budgets.
*   **Performance Management:** Tracking targets for individual staff assessments and contracts.

### Use of the PM&E system, tools and findings
Actual use of tools is essential for improvement. This includes regular progress review meetings, performance improvement plans, and integrating results into budget negotiations.

### Citizen Engagement
Transparency is key. This includes publishing progress reports and using participatory monitoring tools such as:
*   Social audits
*   Balanced scorecards
*   Citizen/Community report cards
*   Public hearings

## 4. IMPLEMENTATION OF THE PM&E FUNCTION

### Human and Financial Resources
MDAs must designate PM&E responsibilities to existing staff or recruit PM&E Officers. DPA will lead a capacity development plan and training modules in coordination with MEHRD and TGMI.

### Implementation Plan and Guidelines
An Implementation Plan will be developed as part of the Performance PM&E System Guidelines, covering:
*   Defining indicators and setting targets
*   Reporting mechanisms
*   Public participation guidance

### Change management
A complementary change management, communications, and risk management strategy will be implemented.

### PM&E for PM&E
The system itself will be monitored by the National RBM Steering Committee based on metrics such as:
*   Institutional set-up and staffing
*   Use of PM&E tools (e.g., Annual Progress Report)
*   Integration of findings into PPBB
*   Efficiency and effectiveness gains achieved.
