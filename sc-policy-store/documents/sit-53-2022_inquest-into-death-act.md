# S.I. 53 of 2022
## INQUEST INTO DEATH ACT
**(Act 47 of 2021)**
### Inquest into Death Act, 2021 (Commencement) Notice, 2022

IN EXERCISE OF THE POWERS CONFERRED BY SECTION 1 OF THE INQUEST INTO DEATH ACT, 2021, THE MINISTER RESPONSIBLE FOR LEGAL AFFAIRS MAKES THE FOLLOWING NOTICE —

#### Citation
1. This notice may be cited as the **Inquest into Death Act, 2021 (Commencement) Notice, 2022**.

#### Commencement of Act 47 of 2021
2. The **Inquest into Death Act, 2021**, shall come into operation on 1 June, 2022.

***

MADE this 19th day of April, 2022.

**WAVEL RAMKALAWAN**
PRESIDENT
