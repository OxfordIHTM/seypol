## Foreword

Overall, the drug problem in Seychelles is becoming a national development issue with multiplier effects in many parts of society. This Master Plan provides a comprehensive framework for the period 2019-2023 to guide the national effort to combat the drug problem.

**Key Objectives:**
* Provide a strategic direction for drug control.
* Coordinate efforts between various agencies.
* Improve the quality of rehabilitation and prevention services.

---

## Introduction

### The Context
Seychelles faces a significant challenge with drug abuse, impacting health, security, and social development. The National Drug Control Master Plan 2019-2023 is designed to address these issues through a multi-sectoral approach.

### Strategic Pillars
1. **Prevention:** Reducing demand and preventing the initiation of drug use.
2. **Treatment & Rehabilitation:** Improving access to and quality of care for drug users.
3. **Law Enforcement:** Reducing availability by disrupting supply chains and trafficking.

---

## Situation Analysis

### Drug Trends
* **Heroin:** Remains a primary concern with high rates of dependence.
* **Cannabis:** Widespread use across various demographics.
* **Synthetic Drugs:** Increasing prevalence of methamphetamine and other synthetic stimulants.

### Impact on Society
* **Health:** Rise in HIV/AIDS and Hepatitis C infections among injecting drug users.
* **Social:** Increased crime rates and family breakdown.
* **Economic:** Loss of productivity in the workforce.

---

## Strategic Framework

### Strategic Goal
To reduce the demand for and supply of illicit drugs in Seychelles through a balanced approach of prevention, treatment, and enforcement.

### Strategic Objectives
* **Objective 1:** Strengthen prevention programs to reduce the prevalence of drug use.
* **Objective 2:** Expand and improve rehabilitation and reintegration services.
* **Objective 3:** Enhance the capacity of law enforcement to interdict drug trafficking.

---

## Implementation Plan

### Action Areas
* **Policy Reform:** Reviewing and updating existing drug laws and policies.
* **Capacity Building:** Training for health workers and law enforcement officers.
* **Community Engagement:** Partnering with NGOs and community leaders to promote prevention.

### Resource Allocation
Funding will be sourced from the national budget and international partnerships to ensure sustainable implementation.

---

## Monitoring & Evaluation

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* Number of people accessing rehabilitation services.
* Percentage of the population reached by prevention campaigns.
* Volume of illicit drugs seized by law enforcement.

### Review Mechanism
Quarterly progress reports will be submitted to the National Drug Control Board, with a comprehensive mid-term review scheduled for 2021.
