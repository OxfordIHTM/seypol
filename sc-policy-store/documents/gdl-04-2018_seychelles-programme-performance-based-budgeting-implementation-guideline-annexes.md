# Terms of reference for Portfolio and MDA Structures and key personnel

## Terms of Reference: PPBB Portfolio Working Groups

### Objective
These Terms of Reference (TORs) set out the role and responsibilities of a PPBB Portfolio Working Group (PWG) in the PPBB budget preparation, implementation and reporting process.

### Background
The Government of Seychelles (GoS) is implementing Programme Performance-based Budgeting (PPBB). These TORs apply to this process.

#### Composition of a Portfolio Working Group
Each Portfolio will set up a Portfolio Working Group as suits its structure, provided that the Portfolio Working Group includes the key decision-makers. Portfolio Working Groups must be chaired by a Principal Secretary of the parent ministry. It is advised that the Portfolio Working Group include the following actors:

* Principal Secretary (chair)
* Other Principal Secretaries (if applicable)
* Agency Chief Executive Officers
* Heads of Accounts
* Human Resource Directors
* Director Generals / Programme Managers
* Special Advisors (if applicable)
* The MoFTEP financial analysts for the Portfolio MDA

### Objective of a PPBB Portfolio Working Group
The objective of PPBB Portfolio Working Groups is to ensure that Portfolio budgets and actual expenditure over the medium term reflect agreed, shared Portfolio objectives and reflect agreed Portfolio expenditure priorities to achieve these objectives.

### Role and responsibilities of PPBB Portfolio Working Groups

**Strategic PPBB Budget Preparation Phase:**
* Identify portfolio available resources;
* Discuss MDA forward service delivery improvement priorities and determine key Portfolio priorities;
* Consider earmarked and unearmarked resources;
* Consider expenditure rigidities within component MDA budgets over the medium term;
* Determine the size of resources that are unearmarked;
* Provide indicative ceilings to each MDA;
* Prepare a presentation for the MoFTEP;
* Review Portfolio MDA’s MTES submissions.

**Budget Planning Phase:** Review MDA PPBB Statements and coordinate the preparation for the MoFTEP Budget Review meeting.

**Budget Execution:** Review budget implementation progress in line with Portfolio objectives and priorities.

## Terms of Reference: MDA PPBB Working Groups

### Objective
Set out the role and responsibilities of a MDA PPBB Working Group. Each PPBB Working Group will be responsible for the PPBB MTES, detailed budget proposals as a PPBB Statement, and finalised PPBB Statement for submission to the National Assembly.

### MDA PPBB Working Group composition
* The PS or CEO
* The Head of Accounts
* The HR director
* Divisional / programme managers
* Any advisors to the MDA

### Role and responsibilities of the MDA PPBB Working Group

**Programme structures and performance information design role:**
* Review the need to adjust the Programme Structure;
* Submit rationale for changing programme structure to the MOFTEP;
* Review PPBB performance indicators and data quality.

**Strategic PPBB Budget Preparation Phase:**
* Review MDA expenditure trends;
* Agree priority objectives for the medium term;
* Agree criteria for rating and ranking new spending proposals;
* Determine possible MDA savings and efficiency gains;
* Oversee the preparation of the MTES document.

**Budget Planning Phase:**
* Discuss narrative content of the PPPB Statement;
* Review MDA baseline and new spending proposals;
* Discuss and agree performance targets.

**Budget Presentation Phase:** Adjust draft PPBB Statement to the final budget ceiling.

**Preparation for budget implementation:**
* Review apportionment of expenditure to cost centres;
* Coordinate MDA procurement plan and cash flow projection.

**Budget implementation and reporting:**
* Monthly review of programme-based expenditure reports;
* Quarterly review of non-financial performance measures;
* Coordinate the mid-year review process.

**After the end of the fiscal year:** Drive the preparation of the Section 31 report and APR.

## Terms of Reference: MDA PPBB Technical Teams

### Objective
Support the MDA Working Group by undertaking the detailed technical work required to develop a MTES and PPBB Statement.

### Composition of a PPBB MDA Technical team
* MDA Head of Accounts
* MDA Human Resource Director
* Designated writer for MTES and PPBB Statements

### Role and responsibilities of MDA Technical Teams

**Strategic Phase:**
* Undertake expenditure analysis;
* Identify available resources (ceilings, receipts, donor funds);
* Calculate MDA baselines using the PPBB Tool;
* Draft the MDA MTES.

**Detailed Budget Planning Phase:**
* Advise on adjustments to fit proposals within ceiling;
* Implement agreed adjustments through the PPBB Tool.

**Budget Implementation:**
* Coordinate break-down of allocations by cost centre;
* Prepare monthly, quarterly and half-yearly expenditure reports.

**Ex-post reporting:** Compile a draft Section 31 Report.

## Terms of Reference: Accounting Officers

### Objective
Responsible for the allocation and use of resources within an MDA to achieve MDA objectives.

### Key Responsibilities
* **PPBB Protocols Manual:** Instruct the Technical Team to prepare, review, and adopt a manual.
* **Appointment of Programme Managers:** Delegate accountability for each programme and sub-programme.
* **Budget Preparation:** Convene and chair the PPBB Working Group; ensure quality and timely submission of MTES and PPBB Statements.
* **Budget Implementation:** Ensure resource use aligns with appropriated budget and financial instructions; approve virements.
* **Year-end:** Oversee and approve the Section 31 Report.

## Terms of Reference: PPBB Programme Managers

### Objective
Responsible for the use of resources in the Programme, and for monitoring and reporting on performance measures.

### Role and responsibilities

**Strategic Phase:**
* Ensure programme baselines are reliable;
* Develop and cost new spending proposals;
* Develop performance measures and targets.

**Budget Planning Phase:**
* Ensure detailed budget for the programme is correct;
* Provide text inputs for the PPBB Statement.

**Budget Presentation Phase:** Agree budget adjustments based on final ceilings.

**Preparation for Implementation:** Coordinate programme procurement plans and cash flow projections.

**Budget Implementation:**
* Monitor expenditure and use of resources;
* Manage performance measure data collection;
* Approve virements between sub-programmes.

**Year-end:** Provide data and explanations for the Section 31 APR.

## Document 2: MTES Template Guidelines

### Objectives
Assist in submitting a high-quality MTES. Poor submissions can negatively affect future funding.

### Part-by-Part Guidance
* **Part 1: Expenditure Strategy at a Glance** - Insert tables from PPBB Tool: Consolidated proposal, Summary Strategy, and Scenario B Request.
* **Part 2: Strategic Overview** - Summarize mandate, strategic achievements, and external/internal challenges.
* **Part 3: Strategic Priorities** - Identify 5 top priorities linked to challenges; must be strategic, not operational activities.
* **Part 4: Revenue** - List available resources; explain non-consolidated fund revenue.
* **Part 5: Budget Proposal** - Detail Scenario A (within ceiling) and Scenario B (additional requests). Explain the logic for savings and the prioritisation of NSPs.
* **Part 6: Performance Information** - Define outcomes, outcome indicators, contributing indicators, and targets.
* **Part 7: Scenario B** - Justify the importance of additional requests.

2. **Budget Summary** (Table from tool Sum sheet)

    * Purpose of Programme

    * Consolidated programme expenditure estimates
    * Performance measures table

# Draft Section 31 Report

### Components:
* **Summary of Preliminary Outturn**
* **Strategic Overview:** Performance summary, objective review, and key achievements/failures.
* **Budget Overview:** Revenue and Expenditure outturns with variance explanations.
* **Programme Sections:** For each programme:
    * Strategic overview
    * Performance Measures vs. Targets
    * Financial Performance (Variances by sub-programme and economic item)
* **Additional Tables:** PSIP implementation and economic item details.

# PPBB Tool Guidance

### Tool Structure
* **Costing Sheets:** Manpower, Grat, GS, PSIP, MCO, NSPs.
* **Consolidation Sheets:** PBudget, MTES, ScenA, Summary Request.
* **Budgeting Sheets:** Workspace (strategic trade-offs), ScenB (additional requests).
* **Background Sheets:** Index, Names, Lists, etc.

### Key Operational Logic
1. **Project Baselines:** Cost existing payroll, gratuities, goods/services, and existing PSIP/MCO projects.
2. **Budgeting:** Use the Workspace sheet to adjust baselines and add NSPs to fit the ceiling.
3. **Scenario B:** Prioritize requests for funding above the ceiling.

### Common Principles
* All data in SR thousands (SR '000s).
* Do not delete/add columns (breaks referencing).
* Save files as **.XLSM** to preserve macros.
* Use drop-down menus to ensure tool stability.

# Guidance to prepare PPBB Protocols Manual

### Purpose
Establish a customized internal manual to adapt MDA business processes to the PPBB system.

### Suggested Content

**1. Budget Planning/Preparation**
* MTES timing and responsibility
* Budget preparation templates
* Cash flow forecasting and procurement planning

**2. Budget Execution**
* LPO/PV approval processes
* Procurement thresholds and contract management
* Virement request and approval flow

**3. Monitoring and Reporting**
* Financial and non-financial monitoring systems
* Frequency of reports and management meetings
* Section 31 report preparation protocols
