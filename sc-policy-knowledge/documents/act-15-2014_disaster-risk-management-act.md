## PART I — PRELIMINARY

1. This Act may be cited as the **Disaster Risk Management Act 2014** and shall come into force on such date as shall be determined by the Minister, way notice published in the Gazette.
3. In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires—

* **"disaster"** means a serious disruption of the functioning of a community or a society involving widespread human, material, economic or environmental losses and impacts, which may be caused by natural hazards or human inducement, including arising from accident, social disruption, nature or human intervention, that exceeds the ability of the affected community to cope using its own resources.

* **"disaster risk"** means the potential disaster losses, in terms of human lives, health, livelihoods, assets and services, which or that could occur to a society community or a society over a specified some future time period.

* **"disaster risk reduction"** means the concept and practice of reducing disaster risks through systematic systematic efforts to analyse and manage the causal factors of disasters. It reduces disaster risk through reducing exposure to hazards, increasing vulnerability of people, and increasing heightened vulnerability of people, and improving capacities to manage risk of the environment, and improved preventiveness for avoiding disaster.

* **"disaster risk area"** means an area where a disaster has occurred or an area determined as a disaster risk area in accordance with the provisions of this Act by contingency planning processes and relevant determinations.

* **"disaster area"** means a district, region, island or part thereof declared or designated as a disaster area for intervention and assistance as a result of a disaster related losses.

* **"Division"** means the Disaster Risk Management Division established under section 3;

* **"Director General"** means the person appointed under section 4;

* **"early warning systems"** means the set of capacities invented to generate and disseminate timely and actionable warnings to at-risk populations and vulnerable individuals, communications and organisations throughout the hazard chain, to enable them to respond and sufficiently time to reduce the effects the possibility of their losses.

* **"Ford"** means the National Disaster Risk Management Fund established by section 11;

* **"Government"** includes a Ministry, Department of Government, Statutory Corporation, any other statutory body, municipality government agency or any other organ of government;

* **"Minister"** means the Minister responsible for disaster risk management.

* **"National Council"** means the National Disaster Risk Management Committee established by section 5;

* **"prescribed"** means prescribed by regulations made under this Act.
