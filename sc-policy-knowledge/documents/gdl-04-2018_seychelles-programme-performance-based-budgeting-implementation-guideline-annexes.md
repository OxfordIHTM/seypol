# Terms of reference for Portfolio and MDA Structures and key personnel

## Terms of Reference: PPBB Portfolio Working Groups

### Objective

These Terms of Reference (TORs) set out the role and responsibilities of a PPBB Portfolio Working Group (PWG) in the PPBB budget preparation, implementation and reporting process.

### Background

The Government of Seychelles (GoS) is implementing Programme Performance-based Budgeting (PPBB). These TORs apply to this process.
# Program Based Budgeting (PPBB) Portfolio Working Groups

A key objective of PPBB is to strengthen the links between Government’s objectives and priorities, and the budget. This requires Ministries, Departments and Agencies (MDAs) to undertake medium term budgeting according to expenditure programmes, and provide systematic information on their expected performance.

In some cases Ministries / Departments and their associated agencies have elected to work in Portfolios to ensure medium term alignment between their expenditure programmes and their shared Portfolio objectives and priorities. A Portfolio comprises a Ministry and its associated agencies of one functional sector. In these cases, a PPBB Portfolio Working Group must be formed.

Porfolio MDAs may also meet during budget implementation to review execution across MDAs. In this case, a Portfolio PPBB Working Group may be formed.

These generic terms of reference set out a generic structure and responsibilities of a Portfolio Working Group. However, Portfolios may deviate from this norm, based on portfolio-specific considerations.

#### Composition of a Portfolio Working Group

Each Portfolio will set up a Portfolio Working Group as suits its structure, provided that the Portfolio Working Group includes the key decision-makers. Portfolio Working Groups must be chaired by a Principal Secretary of the parent ministry. It is advised that the Portfolio Working Group include the following actors:

* Principal Secretary (chair)
* Other Principal Secretaries (if applicable)
* Agency Chief Executive Officers
* Heads of Accounts
* Human Resource Directors
* Director Generals / Programme Managers
* Special Advisors (if applicable)
* The MoFTEP financial analysts for the Portfolio MDA

### Objective of a PPBB Portfolio Working Group

The objective of PPBB Portfolio Working Groups is to ensure that Portfolio budgets and actual expenditure over the medium term reflect agreed, shared Portfolio objectives and reflect agreed Portfolio expenditure priorities to achieve these objectives.

### Role and responsibilities of PPBB Portfolio Working Groups

Portfolio Working Groups are active particularly in the strategic phase of the PPBB budget preparation process, but will also review the Portfolio budgets in the detailed budgeting phase and during budget execution.

**Strategic PPBB Budget Preparation Phase:** The role of the Portfolio Working Groups during the strategic phase is to agree on how the Portfolio’s total available resources will be divided amongst MDAs before MDAs undertake a Medium Term Expenditure Strategy (MTES). The purpose of this is to look at the strategic trade-offs across the budget lines of Portfolio MDAs in line with Portfolio objectives and priorities.

The Portfolio PPBB Working Group will:

* Identify portfolio available resources;
* Discuss MDA forward service delivery improvement priorities and determine key Portfolio priorities;
* Consider earmarked (for a specific MDA) and unearmarked (not yet allocated for specific purposes of one MDA) resources;
* Consider expenditure rigidities within component MDA budgets over the medium term;
* Determine the size of resources that are unearmarked and can be moved between MDAs given past expenditure amounts by MDA and forward priorities (‘free’ resources);
* Provide indicative ceilings to each MDA comprising: (i) their earmarked resources; (ii) an allocation to cover expenditure rigidities; (iii) a share of ‘free’ resources in line with the priority assigned by the Portfolio to the MDAs services;
* Prepare a presentation to discuss the decided indicative MDA ceilings with the MoFTEP; and
* Review Portfolio MDA’s MTES submissions to ensure that proposals are in line with Portfolio objectives, discussions and decisions.

**Budget Planning Phase**: The role of the Portfolio Working Group in the Budget Planning Phase will be to ensure the alignment of detailed Portfolio MDA budgets with the Portfolio priorities. In order to fulfil this role the Portfolio Working Group will meet to review MDA PPBB Statements before their submission to the MoFTEP. The Portfolio Working Group will also coordinate the preparation for the MoFTEP Budget Review meeting, if the Portfolio is called to discuss its budget submission.

**Budget Execution**: During budget execution Portfolio PPBB Working Groups may meet to review budget implementation progress in line with Portfolio objectives and priorities. This might occur even in Portfolios that did not follow a Portfolio-based initial budget process.

## Terms of Reference: MDA PPBB Working Groups

### Objective
# Terms of Reference (TORs)

These Terms of Reference (TORs) set out the role and responsibilities of a MDA PPBB Working Group in the PPBB budget preparation, implementation and reporting processes. When a ministry is a unitary ministry (in other words responsible for one coherent functional sector) there will be only one PPBB Working Group for the ministry. When a ministry comprises several departments and each is in charge of a different functional sector, there may be a PPPB Team for each department. Each agency will have a PPBB Working Group. Each PPBB Working Group will be responsible for the PPBB MTES, detailed budget proposals as a PPBB Statement, and finalised PPBB Statement for submission to the National Assembly.

## Background

The Government of Seychelles (GoS) is implementing Programme Performance-based Budgeting (PPBB). These TORs apply to this process.

A key objective of PPBB is to strengthen the links between Government’s objectives and priorities, and the budget. This requires Ministries, Departments and Agencies (MDAs) to undertake medium term budgeting according to expenditure programmes, and provide systematic information on their expected performance.

The budget preparation process now includes a strategic phase during which MDAs consider how best to finance their priority objectives over the medium term, given their resources. The output from this phase is a Medium Term Expenditure Strategy (MTES), the MDA’s key budget submission instrument.

The strategic phase is followed by a budget planning phase. In this phase MDAs will develop their detailed medium term budgets, within ceilings determined by the MoFTEP. These ceilings will be calculated based on the MTESs and national priorities. The final stage of PPBB budget preparation is the PPBB Statement and presentation phase, during which the public PPBB budget documentation is prepared and presented to Parliament.

PPBB also drives budget implementation, insofar as cash flow projections, procurement plans, expenditure controls and budget reporting will be by programme. The mid-year review and mid-year reports will also be on a programme basis.

These generic terms of reference set out a generic structure and responsibilities of a MDA PPBB Working Group. However, MDAs may deviate from this norm, based on MDA-specific considerations, provided that all the functions of developing a MTES, detailed budgets and budget documentation are covered.

## MDA PPBB Working Group composition

The MDA PPBB Working Group will comprise:

* The PS or CEO
* The Head of Accounts or the officer with Heads of Account responsibilities
* The HR director
* Divisional / programme managers
* Any advisors to the MDA.

## Objective of a MDA PPBB Working Group

The objective of the MDA PPBB Working Group is to ensure that MDA budgets and actual expenditure over the medium term reflect agreed MDA objectives and expenditure priorities to achieve these objectives.

## Role and responsibilities of the MDA PPBB Working Group

Overall the MDA PPBB Working Group is responsible to provide guidance on, review and approve **the MDA PPBB Protocols Manual**, in line with the PPBB Guideline Main Document.

The MDA PPBB Working Group is active in all the budget phases.

**Programme structures and performance information design role**: The MDA PPBB Working Group takes primary responsibility for reviewing the MDA programme structure and performance indicators. Specifically MDA PPBB Working Groups will at the start of the MTES phase:

* Review the need to adjust the Programme Structure based on changes in mandate, or if the existing structure does not lend itself to the allocation and management of the MDA’s budget in line with its objectives.
* Submit rationale for changing programme structure to the MOFTEP
* If agreed by the MOFTEP, adjust the programme structure following the process set out in the PPBB Guidelines Main Document;
* Review the PPBB performance indicators for the MDA in line with the criteria set out in the PPBB Guidelines Main Document, and MDA priorities, resources and performance;
* Review PPBB performance indicator data quality;
* Adjust the performance information following the process set out in the PPBB Guidelines Main Document.

**Strategic PPBB Budget Preparation Phase:** The role of the MDA PPBB Working Group during the strategic phase is to undertake and coordinate the MDA MTES. The MTES is the key instrument for MDAs to agree how available resources will be used strategically, to achieve MDA objectives.

In the development of a MTES the MDA PPBB Working Group will:

* Review MDA expenditure trends;
* Discuss and agree the priority objectives of the MDA for the medium term;
* Agree criteria at the MDA level for rating and ranking new spending proposals;
* Ensure that all MDA revenue sources are included in the assessment of MDA available resources by the Technical Team;
* Review the calculation of baseline expenditure by the Head of Accounts / MDA Technical Team;
* Determine possible MDA savings and efficiency gains;
* Identify new spending proposals and cost, rate, rank and describe them for the MTES;
* Agree the expenditure strategy (the trade-off between baselines, NSPs and savings within available resources); and
* Oversee the preparation of the MTES document.
* Provide inputs into the MDA presentation to the MoFTEP MTES hearing, and participate in the hearing as required.

### Budget Planning Phase

The MDA PPBB Working Group is responsible for overseeing the preparation and submission of the MDA PPBB Statement together with the detailed excel tables. During this phase the MDA PPBB Working Group will:

* Discuss the narrative content of the PPPB Statement (achievements, challenges and priorities), and assign writing tasks and deadlines.
* Review the MDA baseline calculated earlier.
* Check for additional new spending proposals that emerged after the MTES process.
* Check available resources given the MoFTEP issued ceilings, and assess the adjustments that are needed to the MTES proposals to fit within ceiling.
* Discuss and agree performance targets, give the allocation of available funding.
* Review the compiled PPBB Statement before submission to the Minister and/or Board for approval.
* Provide inputs into the MDA presentation to the MoFTEP budget hearing, and participate in the hearing as required.

### Budget Presentation Phase

After receiving the final budget ceiling for the MDA from the MoFTEP, the MDA PPBB Working Group will adjust the draft PPBB Statement to the ceiling as required, assign responsibilities for adjusting the draft PPBB Statement and review the finalised text before submission to the Minister and/or Board for approval, and the MoFTEP for inclusion in the budget documentation.

### Preparation for Budget Implementation

It is the responsibility of the MDA PPBB Working Group to review:

* Apportionment of expenditure from the programme structure to the cost centres (if any) of the MDA. This is to ensure control of expenditure during budget implementation, in line with programme appropriations;
* Coordinate the preparation of a MDA procurement plan, comprising the aggregation of programme procurement plans by programme managers; and
* Coordinate the preparation of a MDA cash flow projection, taking into account the procurement plan.

### Budget Implementation and Reporting

The MDA PPBB Working Group is responsible for monitoring the implementation of the budget by Programmes, in line with the budget appropriations. Secondly, during budget implementation the MDA PPBB Working Group will agree all virements of funds between programmes. Thirdly, the MDA PPBB Working Group will conduct their monitoring of budget implementation through the monthly and quarterly reporting requirements, and agree to the reports submitted. Specifically, the MDA PPBB Working Group will:

* Meet monthly to review the monthly programme-based expenditure reports against the budget and the cash flow plan. The MDA PPBB Working Group will identify where corrective action is required, for the PS / CEO to implement. Virement requests from programmes will be discussed during the monthly meetings, assessed and approved.
* Meet quarterly to review the quarterly programme-based expenditure reports against the budget and cash flow plan, and review the non-financial performance measures. The MDA PPBB Working Group will identify where corrective action is required, for the PS / CEO to implement.
* Coordinate the mid-year review process, including the provision of updated performance measures to the MoFTEP.

### After the End of the Fiscal Year

The MDA PPBB Working Group will be responsible to drive the preparation of the Section 31 report, by:

* Compiling the tables of the Report, noting where material deviations from budget occurred, and seeking explanations from the relevant Programme Managers;
* Discussing the narrative content of the Report, agreeing content and assigning writing tasks;
* Compile and review the APR, and submitting to the Minister / Board for their information, before submitting to the Accounting Officer for formal signature and submission to the Minister of Finance.

# Terms of Reference: MDA PPBB Technical Teams

## Objective

These Terms of Reference (TORs) set out the role and responsibilities of a PPBB MDA Technical Team in the PPBB budget preparation process.

## Background

The Government of Seychelles (GoS) is implementing Programme Performance-based Budgeting (PPBB). These TORs apply to this process.

These generic terms of reference set out a generic structure and responsibilities of a MDA PPBB Technical Team. However, MDAs may deviate from this norm, based on MDA-specific considerations, provided that all the functions of developing a MTES, detailed budgets and budget documentation are covered.

## Composition of a PPBB MDA Technical team

Each MDA must set up a Technical Team as suits its structure and available human resources. The MDA must appoint a coordinator who will coordinate and direct the work of the technical team. It is advised that a MDA Technical team include following actors:

* MDA Head of Accounts or the officer fulfilling the duties of the Head of Accounts
* MDA Human Resource Director
* One person designated to undertake the writing tasks associated with the development of a MTES and PPBB Statements.

The Coordinator will be appointed at the MDA level, and will report to the Chairperson of the MDA Working Group. The Technical team will liaise with MDA Director Generals or programme managers as required.

## Objective of a MDA Technical Team

The objective of PPBB MDA Technical Team is to support the MDA Working Group by undertaking the detailed technical work required to develop a MTES and PPBB Statement. The Technical Team would be expected to draw on expertise from the technical specialists, where needed.

## Role and responsibilities of MDA Technical Teams

MDA Technical Teams will be active in all phases of the budget process.

Overall the MDA Technical Team is responsible for the development of **the MDA PPBB Protocols Manual**, in line with the PPBB Guideline Main Document.

The role of the MDA Working Groups during **the strategic phase** is to agree on strategic trade-offs across the budget lines of MDA MDAs in line with MDA objectives and priorities, within available MDA resources. The MTES is the key instrument for MDAs to this end. Developing a MTES requires a coordinated MDA-based process of assessing MDA revenue, baselines, and potential savings and efficiency gains to calculate the space available for new policy initiatives and projects and acceleration of existing initiatives and projects.

In the development of a MTES the Technical Team, will:

* Undertake expenditure analysis to review MDA expenditure trends;
* Identify MDA available resources, taking into account the MoFTEP issued indicative MDA envelope, retained receipts (if any) projections and donor cash resources (if any), and ensure that the associated expenditure is included in the baseline and budgets;
* Calculate MDA baselines using the PPBB Tool;
* Seek MDA efficiency gains;
* Determine the magnitude of MDA new spending space, taking into account MDA savings / efficiency gains using the PPBB Tool;
* Advise programme managers to compile and cost new spending proposals, and enter data into the PPBB Tool;
* Ensure compliance of MDA new spending proposals with MDA Working Group requirements and collate them into a consolidated document for the MDA Working Group to consider;
* Draft the MDA MTES for consideration by the MDA Working Group;
* Prepare presentations for use by the MDA in the MTES hearing with the MoFTEP;
* Undertake any other technical work as requested by the MDA Working Group.

The role of the MDA PPBB Technical Team during the PPBB **Detailed budget planning phase** is to:

* Advise the PPBB Working Group on the magnitude of the adjustments needed to fit the MDAs proposals within ceiling.
* Work with Programme Managers to identify adjustments to the MTES proposals to fit within ceiling and prepare options for the PPBB Working Group.
* Ensure that Programme Managers are reviewing their PPBB performance targets in line with resources.
* Implement the agreed adjustments through the PPBB Tool and compile the Draft MDA PPBB Statement for review by the Working Group, approval by the Minister/Board and submission to the MoFTEP.

During the **Budget presentation phase**, the Technical Team will repeat the adjustment process set out in the paragraph above to bring the final PPBB Proposals. The Technical Team will also assist the Accounting Officer to prepare for presentation of the MDAs proposals to the National Assembly.

During **Budget Implementation**, the Technical Team will:

* Coordinate the break-down of programme allocations by cost centre (if any).
* Coordinate the preparation of procurement plans by Programme Managers.
* Prepare cash flow projections for approval by the PPBB Working Group.
* Prepare the monthly, quarterly and half-yearly PPBB expenditure reports for review by the PPBB Working Group.
* Manage the Performance Information recording tool.
* Prepare quarterly and half-yearly performance reports, by coordinating with Programme Managers for submission of performance.

During **ex-post reporting**, the Technical Team will:

* Provide instructions to Programme Managers on information required for the Section 31 Report.
* Compile a draft Section 31 Report for review by the PPBB Working Group, and approval by the Accounting Officer, Minister and/or Board.

### Work Programme of the Technical Team

The PPBB MDA Coordinator will develop a detailed work programme for the technical team against the high level MTES schedule, setting technical team meetings and delegating tasks to team members, and ensuring that the deadlines are met.

## Terms of Reference: Accounting Officers

### Objective

These Terms of Reference (TORs) set out the role and responsibilities of Accounting Officers in the PPBB system in budget preparation, implementation and reporting. Accounting officers are responsible for the allocation and use of resources within an MDA, to achieve MDA objectives.

### Preparing a MDA PPBB Protocols Manual

The Accounting Officer must instruct the Technical Team to prepare a PPBB Protocols Manual for the MDA, setting out the MDA’s specific processes, roles and responsibilities.

The Accounting Officer is responsible to ensure that the Manual is completed, reviewed and adopted by the PPBB Working Group.

The Accounting Officer is responsible to ensure that the Manual is reviewed every year and revised.

### Appointment of Programme Managers

The Accounting Officer for a MDA is accountable for the use of resources as appropriated and for the achievement of performance objectives and targets as stated. The Accounting Officer will delegate this accountability to Programme Managers, with one Manager appointed against each programme and sub-programme. There may not be a one-on-one relationship between programme managers, and the managers of administrative cost centres or divisions. Each Accounting Officer will decide criteria for appointing Programme Managers if there are non-direct relationships. The PPBB Implementation Guidelines issued by the MoFTEP provides guidance in this regard.
# Role and responsibilities in Budget Preparation

### Strategic PPBB Budget Preparation Phase
During the **Strategic PPBB Budget Preparation Phase** Accounting Officers will / are responsible to:

* Serve on the Portfolio PPBB Working Groups if this group is constituted by the Portfolio to represent the MDA.
* Convene and chair the PPBB Working Group.
* Ensure the appointment of a PPBB Technical Group, specifically a person to write the narrative sections of the MTES and ensure that it is compiled correctly.
* Oversee the work of the Technical Team.
* Lead on making trade-off decisions within the PPBB Working Group.
* Ensure the quality of the MTES.
* Submit the MTES to the Board and/or Minister.
* Submit the MTES to the MoFTEP on time.

### Budget Planning Phase
During the **Budget Planning Phase** Accounting Officers will / are responsible to:

* Fulfil the same duties regarding chairing and leading the PPBB Working Group and oversight of the work of the Technical Team.
* Ensure the quality of the PPBB Statement.
* Submit the PPBB Statement to the Board and/or Minister.
* Submit the PPBB Statement to the MoFTEP on time.

### Budget Presentation Phase
During the **Budget Presentation Phase**, Accounting Officers will / are responsible to:

* Fulfil the same duties regarding chairing and leading the PPBB Working Group, oversight of the work of the Technical Team, and preparing, approving and submitting the PPBB Statement.
* Ensure the PPBB Statement is submitted on time to the MoFTEP.
* Prepare for presentation of the MDAs PPBB Statement to the National Assembly.
* Assemble and lead the technical team for appearing before the National Assembly, if needed.

### Preparation for Budget Implementation
During the **Preparation for budget implementation** Accounting officers will / are responsible to:

* Chair and lead the PPBB Working Group’s work during this phase.
* Oversee the work of the Techncial Team to prepare cash flow submissions and procurement plans.
* Ensure the submission of cashflow projections and procurement plans on time to the MoFTEP.

### Budget Implementation and Reporting
During **Budget implementation and reporting** Accounting officers will / are responsible to:

* Ensuring the use of resources in line with the appropriated programme budget, and within the financial instructions issued by the MOFTEP in line with the PFMA and any other instructions.
* Convening the PPBB Working Group.
* Overseeing the work of the Technical Team.
* Chairing and leading the work of the PPBB Working Group.
* Approving requests for virements in line with PPBB Working Group discussions.
* Receive monthly reports on the implementation of the MDA’s budget, monitoring progress against financial and non-financial commitments.
* Ensuring that corrective action is taken to address material deviations.
* Lead the MDA during the mid-term review.

### After the End of the Fiscal Year
**After the end of the fiscal year Accounting Officers will / are responsible to:**

* Oversee the preparation of the Section 31 Report, through convening and leading the work of the PPBB Working Group, and overseeing the Technical Team.
* Approve the Section 31 Report.
* Ensure that the Board and Minister have seen the Section 31 Report and their comments have been taken into account.
* Submit the Section 31 Report on time to the MoFTEP.
# Terms of Reference: PPBB Programme Managers

## Objective

These Terms of Reference (TORs) set out the role and responsibilities of a Programme Manager and sub-programme manager in the PPBB system in budget preparation, implementation and reporting. Programme Managers are the individuals who are responsible for the use of resources in the Programme, in line with the programme budget, and for monitoring and reporting on performance measures against the programme.

## Background

The Government of Seychelles (GoS) is implementing Programme Performance-based Budgeting (PPBB). These TORs apply to this process.

A key objective of PPBB is to strengthen the links between Government’s objectives and priorities, and the budget. This requires Ministries, Departments and Agencies (MDAs) to undertake medium term budgeting according to expenditure programmes, and provide systematic information on their expected performance.

The budget preparation process now includes a strategic phase during which MDAs consider how best to finance their priority objectives over the medium term, given their resources. The output from this phase is a MDA Medium Term Expenditure Strategy (MTES), the MDAs’ key budget submission document.

The strategic phase is followed by a budget planning phase. In this phase MDAs will develop their detailed medium term budgets, within ceilings determined by the MoFTEP. These ceilings will be calculated based on the MTESs and national priorities. The final stage of PPBB budget preparation is the PPBB Statement and presentation phase, during which the public PPBB budget documentation is prepared and presented to Parliament.

PPBB also drives budget implementation, insofar as cash flow projections, procurement plans, expenditure controls and budget reporting will be by programme. The mid-year review and mid-year reports will also be on a programme basis.

The PPBB system also requires Programme Managers to be in place. Programme Managers are the individuals who are responsible for the use of resources in the Programme, in line with the programme budget, and for monitoring and reporting on performance measures against the programme.

This Terms of Reference provide generic roles and responsibilities of Programme Managers. For each MDA the exact roles and responsibilities will be set out in the MDA PPBB Protocols Manual.

## Appointment of Programme Managers

The Accounting Officer for a MDA is accountable for the use of resources as appropriated and for the achievement of performance objectives and targets as stated. The Accounting Officer will delegate this accountability to Programme Managers, with one Manager appointed against each programme and sub-programme. There may not be a one-on-one relationship between programme managers, and the managers of administrative cost centres or divisions. Each Accounting Officer will decide criteria for appointing Programme Managers if there are non-direct relationships. The PPBB Implementation Guidelines issued by the MoFTEP provides guidance in this regard.

## Role and responsibilities of a PPBB Programme Manager

**Strategic PPBB Budget Preparation Phase:** Programme managers will serve on MDA PPBB Working Groups and will provide inputs into the preparation of a MTES as required, drawing on further technical resources and information in his/her programme, including sub-programme managers. In some cases sub-programme managers will also serve on the MDA PPBB Working Group. Specifically Programme Managers (and sub-programme managers for sub-programmes) will be responsible for:

* Cooperating with the MDA Technical Team.
* Ensuring that programme baselines are reliable and complete.
* Developing, costing and describing new spending proposals, using the prescribed tools in coordination with the financial controller.
* Developing new performance measures if required and determining targets given available resources.
* Drafting text for the MTES as required.

**Budget Planning Phase**: Programme managers will serve on MDA PPBB Working Groups and will provide inputs into the preparation of the draft PPBB Statement as required, drawing on further technical resources and information in his/her programme, including sub-programme managers. In some cases sub-programme managers will also serve on the MDA PPBB Working Group. Specifically Programme Managers (and sub-programme managers for sub-programmes) will be responsible for:

* Cooperating with any requests from the PPBB Technical Team;
* Ensuring that the detailed budget for the programme is correct;
* In the first year ensuring that ongoing PSIP projects are correctly classified in the economic classification segment;
* Proposing targets for all performance measures in line with allocated resources;
* Providing text inputs into the PPBB Statement as required for the programme.
* If the manager of a programme with sub-programmes, coordinating inputs from sub-programme managers.

**Budget Presentation Phase:** Programme managers will serve on MDA PPBB Working Groups and will provide inputs into the preparation of the final PPBB Statement as required. Specifically Programme Managers (or sub-programme managers) will:

* Cooperate with any requests from the PPBB Technical Team;
* Agree adjustments to their draft PPBB Statement budgets based on the programme’s final ceiling as decided by the MDA PPBB Working Group, based on the MoFTEP final MDA ceiling.
* Ensure that the final detailed budget is a reliable predictor of actual expenditure;
* Ensure that the published performance targets are achievable, and fair given resources; and
* Provide final text inputs as required by the PPBB Working Group.

### Preparation for budget implementation
Programme managers will serve on MDA PPBB Working Groups and will provide inputs into the preparation for budget implementation as required. Specifically Programme Managers (or sub-programme managers) will

* Advise on and check the apportionment of expenditure from the programme structure to the cost centres (if any) of the Programme;
* Coordinate the preparation of a Programme procurement plan;
* Prepare a Programme cash flow projection in collaboration with the Head of Accounts or any other official undertaking Head of Accounts duties; and
* If the Programme Manager of a programme with sub-programmes, collate the procurement plan and cash flow projection inputs of sub-programme managers for the programme.

### Budget implementation and reporting
Programme Managers are responsible for the use of resources in line with the appropriated programme budget, and within cross-government virement rules such as limits on moving funding from capital to recurrent expenditure. They are also responsible for approving virements between sub-programmes, and monitoring the achievement of non-financial performance targets.

Depending on the responsibilities set out in the MDA’s PPBB Protocols Manual, Programme Managers may be responsible for approving Local Payment Orders for all expenses against the programme’s budget.

Specifically during budget implementation Programme Managers (or sub-programme managers for sub-programmes) will

* Receive monthly reports on the implementation of the programme’s budget, against the sub-programmes and main economic items of expenditure as approved in the budget, and apportioned to cost centres. The Programme Manager will be responsible for taking corrective action to address material deviations if all cost centres fall under the Programme Manager’s department, division or unit. If not, the Programme Manager will be responsible for reporting the need for corrective action to the relevant department, divisional or unit manager, and if no corrective action is taken, reporting the matter to the PPBB Working Group.
* Be responsible for the management of the performance measures used against the programme’s budget, ensuring that data is collected and stored, and that the measure is calculated as regularly as is appropriate, given the measure.
* Monitor the achievement of performance measure targets, as regularly as is appropriate given the measures and the needs of programme management. The Programme Manager will be responsible for taking corrective action to address material deviations if all cost centres fall under the Programme Manager’s department, division or unit. If not, the Programme Manager will be responsible for reporting the need for corrective action to the relevant department, divisional or unit manager, and if no corrective action is taken, reporting the matter to the PPBB Working Group.
* The Programme Manager will be responsible for approving virements between sub-programmes, if a programme comprises several sub-programmes. In such cases the Programme Manager must ensure that the sub-programme manager of the sub-programme that will have fewer resources, has agreed to the virement of funds. The Programme Manager will be responsible for approving virements between the budgets of cost-centres that are funded from the programme in a similar way.

# 2. Instructions for using the PPBB budget tool 2019

## 2.1 Introduction

### Tool purpose

The purpose of the tool is to help MDAs record and calculate the financial implications of their budgeting decisions for the 2019 to 2021 period, within ceiling.

The start point of the tool is projecting baselines (a costing exercise to estimate the forward cost of current policies and service delivery practices) from mid-March.

The tool then support the MTES preparation in April. The end-point is a budget proposal within ceiling (Scenario A), set out as traditional budget tables by programme and economic items.

### Tool structure

The tool comprises baseline costing sheets, consolidation sheets and background sheets.

The Index sheet provides a description for each sheet, and a link to the sheet. In summary:

* **COSTING SHEETS**
  * **BASELINE PROJECTIONS**
    * FOR MANPOWER BASELINE PROJECTIONS – “MANPOWER” and “GRAT”
      * MANPOWER is used to project basic pay and allowances
      * GRAT is used to project gratuities
    * FOR GOODS AND SERVICES – “GS”
    * FOR the baseline projection of EXISTING PSIP PROJECTS -- “PSIP”
    * FOR the baseline projection of existing Minor Capital Outlays – “MCO”
    * FOR the costing of New Spending Proposals – “NSPs”
* **CONSOLIDATION SHEETS**
  * “PBUDGET” is a reference sheet that you can generate at any time while undertaking costing to see a summary of all the cost elements
  * “MTES” is the consolidation sheet in which the result of the baseline costing is compared to the issued ceilings.
  * “SCENA” is to be used to generate your budget proposal tables for ScenA, but only when all over and under amounts have been cleared. **It is also where you can generate the ScenA NSPs, MTES Summary and Savings Consolidation table for including in the MTES Document.**
  * “SUMMARY REQUEST” provides a consolidated view of your ScenA and Scen B proposals.
* **BUDGETING SHEETS**
  * “WORKSPACE” is the sheet on which you set out the financial implications of your budget decisions by indicating which budget lines of your baseline projection should be higher than indicated, and which lower. On the WORKSPACE sheet for the budget submission, you have to remain within Scenario A, or your ceilings. All your spending, both baseline costing and NSP proposals, are set out in this sheet by programme and economic categories.
  * “ScenB” is the sheet in which you request additional allocations over the ceilings that have been issued.
* **BACKGROUND SHEETS**
  * These sheets contain the background names and lists that make the tool work. They are:
    * INDEX, Names, Bsheet, BSheet2 and LISTS
    * PROGS, PERS and CONS (Hidden)
    * PINPUT (Hidden)

### The tool logic

The tool treats baseline projections and costing of new spending proposals separately.
# BASELINE PROJECTIONS

* The tool costs each main economic category separately, and consolidates the information in **PBUDGET** and the **MTES** sheets. This is because each type of economic category has unique cost projection requirements.
* **THUS**
    * **WAGES AND SALARIES IN CASH** is costed through the manpower and gratuity projection exercises. These exercises are done on the **MANPOWER** and **GRAT** sheets.
    * **WAGES AND SALARIES IN KIND** is costed as part of the Goods and Services costing on the **GS** sheet.
    * **GOODS and SERVICES** are costed on the **GS** sheet and starts from the 2017 budget. **Once you are satisfied that your baseline costing for economic goods and services items is robust, changes to fit in ceiling should be made on the WORKSPACE sheet, not the GS sheet. Otherwise these cuts you make, will not register as savings and cuts.**
    * The projection for **NON FINANCIAL ASSETS** is either for Minor Capital Outlays, or for PSIP projects. There is a sheet for each of these. Forward expenditure on existing PSIP projects are projected on the **PSIP** sheet. Forward expenditure on Minor Capital Outlays allocations are done on the **MCO** sheet.

All **NEW SPENDING PROPOSALS** are costed on the **NSPs** sheet notwithstanding which economic category. This means that new manpower proposals are costed on this sheet, not on the manpower sheet.

**The sheets are not locked! So be careful.**

**Do not add or delete columns:** The tool depends on referencing. Some of this referencing is hard coded. If you add or delete columns the referencing will fail and the tool will not work.

**You can delete rows, BUT ONLY IN THE FORMATTED DATA ENTRY AREA.** Particularly in the Manpower sheet, do not delete rows that run through the summary table at the bottom.

**Do not over-write formulas, unless the sheet tells you that you can (e.g. when you override the increases)**. If you accidently do, you can generally copy the formula from the line above or below into the overwritten cell.

## Using the manpower sheet

### Costing methods

The tool is set up to manage three basic costing methods:

* **Fixed percentage**: this means that the amount is fixed (the cost does not vary with the number of units as a cost driver), and the cost will increase every year by a percentage
* **Variable cost**: this means that cost varies depending on the number of cost driving units. The cost increases each year depending on the projected number of units, and the increase in the cost per unit.
* **Lumpsum costing**: this means that the cost you are calculating is not included or cannot be derived from the amount in the base year (2017 actual expenditure). Lumpsum can also be used to adjust a 2017 actual expenditure amount to 2018 budget. In other words, you can project the amount as previously using for example variable method, but adjust the projection in lumpsum to the 2018 amount.

**You will see the workbook also projects 2018. Why is this?**

But I already have a 2018 budget, why am I projecting 2018? Your budget is the funding you have been allocated. The projection indicates, using the variables you have selected, how much it is likely to cost you to undertake your activities and services this year. If the projection is more than your budget, it would be advisable to already start looking for efficiency savings, so that you do not run out of money before the end of the year. A projection for 2018 higher than your budget does not mean you can argue for additional funding mid-year: all MDAs are likely to be in the same position.

## Projecting your NSP cost

### Using the NSP sheet

The final costing sheet is the NSP sheet. On this sheet the tool allows you to cost out the new spending proposals from programmes, that would go forward for assessment.

#### When to cost your NSPs

Before starting to cost NSPs, it is important to run the MTES sheet first, to make an assessment of your spending space. This will give you an indication of the volume and quantity of NSPs that have a reasonable chance of proceeding to be funded. There is no use in costing out NSPs that will never be funded.

Once you have taken the measure of the spending space, you can cost NSPs and rerun the PBUDGET and MTES sheets

#### The sheet design

The sheet is like the WORKSPACE and G&S sheets, insofar as it keeps a running tally of your work visible while you work. This is at the top of the sheet. It shows the total financial cost of your NSPs as you cost them out, for each of the years, by main kind of cost (recurrent, MCOs or PSIP).

The work of the sheet is done in the bottom part, from line 12 onwards, with headings for the columns and instructions for each column in rows 9 to 11.

The sheet also incorporates two buttons, one to add lines (as for all costing sheets), and one to generate a summary table on the sheet itself, if you want to see what your costing is looking like by programme and NSP.

#### Steps to cost NSPs

##### 1. Designing your NSP

The NSP sheet is set out to allow deconstructing any NSP into three layers. **IMPORTANT: the sheet requires each line item of the NSP to be identified, at the line item level.**
A first step therefore is to list, outside of the sheet, the different types of actions that will make up your NSP, and then for each of these, to list all the line items that will be affected by the action. An example is provided below.

![](data:image/x-emf;base64...)

**Note that you can set out the NSP to suit your MDA and the number of NSPs**. For example, your NSP may have strengthening of the inspection unit as one of the cost components of a larger NSP, like building MDA capacity. So then the new staff will be shunted as a cost item rather than a cost component. Then you would use different economic item lines corresponding with the same cost component, namely the basic pay and the allowance economic items for a new staff member, identified as a cost.

One the other hand, not all NSPs will have more than one component, and not all components will have more than one cost. If you have an NSP that has one component and one cost, you can just repeat the NSP name in the cost component and cost item columns.

### 2. Using the sheet

Once you have set the conceptual design of the NSP out, you can start using the sheet.

Enter this name here – make sure that it is a short name, and that you enter it consistently for all the line that are identified with the NSP. # Guidance to prepare PPBB Protocols Manual

**Guidance Note**
# Preparing a PPBB Protocols Manual

With the introduction of PPBB, MDAs will prepare and implement their budgets very differently. This will require a change to the underlying MDA business processes for budget planning and implementation. Each MDA will prepare its own Protocol Manual to suit its own underlying processes. This Guidance Note suggests the topics that should be covered in these Manuals.

The starting point for MDAs should be for Programme managers and FCs to look at current processes and see what needs to change for the introduction of PPBB. Revised protocols should be established to facilitate efficient budget processes while ensuring adequate control.

There should be a system for ensuring that the manual is kept up-to-date, is distributed to all staff (and new staff), and that training on the protocols is provided to new and existing staff.

## Suggested processes to cover in the Protocol Manual

For each of the topics below, the Manual should cover:
1. **Steps** for each process
2. **Roles & responsibilities and oversight**
3. **Roles of programme managers** in each of the processes

Protocols for budget execution should ensure/highlight segregation of duties.

### Budget planning/preparation

* **Strategic budget planning**, including medium-term expenditure strategies (MTES) – timing, responsibility, templates (using templates from MoFTEP, with sub-templates [e.g. for programme manager input] developed by MDA, as needed)
* **Budget preparation** – timing, responsibility, templates (using templates from MoFTEP, with sub-templates [e.g. for programme manager input] developed by MDA, as needed)
* **Cash flow projections/forecasting** – initial forecasts and the updates of these forecasts throughout the year – timing, responsibility, templates (using templates from MoFTEP, with sub-templates [e.g. for programme manager input] developed by MDA, as needed)
* **Procurement plans** – timing, responsibility, templates (using templates from MoFTEP, with sub-templates [e.g. for programme manager input] developed by MDA, as needed)

### Budget execution

**Processing of non-salary expenditures**
* Approvals of Requests for LPOs, LPOs and PVs (ex-ante and ex-post controls)
* Recording of commitments (and, ideally, process for controlling commitments)

**Processing of transactions**

**Managing procurement and suppliers**
* Procurement procedures (thresholds, procurement method, steps, and roles & responsibilities), including services (consultancy). These will include procurement for: recurrent and minor capital goods and works, and major capital goods and works (PSIP)
* Contract management (both PSIP and service contracts)

**Virements**
* Raising requests for virements for internal reallocations
* Approval of internal reallocation requests
* Raising requests for virements for reallocations which must be approved by MoFTEP
* Approval of external virement requests
* Preparation of virement reports for senior management and programme managers – frequency of reports and responsibility for their preparation and circulation

## Monitoring and Reporting

* **Financial and non-financial performance monitoring**
    * Identification of the types of financial and non-financial monitoring required to support ex-post controls
    * Analytical requirements to support programme management (financial and non-financial performance information)
    * Non-financial performance monitoring, including establishment of a management information system to collect data, responsibilities for ensuring data are collected and providing information to programme/sub-programme managers
* **Reporting**
    * Frequency and type of reports to be prepared: financial and non-financial performance reports
    * Generation and circulation of these reports: responsibilities and timing
    * Programme management meetings: frequency, chair and participants, broad agenda (TORs), and information requirements (e.g. financial and non-financial performance reports)
+ **Report templates:** Responsibilities, timing and output format templates for:
    - MDA-level budget monitoring reports (for internal management and monitoring purposes);
    - External reports (e.g. for MoFTEP, including Section 31 reports): MDA-specific templates to support the higher-level MoFTEP templates for these reports

## Programme performance (non-financial) information management

- Responsibility for managing MDA-wide performance indicators
- Responsibility for each programme/sub-programme indicator: calculation, data custody and collection

### Suggested annexes to include in the manual

- Organisational structure (which should be kept up-to-date)
- List of programme managers (against the programme/sub-programme’s objectives)
- MDA-specific templates for budget planning and implementation to support the higher-level MoFTEP templates
- MoFTEP templates for PPBB outputs (which should be kept up-to-date)
