# S.I. 82 of 2013

## NATIONAL AIDS COUNCIL OF SYCHELLES ACT, 2013

(Act 13 of 2013)

### National AIDS Council of Sychelles Act (Commencement) Notice, 2013

In exercise of the powers conferred by section 1 of the National AIDS Council of Sychelles Act, 2013 the Minister of health hereby makes the following Notice —

1. This Notice may be cited as the **National AIDS Citation Council of Sychelles Act (Commencement) Notice, 2013**.

2. The **National AIDS Council of Sychelles Act, 2013** shall come into operation on 1st January, 2014.
