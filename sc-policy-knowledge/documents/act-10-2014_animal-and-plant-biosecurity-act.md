**5.(1) The functions of the Agency shall be-**

(a) regulate the import into the Seychelles of regulated pests and diseases affecting animals, plants, human beings and the environment;

(b) carry out surveillance of pests and diseases and ensure the status of regulated pests and diseases in Seychelles;

(c) prevent the establishment and spread of regulated pests and diseases and the introduction of organisms that might adversely affect animals, plants, human beings and the environment in the environment in Seychelles;

(d) eradicate, contain or control, the presence of regulated pests and diseases in Seychelles that are already present in Seychelles;

(e) prevent the establishment and spread of regulated pests and diseases not already present in Seychelles;

(f) facilitate the import and exportation of animals, animal products, plants and plant products services, other than regulated pests;

(g) facilitate the export of animals, animal products, plants and plant products, in accordance with the sanitary and phytosanitary requirements of the importing country;

(h) to facilitate international cooperation for the prevention of the spread of pests and diseases affecting animals, plants, human beings and the environment.

(i) to participate on the Sanitary and Phytosanitary Committees, Technical Bodies to Committee T SPS of the WTO, International Plant Protection and international standard setting bodies; and

(j) to perform any other functions as determined by the Minister.
