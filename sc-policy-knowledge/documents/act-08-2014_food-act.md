or (4), is liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding a sum for
ten months exceeding 5 years or a fine not exceeding SCR
500,000, or both.

# PART VI - MISCELLANEOUS

## Amendments

50. Any person aggrieved by a decision of the
Competent Authority under this Act may, within twenty-one
days of the date of the decision, appeal to the Minister
under sections 27 or 29, or on order made under section 30, why
such decision should be set aside, modified or varied.
Further, appeal to the Party's Board under the public
Authority Act, 2013 (Act no. 13 of 2013).

51. The Minister may make Regulations for carrying out
intentions of this Act, and the Minister may make
sections which by this Act are required to be prescribed or
necessitated or are expected to be prescribing for giving
effect to this Act.

(1) In making regulations under subsection (1), Workout
shall be taken of the Codes Commission and the World Trade
Organization's agreement on the Application of Sanitary and
Phytosanitary Measures.

59. The Minister, the Competent Authority, the Public
Health Commissioner, any authorised officer shall be
protected from any liability for any act done in good faith
in the exercise of a power or the performance of a
function under this Act.

60. (1) The Food Act (Cap 83) is hereby repealed.

(2) Notwithstanding the repeal of the Food Act:

(a) any order, rule or regulation made under the
repealed Act shall continue in force until the
abrogation, unless replaced or repealed under this
Act.

(b) the approval or authorisation granted under the
repealed Act shall continue to be valid until the
approval or authorisation, as the case may be, is
revoked, modified or expires, or until a new
expiration or revocation of such approval or
authorisation, and

(c) any penalty or forfeiture or punishment
enforced in respect of any offence committed under
this Act shall continue to be enforceable as if this
Act had not been enacted.

61. The provisions of this Act are in addition to and
not in derogation of the provisions of any other written law.
