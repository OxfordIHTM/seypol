## Results Based Management Framework

The Cabinet has approved for the whole government roll-out of Results Based Management (RBM) since the preparatory work beginning relatively immediately and ready for the 2023 budget process.

This involves:
* Adoption of the proposal for the portfolio based sector planning process where the ministries produce a portfolio of priorities, including the refinement of the remaining 3 pillars as well as the formulation of National Development Strategies, which will reflect priorities at the highest level of government.
* Adoption and use of the PM&E tool for monitoring PM&E across government. This involves strengthening institutional capacity and processes to improve the quality of performance information for monitoring and reporting on performance.
* The roll-out of the PM&E pillar as a whole of government approach to ensure coordination and integration (i) between ministries, (ii) between departments, and (iii) between agencies.

To further ensure the successful implementation of RBM and Integration of the four pillars, the whole process will be steered at national level by the High Level RBM Steering Committee which will be the overarching one to ensure successful implementation of the RBM policy framework across government.
The Relative **RBM Committee (RBM)** shall be established as a focal point in each portfolio to implement the RBM policy framework for a more integrated approach to public service management and accountability.

MDA's are requested to provide details of their internal RBM committee by Nominated by the Permanent Secretary of the respective Ministry or the RBM-SC. **Mx. Margaret Mourne** on `mdelay@database.gov.sc`. Further details on the role of the RBM can be found in the **RBM Manual**.

The RBM-SC will be conducting individual briefing sessions with all portfolios and catals of which will be provided shortly.

The **Guy Morel Institute** in partnership with the RBM committee will be undertaking the necessary training for the MDAs, to provide the require skills necessary to implement the RBM concept at the Ministry level.
