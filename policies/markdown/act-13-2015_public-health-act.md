*Commissioner to administer the Act*

4.(1) The Commissioner shall be responsible for the administration of this Act.

(2) The Commissioner shall take necessary measures for ensuring cleanliness, sanitation and prevention of diseases and the maintenance and improvement of the health of the members of the public.

*Public health officers*

5. The Commissioner may, in consultation with the Minister, designate such of his or her functions under this Act to such public health officers, as may be necessary to assist the Commissioner in performing the functions of the Commissioner under this Act, on such terms and conditions as may be considered appropriate.

## PART III - PROTECTION OF WATER SUPPLIES

*Protection of water supplies*

6. The Commissioner shall—

(a) by all practicable means guard against and prevent the pollution of any water supply which is used or may be used for drinking, domestic, recreational or industrial purposes; and

(b) where any such supply has been polluted or is likely to be polluted, take all practicable measures to stop any further pollution and to have the water purified.

*Pollution of water intended for human consumption*

7.(1) A person shall not use or suffer to be used water intended for human consumption, which is polluted or unwholesome.

(2) The Commissioner may, by notice in writing, stop the sale or use of any water by a person or a business undertaking intended for human consumption, if the Commissioner is of the opinion that such water is unsafe or does not comply with the standards prescribed by regulations.

(3) Any person who contravenes or fails to comply with subsection (1) commits an offence and shall, on

**Effect of notice prohibiting occupation**
13. The service of a notice under section 12 on the owner or occupier of a dwelling shall terminate all tenancies and sub-tenancies of the dwelling or part of the dwelling to which the notice relates and any person who, after such notice has been served, occupies or attempts to obtain any rental in respect of use of the dwelling or any part of it for any period during which such notice is in force commits an offence and shall on conviction be liable to a fine not exceeding SCR25,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 3 years or to both such fine and imprisonment.

**Public conveniences**
14. The Commissioner shall ensure that adequate and suitable public sanitary conveniences for males and females are provided and maintained in a clean and sanitary condition.

**Sanitary conveniences**
15. (1) Where it appears to the Commissioner that any building, public facility or part thereof is without sufficient sanitary conveniences, or adequate and suitable separate sanitary conveniences for males and females, the Commissioner shall, by notice in writing, require the owner of the building or facility to provide the building or facility with such sanitary conveniences or such additional, or adequate and suitable sanitary conveniences as may be necessary for the persons using the building or facility, within such period as may be specified in the notice.

(2) Any person who fails to comply with the notice under subsection (1) commits an offence and shall on conviction be liable to a fine not exceeding SCR25,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 3 years or to both such fine and imprisonment.

**Order of insanitary conveniences**
16. (1) Where it appears to the Commissioner that any sanitary convenience provided for or in connection with a building or part thereof is defective or is in a condition that is

prejudicial to health or a nuisance, or is incapable of being reconstructed so as to make it into a satisfactory condition, the Commissioner shall, by notice in writing, require the owner or occupier of the building to execute such works or to take such steps as may be necessary to remedy the defects, within such period as may be specified in the notice.

(2) Any person who fails to comply with the notice under subsection (1) commits an offence and shall on conviction be liable to a fine not exceeding SCR25,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 3 years or to both such fine and imprisonment.

**Sanitary conveniences at workplaces**
17. (1) All workplaces shall be provided with adequate, suitable and separate sanitary conveniences for males and females, at such locations in the workplace and in accordance with such specifications as may be prescribed, unless the Commissioner determines in writing that in the particular circumstances the provision of separate sanitary conveniences for males and females in a specified workplace is unnecessary.

(2) Where it appears to the Commissioner that subsection (1) has not been complied with, he or she shall, by notice in writing, require the owner or occupier of such workplace, as the case may be, to make such alterations to the sanitary conveniences or provide such additional or separate sanitary conveniences within such period as may be specified in the notice.

(3) The owner or occupier shall maintain such sanitary conveniences in a clean and hygienic manner so as not to cause any nuisance to anybody.

(4) Any person who fails to comply with the notice under subsection (2) or fails to maintain such sanitary conveniences in a clean and hygienic manner under subsection (3) commits an offence and shall on conviction be liable to a fine not exceeding SCR25,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 3 years or to both such fine and imprisonment.

**Burial of human remains in unauthorised place**

47. (1) A person shall not bury or cause to be buried human remains in any place other than in a cemetery, burial ground or crematorium or in a vault otherwise than in accordance with the provisions of this Act.

**Permit for exhumation**

48. Any person who has a reason to exhume the remains of a deceased person shall make an application in writing to the Commissioner who shall grant a permit if he or she is satisfied that there are sufficient reasons to grant the application, where—

(a) suitable arrangements have been made for the reinterment or other disposal of the remains; and

49. (1) Where the Commissioner considers it necessary in the public interest that any human remains should be exhumed, he or she shall issue an order directing that the human remains be exhumed and reinterred or cremated under the supervision of an officer named in the order and in such manner.

50.
