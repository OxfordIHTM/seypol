Job Aid for HCWs: Procedures for Notification of Death (Ministry of Health, 2023)

ANY PERSON DIES

For ALL deaths:

- Medical practitioner
(public or private) to
confirm death clinically.
- Issue death notification

for ALL deaths.

- If police case or requires

autopsy, state on
notification.

- Scan a copy of death
notification (legible).

- Give original copy of
death notification to
Next-of-Kin.

- Medical practitioner is
responsible to e-mail
scanned copy of death
notification within 24h to
Civil Status Office at
infodeath@cs.gov.sc and
Ministry of Health at
vitalstats@health.gov.sc.

  If no autopsy indicated:

  If medical autopsy required:

  If police case:

-  Treating medical practitioner issues
Medical Certificate of Cause of
Death (MCCOD) together with
death notification.

-  Explain cause of death to next-of-

kin. Body can be released for funeral
procedures.

-  Original MCCOD in patient notes.
-  MCCOD is not issued to next-of-kin,
but they can request medical report
through standard procedures for
details of death.

-  For deaths occurring at home, refer

case to the treating medical
practitioner to issue MCCOD (can be
public or private clinic).

-  Medical practitioner issuing MCCOD

is responsible to submit copy of
MCCOD within 24h to
infodeath@cs.gov.sc and
vitalstats@health.gov.sc.

-  Provide post-mortem information

sheet and explain need & procedure.

-  Obtain consent for autopsy and fill

relevant forms.

-  Submit patient notes with above to

pathology services.

-  Transfer body to hospital mortuary.

-  Pathology services perform autopsy
per procedures; body release based
on pathologist assessment.

-  Pathologist issues MCCOD; original
copy in patient notes; not issued to
next-of-kin.

- Notify relevant officials at
Seychelles Police through
provided forms, and follow
instructions per police.

- Forensic pathologist guides

procedures, including
autopsy.

- MCCOD issuance by
forensic pathologist.
- Original copy to patient

notes.

- Release of body per police

instructions.

- Next-of-Kin can request any

with last treating clinician).

-  Next-of-Kin can request medical

report through standard procedures
for written documentation of cause
of death.

-  Pathologist is responsible to submit

copy of MCCOD to
infodeath@cs.gov.sc and
vitalstats@health.gov.sc

reports to relevant
department of the
Seychelles Police.

- Forensic pathologist is

responsible to submit copy
of MCCOD to
infodeath@cs.gov.sc and
vitalstats@health.gov.sc.
