3.1 Quality of Life
In an Elderly Needs Assessment in 2010;
 72% of respondents reported that they were very happy.
 Most of the elderly said they enjoyed listening to the radio (93%), praying (88%), watching television (87%),
sleeping (86%) and talking to friends (59%)
 99% of respondents reported having 3 meals a day and a similarly high percentage (96%) felt that they had a
balanced diet.
 However, 69% of the elderly sampled reported that their income was not sufficient to provide for their house-
hold’s food needs.
 When asked to describe their physical health, 32% report they were in good health, 26% that they were in poor
health and 43% that their physical health was average.
 88% of respondents living at home with their rela(cid:415)ves report that they feel safe and cared for at home.
 On the issue of neglect, 14% of respondents report feeling neglected by their family. It is especially those living

(cid:415)ves and 26% of those living alone.

for residents in the ins(cid:415)tu(cid:415)ons with 21% repor(cid:415)ng they have encountered either verbal or emo(cid:415)onal abuse.

The findings of the Assessment support the view that on the whole, most elderly people in Seychelles enjoy a dignified
