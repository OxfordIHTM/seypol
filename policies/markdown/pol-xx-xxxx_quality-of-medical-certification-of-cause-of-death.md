QUALITY OF MEDICAL CERTIFICATION OF CAUSE OF DEATH (MCCOD):
Rapid Assessment Tool

This form contains a set of simple questions for checking common errors to assess the cause of death certification practices. The assessors
of the death certificates are required to go through the eight questions and verify whether there are any of the mentioned errors in the
certificate completed by the certifier. This tool is adapted from ‘Assessing the quality of death certificates: Guidance for the rapid tool’
(University of Melbourne) available at https: //crvsgateway.info/
