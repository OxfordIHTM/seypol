S.I. 54 of 2022

INQUEST INTO DEATH ACT

(Act 47 of 2021)

Inquest into Death (Conduct of Proceedings) Regulations, 2022

IN  EXERCISE  OF  THE  POWERS  CONFERRED  BY  SECTION  30(2)(A)  OF  THE
INQUEST  INTO  DEATH  ACT,  2021,  THE  MINISTER  RESPONSIBLE  FOR  LEGAL
AFFAIRS, IN CONSULTATION WITH THE CHIEF JUSTICE, MAKES THE FOLLOWING
REGULATIONS  —

Citation and commencement

1.

These regulations may be cited as the Inquest into Death (Conduct

of Proceedings) Regulations, 2022.

Interpretation

2.

In these regulations  —

“Public    Prosecutor”  means  a  person  appointed  as  a  public
prosecutor under section 63 of the Criminal Procedure Code, Cap.
54 or other written law;

“State Counsel” means a person holding or acting in the office of
State Counsel, of any description, including a Pupil State Counsel
who has taken and subscribed the Oath of Allegiance before the
Chief Justice pursuant to section 8 of the Official Oaths Act, Cap.
153.

Officers who may assist in conduct of proceedings

3.(1) Any  of  the  following  persons,  at  the  direction  of  the  Attorney
General,  may  assist  the  person  appointed  under  section  3  of  the
Act to hold an inquest into a person’s death  —

(a)

(b)

a Deputy Attorney General;

a State Counsel;

(2) A  person  under  subregulation  (1)  assisting  the  person  appointed
under section 3 of the Act to hold an inquest into a person’s death
may  —

(a)

question  any  witness  at  the  inquest  on  matters  relevant  to
ascertaining  the  information  required  under  section  20(1)  of
the  Act,  including  asking  questions  in  relation  to  any
investigation report, post-mortem report, special examination
or statement;

(b)

tender  any  evidence  relevant  to  the  inquest,  including  any
investigation  report,  post-mortem  report,  special
examination or statement;

(c) make an opening address or a closing address, or both; and

(d)

provide  assistance  on  any  other  matter  necessary  for  the
inquest  subject  to  the  direction  of  the  person  appointed
under section 3 of the Act to hold an inquest into a person’s
death.
