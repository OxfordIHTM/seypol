THE INQUEST INTO
DEATH ACT, 2021
Gazetted on 8th November, 2021
WHY WAS THE LAW ENACTED
To promote justice through investigations, and the identification of the causes and circumstances, of sudden or unexplained
deaths, or deaths that occurred in certain circumstances and make recommendations to reduce the chances of similar deaths
to occur. The Act clarifies clearly the purpose of an inquest into death and address issues relating to post-mortem
examination, the release of a body for transportation out of Seychelles, burial and cremation and in certain instances
exhumation.
