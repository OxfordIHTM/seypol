**Results Based Management Framework**

The Cabinet has approved for the whole of government roll-out of Results Based
Management (RBM), with preparatory work beginning immediately and ready for
the 2023 budget process.
This will include:
- Adoption of the proposal for the portfolio based sector planning
  approach across government to facilitate integration and roll-out of
  the remaining 3 pillars as well as the formulation of National
  Development Strategies, which will reflect priorities at the highest
  level. (NSP policy and guidelines attached)
- Adoption and use of the PM&E tool for monitoring PM&E across
  government. This includes strengthening data quality for PM&E to
  improve the quality of performance information for monitoring and
  reporting of performance.
- The roll-out of the PMS pillar as a whole of government approach to
  include (i) organisation and (ii) individual performance.

To further ensure the successful implementation of RBM and integration of the
four pillars, the whole process will be steered at national level by the High Level
RBM Steering Committee will be the overarching one to ensure successful
implementation of the RBM policy framework across government.
